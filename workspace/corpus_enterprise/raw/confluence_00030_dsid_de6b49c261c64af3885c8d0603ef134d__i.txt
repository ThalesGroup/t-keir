Incident Commander checklist: platform-wide degradation (Hosted API)

h1. Goal
Provide a fast, repeatable checklist for Incident Commanders responding to platform-wide degradation of the Hosted API.

h1. Declare + organize (first 5 minutes)
1) Create incident channel and set severity (P0/P1).
2) Assign roles:
- IC
- Ops lead (SRE)
- Comms lead
- Liaison to Support
3) Start a shared timeline doc (link in channel).
4) Confirm paging of required oncalls:
- SRE
- Infra
- Gateway
- Serving runtime
- (If auth suspected) Security

h1. Triage + stabilize (minutes 5-20)
1) Confirm user impact:
- 5xx rate
- latency
- streaming disconnects
2) Determine blast radius:
- single region vs global
- one route vs multiple
- specific model vs all models
3) Apply quick mitigations (if available):
- tighten rate limits
- disable recent feature flags
- pause deploys
- re-route only a subset of traffic

h1. Decision: do we initiate regional failover?
You should ask three questions:
1) Is this clearly regional? (e.g., only us-east-1 is failing)
2) Is the secondary region healthy and has headroom?
3) Is recovery in the primary likely within 30 minutes?

If (1) and (2) are YES and (3) is NO/unknown, instruct Ops lead to initiate failover per the failover runbook.

*Documentation requirement:* If we are cutting over global traffic, this is an Emergency Change. Capture second-person sign-off in the channel per policy.

h1. Comms
- Update status page at incident start, at major state changes (failover started, failover complete), and at resolution.
- For enterprise customers, coordinate with Support on direct outreach.

h1. Resolution + wrap-up
1) Confirm stability for at least 30 minutes.
2) Announce "resolved" and note whether we remain failed over.
3) Assign postmortem owner and due date.
4) Ensure the exact traffic routing commands used are in the timeline.
