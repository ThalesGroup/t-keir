SLI deprecation and telemetry archival playbook

Summary

This playbook describes a standardized process for retiring (deprecating) an SLI and archiving the associated telemetry and dashboards. It covers pre-deprecation validation, stakeholder approvals, data retention decisions, dashboard migration or removal, alert rule transitions, automation steps, and rollback procedures. The goal is to avoid data loss, preserve historical context for postmortems and audits, and keep our dashboard surface area manageable.

Scope and applicability

- Applies to SLIs owned by platform services and tenant-facing inference services.
- Covers metric series, dashboard panels, synthetic checks, alerting rules, and related runbooks.
- Does NOT change SLO contracts without explicit billing/sales/legal signoff. SLO change is a separate process linked in Related Pages.

Goals

1. Safely remove noisy or redundant SLIs within a 6-week window.
2. Archive telemetry (raw or rolled) to a cold storage tier for at least 1 year unless compliance requires longer.
3. Ensure dashboards and alerts are updated or removed with minimal operational disruption.
4. Keep an auditable trail of approvals and migration steps.

Key stakeholders and roles

- SLI Owner: service/team that created and owns the SLI (required for signoff).
- Platform Observability (Obs) Team: reviews archival plans, runs queries, manages long-term storage.
- Platform Operators: run the automation and update dashboards/alerts.
- Incident Response (IR): notified of retired SLIs that are part of incident playbooks.
- Compliance/Billing: consulted when retention affects legal or billing evidence.

Decision criteria for deprecation

Deprecation may proceed when one or more criteria are met:

- SLI duplicates another signal that is in active use and provides no incremental actionability.
- SLI has shown zero-variability (flatline) for >90 days and is not expected to change.
- Ownership is unknown after a 30 day ownership-sunsetting campaign.
- New consolidated SLI replaces the old one (migration plan exists).

Pre-deprecation checklist (required before deprecation window opens)

1. Confirm SLI identity and metric names (series keys): 
   - metrics example: service.response.latency.p50, service.kv_cache.hit_rate, platform.request.error_rate
2. Verify SLI usage: dashboards referencing panels, runbooks, alerting rules (list locations).
3. Query historical variability: provide 90-day, 1-year rolls (p50/p95/stddev). Attach sample queries.
4. Notify owners and consumers: email + #channel post with link, 14-day notice (minimum).
5. Propose retention target: active hot tier (30 days), warm tier (31-90 days), cold archive (>=91 days).
6. Create an Approval Issue (JIRA or internal tracker) with approvers: SLI Owner, Obs Lead, Platform Eng Manager.

Approval and scheduling

- Approvers must respond within 7 business days. Silence after 7 days does NOT imply approval; escalate to Platform Eng Manager.
- Approved deprecations are scheduled into a 6-week window with the following phases:
  Week 0: Announcement and snapshotting of dashboards/queries.
  Week 1–3: Data re-routing to archival pipeline and synthetic check adjustment.
  Week 4: Alert mute and dashboard hide-by-default.
  Week 5: Soft-delete metric ingestion (stop production writes).
  Week 6: Hard-archive and dashboard removal.

Detailed procedures

1) Inventory and snapshot dashboards

- Export all referenced dashboards (JSON) and save to: gitlab.com/redwood-observability/archives/<service>-sli-retirements.
- Collect exact panel IDs and queries. Example export CLI (Grafana):

  curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $GRAFANA_TOKEN" https://grafana.redwood.internal/api/dashboards/uid/<UID> > dashboard-<UID>.json

- Record the mapping table: | dashboard_uid | panel_id | metric_series | consumer_playbook |
  Example row: | infra-latency-2025 | 7 | service.response.latency.p95 | infra/foreground-latency-playbook |

2) Archive telemetry pipeline

- For Prometheus metrics, create a rule that writes rollups to the long-term TSDB (Thanos/MetricStore) with retention tags. Example PromQL rollup: 

  - record: archived:service_response_latency_p95:30d
    expr: quantile_over_time(0.95, service_response_latency_seconds[30d])

- For tracing/trace-derived SLIs, export span aggregates to the cold bucket (S3 path: s3://redwood-obs-archives/<service>/sli-archives/YYYYMMDD).
- All archival writes must include labels: archived_by=platform-sli-deprecation, origin_sli=<original_metric_name>.

3) Alert transition plan

- Identify alert rules tied to the SLI. For each rule: 
  - Replace condition with equivalent on the new signal if one exists.
  - If no replacement exists, create a temporary alert that fires to a designated mailbox (not Ops channel) for 30 days before full removal.
  - Mute alerts at the policy layer during the soft-delete phase to avoid alert storms.

- Example Datadog rule change (pseudocode):

  FROM: avg(last_5m):anomalies(service.response.latency.p95, 'basic', 2, direction='above', alert_window='last_5m')
  TO:   avg(last_5m):anomalies(archived:service_response_latency_p95:30d, 'basic', 2, direction='above', alert_window='last_5m')

4) Dashboard lifecycle actions

- Week 4: Set panels to hidden_by_default=true and add an explanatory banner to the dashboard header: "This panel is scheduled for removal (see SLI deprecation playbook)."
- Week 6: Remove panels and delete dashboards only after a final sweep confirms no active runbook references.

5) Communicate changes and training

- Publish a consolidation note in #platform-observability with links to: Approval Issue, archived JSON, and sample queries.
- Update the Platform Oncall Runbook page with the new canonical signals and add a migration note where appropriate.
- Host a 30-minute Q&A with consumers in the first week after announcement.

Retention and compliance matrix

| Data type | Hot | Warm | Cold / Archive | Minimum retention | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Raw Prom metrics | 30d | 60d | S3 cold bucket | 365d | Compliance may require longer for audits |
| Rolled TSDB (1h) | 90d | 180d | S3 compressed store | 1095d | Useful for long-term trend analysis |
| Traces (aggregate) | 14d | 90d | S3 aggregate | 365d | Full traces retained only on request |

Automation snippets

- Archive rollup creation (example script):

  #!/usr/bin/env bash
  SERVICE=$1
  curl -X POST $PROMETHEUS_API/rules -H "Authorization: Bearer $PROM_RULE_TOKEN" -d '{"record":"archived:'$SERVICE'_p95:30d","expr":"quantile_over_time(0.95,'$SERVICE'_response_seconds[30d])"}'

- Dashboard export helper (python):

  import requests, os
  headers = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.getenv('GRAFANA_TOKEN') }"}
  r = requests.get(f"https://grafana.redwood.internal/api/dashboards/uid/{os.getenv('UID')}", headers=headers)
  open('dashboard.json','w').write(r.text)

Audit, rollback, and verification

- Verification steps after archival: 
  1. Confirm sample queries return expected archived rollup values.
  2. Confirm dashboard panels are hidden and linked references reported as resolved.
  3. Confirm alert rules are either migrated or set to mailbox-only.

- Rollback procedure (if issue detected during Week 5 or 6):
  1. Re-enable ingest for the metric (flip the soft-delete flag in the ingest gateway).
  2. Re-import dashboard JSON from the archive repo and unhide panels.
  3. Recreate alerts from exported definitions.
  4. Record the rollback in the Approval Issue and notify stakeholders.

Operational metrics to track success

- Number of SLIs retired per quarter. Target: 8–12.
- Mean time from approval to hard-archive. Target: <= 6 weeks.
- Percentage of retired SLIs with archived dashboards and rollback artifacts. Target: 100%.
- Incidents attributable to SLI removal. Target: 0 (investigate any occurrence).

Post-retirement retrospective cadence

- Two weeks after hard-archive, Obs Team runs a 60-minute retrospective with SLI Owner and Platform Operators to review any surprises, edge-cases, or gaps in the checklist.
- Outcomes recorded in the Approval Issue and this playbook is updated as needed.

Housekeeping and lifecycle policy

- All SLI deprecation events are logged under the git repo path and a single line summary is posted to the platform-ops monthly digest.
- Every 12 months the Obs Team runs an SLI audit to identify candidates for consolidation.
