Onboarding, Career Safety Net, and Internal Tools Compact

Overview:

This compact playbook bundles three closely related employee experiences: the first 90 days of onboarding, the 'career safety net' used to manage role transitions and expectations, and a concise internal-tools quickstart required to be productive at Redwood. It is intended for hiring managers, new hires, people-ops partners, and managers handling role transitions.

Goals:
- Reduce time-to-impact for new hires to 30 days for basic production contributors, 60 days for engineers contributing to core runtime, and 90 days for cross-functional ownership of projects.
- Provide a visible, humane set of expectations (the Career Safety Net) for employees facing role changes, performance improvement, or return-from-leave transitions.
- Standardize onboarding tool access so engineering and non-engineering teams can complete the technical checklist in the first week.

1) First-90 Onboarding framework

Intro: The First-90 is split into three clearly defined phases with owner responsibilities and measurable outcomes. Managers must publish a simple 90-day plan for each hire in the onboarding doc within 48 hours of start date.

Phases and outcomes:
- Day 0–7: Orientation + Access
  - Outcomes: account creation, README for team, local dev + CI run, one small doc or PR merged.
  - Owner: Hiring manager + buddy.
  - Must-complete checklist items:
    1. HR paperwork completed (People Ops)
    2. Okta + SSO validated and 2FA registered
    3. Internal mailbox and Slack channels joined
    4. Dev environment: run unit tests locally and submit a tiny fix/typo PR

- Day 8–30: Contribution + Feedback
  - Outcomes: shipping a first scoped task (bug fix, evaluation harness test, doc change), first check-in with manager, initial career conversation.
  - Owner: Manager (weekly 1:1)
  - Success metrics: PR landed, code review feedback implemented, buddy signoff on operational readiness.

- Day 31–90: Ownership + Integration
  - Outcomes: ownership of a small feature, runbook or eval suite component; documented handoff for cross-team dependencies.
  - Owner: Manager (biweekly 1:1)
  - Success metrics: service/deck ownership identified, promotion of knowledge via a 30-min team demo, and an updated career plan in Lattice/Growth platform.

2) Career Safety Net (CSN) — humane role transitions and support

Purpose: The CSN is a people-first protocol to support employees who are reassigned, returning from extended leave, or subject to a performance conversation. It codifies expectations, resources, and timelines so transitions are explicit and fair.

Principles:
- Transparency: clear goals, measurable checkpoints, and written agreements (no surprise change of expectations).
- Time-bounded: typical CSN plans last 6–12 weeks depending on context.
- Support-first: training, mentorship, and reasonable load reduction when appropriate.
- Escalation clarity: defined steps if objectives are not met.

CSN template (manager-owned):
- Context and objective (one paragraph)
- Timeline and checkpoints (weeks 1, 3, 6, 9)
- Specific success criteria (3–5 measurable outcomes)
- Adjustments to workload and support (mentor hours, training modules, paired work)
- People Ops and HR touchpoints (names and frequency)
- Review date and next steps (promote, extend, role change)

Example success criteria (engineer):
- Ship three completed bugfixes affecting latency >20% within 6 weeks
- Document two runbook pages and own one weekly on-call rotation by week 9

Escalation steps if CSN objectives are not met:
1. Manager and People Ops meet to reassess and document next 4-week plan.
2. If no sustained improvement: formal HR performance process, with People Ops and legal review as needed.

3) Benefits, Perks, and Accessibility highlights

This section captures the practical touchpoints new hires need to use benefits and ensure accessibility needs are handled early. It is a companion to the full Benefits Catalog.

Key actions for new hires:
- Enroll in benefits within 30 days (People Ops sends enrollment link automatically).
- Request ergonomic equipment via https://it-requests.redwood.example/ergonomics (allow 10 business days).
- Submit accommodation requests to accommodations@redwood.example — People Ops will triage within 3 business days.

Minimum accommodation SLA (internal):
- Acknowledgement: 3 business days
- Initial plan/short-term accommodations: 10 business days
- Long-term solution or procurement (e.g., specialized hardware): 30 business days

Mental health and caregiver support:
- Confidential counseling sessions (up to 8 sessions per calendar year) with provider details in the Benefits Catalog.
- Parent transition checklists for flexible return-to-work and phased schedules.

4) Internal tools quickstart (minimal commands and access flow)

Objective: get a new hire capable of completing the Dev + Prod checklist within the first week. This section lists the most common commands, access flows, and troubleshooting tips.

Authentication and identity
- Okta SSO: Ensure you can log into Okta and enroll 2FA (device or passkey).
- Service account requests: rbw-admin request --type service --team <team-name> (people-ops approves within 1 business day for standard needs)

Code and CI
- Clone the monorepo: git clone git@repo.redwood.example:infra/monorepo.git- Create a branch: git checkout -b feat/onboard-<initials>- Run tests: ./dev/run_tests.sh --fast- Submit PR: hub pull-request -b main -m "Onboard: fix typo"

Observability and runbooks
- Dashboard access: observability.redwood.example -> login via Okta- On-call handover doc: /confluence/eng-sre/runbooks/oncall-handover-template

Common troubleshooting steps (order matters)
1. Check Okta status (https://status.okta.com)
2. Validate network and VPN (rbw vpn status)
3. Revoke and reissue token: rbw-auth refresh --force
4. If still blocked, open IT ticket and tag Manager + People Ops (required for expedited access)

6) Role ladders, promotion signals, and coaching cadence

Role ladder reference: managers must map hires to a role ladder within 30 days and document the expected milestones for promotion. This section summarizes signals we value across functions:
- Engineering: scope of ownership, architectural impact, mentoring, and reliability outcomes (SLOs improved or maintained).
- Product: shipped outcomes with measurable metrics, cross-functional leadership, and customer impact.
- Design & DX: end-to-end UX improvements, measurable engagement improvements, and design systems contributions.

Promotion cadence and signals:
- Typical review windows: quarterly calibration cycles.
- Expectation: a minimum of 6–12 months in-role for a typical promotion, unless extraordinary impact is documented.

Coaching cadence:
- Monthly growth 1:1s focused on skills and career plan (documented in Lattice/Growth).
- Quarterly calibration: manager and people-ops review progress and adjust plan.

Governance and ownership
- People Ops owns the CSN templates and benefits enrollment process.
- Hiring managers are accountable for the 90-day plan and career mapping.
- IT owns first-line access provisioning and hardware procurement.

Feedback loop and continuous improvement
- New hires are asked to complete a 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day pulse survey. Results are triaged monthly by People Ops and aggregated into a quarterly onboarding health report.
- Managers must act on escalations within 3 business days. Persistent blockers are escalated to the Head of People Ops for resolution.
