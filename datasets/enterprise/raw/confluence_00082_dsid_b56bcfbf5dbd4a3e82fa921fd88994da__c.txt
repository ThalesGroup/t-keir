Compensation, Wellbeing & Onboarding Framework

Overview

Purpose:
This document defines Redwood’s integrated approach to total compensation, wellbeing benefits, onboarding guarantees, and the critical internal tools and checkpoints that ensure new hires and role transitions succeed. It is a single-source reference for managers, people-ops partners, hiring teams, and new joiners.

Scope:
Applies to all US and EU employees, contractors on company payroll, and People Ops-managed contingent workers. For country-specific deviations (e.g., statutory leave differences), see the regional supplements linked in Related Pages.

Principles

- Predictable: employees and managers should be able to forecast total rewards and onboarding timelines.
- Transparent: components of pay, equity, and benefits are documented and accessible.
- Inclusive: wellbeing and perks are designed for diverse family and life needs.
- Operational: onboarding and provisioning have measurable SLAs and clear owners.

Total Compensation Components

1) Base Salary
- Paid semi-monthly in local currency.
- Bands are published internally (People Ops maintains band matrix). Managers must consult compensation@redwood for off-band offers.

2) Variable / Bonus
- Individual performance-based bonus target for eligible roles. Paid annually after calibration.

3) Equity
- Standard grant sizes by level and role family. Vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff unless otherwise negotiated.

4) Benefits Allowances
- Relocation: case-by-case; People Ops approval required.
- Home office stipend: one-time $600 (USD) for new hires; recurring ergonomic stipend available upon manager sign-off.

Health & Wellbeing

- Medical/Dental/Vision: Core plans with options to upgrade. Enrollment window: first 30 days from start date.
- Mental health: unlimited access to the Redwood EAP and up to 20 therapy sessions per calendar year reimbursed depending on region.
- Parental leave: primary caregiver 20 weeks fully paid; secondary caregiver 8 weeks. Reduced schedules and phased returns encouraged.
- Care and Family Support: backup care stipend and fertility benefits included in the benefits portal.

Time Off & Flexible Work

- PTO: flexible policy with expectation of at least 15 working days taken per year. Managers should approve plans to cover team deliverables.
- Holidays: company-observed calendar published annually.
- Sabbatical: eligibility after 5 years of tenure; see Sabbatical Policy for details.

Perks & Stipends

- Commuter/parking stipend where regionally appropriate.
- Learning & development: $2,000 annual L&D allowance per employee.
- Conference budget: separate manager-approved allocation for role-critical conferences.

Career and Role Progression

Role Ladder Summary (examples):
| Level | Typical Title examples | Core expectations | Promotion cadence |
|-------|------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| L1 | Associate / Junior Eng | Execute well-defined tasks, pair frequently | 12-18 months based on proficiency |
| L3 | Senior / Sr PM | Independent project leadership, mentoring | 12-24 months, evidence-based review |
| L5 | Staff / Principal | Cross-team influence, architecture ownership | Promotion requires portfolio & last-12mo impact |

Competencies evaluated for promotion: technical craft, product judgement (if applicable), cross-team collaboration, mentoring & coaching, and business impact. People Ops and managers jointly assemble promotion packets; calibration occurs quarterly.

Hiring & Onboarding: Guaranteed Service Levels

To reduce time-to-impact, Redwood guarantees the following operational SLAs for all new joiners unless explicitly stated in the offer letter:

- Offer to Start Logistics: 3 business days for equipment approval and procurement initiation after signed offer.
- Device Provisioning: 7 business days from procurement order to delivery for standard desktop/laptop models. For remote hires where shipping delays occur, a loaner device is provided within 48 hours.
- Access & Accounts: 2 business days for core accounts (Google Workspace, Slack, HRIS). Additional services (cloud accounts, repo access) follow the checklist below.

New Hire Onboarding Checklist (Manager-owned items in bold)

1. Pre-start (People Ops / IT)
  a. Send welcome packet and first-week schedule (People Ops).
  b. Create core accounts and invite to team channels (IT/SRE).
  c. Ship equipment and VPN onboarding instructions (IT).

2. Day 0-3 (Manager + Buddy)
  a. Welcome meeting and role context (Manager).
  b. Buddy pairing established with 1:1 schedule for first 30 days (Manager).
  c. Goals for first 30/60/90 days documented in Go-To-Market plan (Manager & new hire).

3. Week 1 (Hiring Team)
  a. Product, infra, and security orientation sessions scheduled.
  b. Access requested for code repos, staging clusters, and internal dashboards.
  c. First small production task assigned to validate end-to-end access.

4. 30/60/90 Day Reviews
  - 30-Day: operational readiness check (Manager + People Ops).
  - 60-Day: feedback and training gap assessment.
  - 90-Day: calibration meeting; confirm role ramp and any adjustments.

Required items:

- Slack: add to #team, #eng-all and #product-all
- Okta: standard SSO groups
- GitHub: team repo access: [repo-list]
- Cloud: staging read, dev write; prod access on approval ticket ID [insert]
- Datadog: readonly, expand after training

Operational Owners & Escalation

- People Ops: owner for benefits enrollment, offer execution, and onboarding cadence.
- IT/Device Fleet: owner for procurement, loaner program, device retirements.
- Security: owner for access approvals to production systems.
- Manager: owner for role clarity, 30/60/90 planning, and performance check-ins.

Escalation path (if SLAs breached):
1) Submit a People Ops ticket (priority: onboarding-sla)
2) If unresolved after SLA window, escalate to the People Ops lead: people-lead@redwood.com
3) If still unresolved after 48 hours, escalate to Head of People Ops.

Internal Tools & Tooling Guides

Key tools used during first 90 days:
- HRIS (Workday/WorkOS): benefits enrollment, time-off requests.
- IT ticketing (Jira Service Desk): equipment and access requests.
- Confluence: central internal docs (this page).
- GitHub: code collaboration; temporary access policies described in Security onboarding.
- Datadog / Ops dashboards: read-only for new hires; training course required before escalation.

Quick CLI snippet: Requesting a loaner device (internal CLI called redwood-it)

$ redwood-it request-device --type loaner --duration 30 --user "jane.doe@redwood.com" --reason "start delay"

Manager Checklist for Role Changes & Promotions

- Submit promotion packet at least 4 weeks before the intended promotion date. Packet must include role expectation, evidence of impact, and at least two peer endorsements.
- People Ops will schedule calibration and notify payroll for compensation changes.

Guidance for International Hires

- Local statutory differences: time-off, parental leave, and taxes may differ. People Ops provides country supplements and local country managers should consult with payroll partners for specifics.
- Onboarding SLAs may extend due to shipping or local banking setup. Loaner programs partially mitigate delays.
