Talent intake, time-to-hire SLAs, and manager responsibilities

Overview

Purpose
This document defines the standardized talent intake workflow, time-to-hire SLAs, interview loop design expectations, and explicit manager responsibilities for hiring and for the first 90 days of onboarding. It unifies recruiter and manager handoffs, reduces time-to-fill variability, and creates clear escalation points to preserve candidate experience and enable predictable capacity planning.

Audience
- Hiring managers
- Recruiters
- People Partners
- Hiring committee members
- People-ops leadership

Context and goals
- Reduce average time-to-offer from 52 days to target SLA of 21 days for IC roles and 30 days for manager roles.
- Ensure every role has a signed intake and a calibrated scorecard before candidates are sourced.
- Define manager commitments during hiring and onboarding so People-ops can measure compliance and coach where needed.

Key definitions
- Time-to-hire (TTH): Days from Approved Intake (see step 2) to Offer Acceptance.
- Time-to-offer: Days from Approved Intake to Offer Extension.
- Approved Intake: Recruiter + Hiring Manager + Finance signoff documented in Greenlight Intake form.
- Calibrated scorecard: Role rubric with top-3 success criteria and interview question mapping.

1) Intake and approvals (owner: Hiring Manager, recruiter as co-owner)
1.1 Intake submission\- Manager opens Position Intake form in Greenlight within 3 business days of hiring need discovery. Include:
  - Role brief (one-paragraph), mission, org placement
  - Required vs. nice-to-have skills
  - Scorecard: top 3 success indicators and examples of evidence
  - Expected compensation band and remote/location constraints
  - Resourcing priority (replace/scale/new)

1.2 SLAs for approvals\- People-ops will respond to new intake submissions as follows:
  - Manager acknowledgement: immediate auto-receipt from Greenlight (0 days)
  - Recruiter assignment: within 2 business days
  - Finance compensation check: within 3 business days
  - Final intake approval (Recruiter + People Partner): within 5 business days

1.3 Reject/iterate criteria\- If salary/level misalignment or unclear scorecard, intake returns to manager with clear comments and expected revision window of 3 business days.

2) Interview loop design and calibration (owner: Recruiter + Hiring Manager)
2.1 Minimum standards for interview loops (applies to all IC and manager hires)
  - Screening call (30m) + technical or role assessment (take-home or pair; if used, include clear success rubric)
  - 3–5 onsite/virtual interviews capped at 4 hours total for ICs; manager loops may be up to 5 hours
  - Each interviewer receives the calibrated scorecard and explicit question mapping at least 24 hours before interview
  - Diversity of perspectives: at least one cross-functional stakeholder and one peer-level interviewer

2.2 Interviewer training and calibration\- People-ops runs quarterly interviewer calibration sessions. Managers must certify at least 2 interviewers per role as calibrated in Greenlight before scheduling onsite loops.

2.3 Interviewer no-no list
  - Do not ask for compensation history
  - Avoid hypothetical leading questions that are not scored on the rubric
  - Do not make offers or commitments outside Offer Authority matrix

3) Time-to-hire SLAs (owner: People-ops; metrics surfaced to manager dashboards)
3.1 Target metrics (measured per role family and level)
  - Target time-to-offer (IC): 14 business days from Approved Intake to Offer Extension
  - Target time-to-offer (Manager): 21 business days from Approved Intake to Offer Extension
  - Target time-to-accept (all roles): 7 business days from Offer Extension to Candidate Acceptance
  - Target time-to-start: 21 days from Offer Acceptance to Employee start date for internal hiring; 28 for external candidates

3.2 Escalation thresholds
  - Warning: role exceeds 14 business days (IC) without an offer — auto-notification to Hiring Manager + Recruiter
  - Escalation: role exceeds 21 business days (IC) — People-ops flags on weekly hiring review and schedules a triage meeting
  - Executive escalation: roles open > 45 calendar days for mission-critical positions

3.3 Measurement and dashboards\- Data sources: Greenlight intake metadata, ATS pipeline timestamps, compensation approvals. KPIs updated daily on People-ops hiring dashboard and available to managers via Manager Portal.

4) Offer approvals and negotiation playbook (owner: People-ops + Finance)
4.1 Offer checklist before extension
  - Approved budget and comp band documented
  - Background check / compliance required (role-dependent) flagged
  - Offer packet with role summary, manager expectations, and onboarding start window ready

4.2 Negotiation guardrails
  - Manager may approve up to +10% above midpoint for IC roles with People-ops signoff; >+10% requires VP/Finance approval
  - For counter-offers, maintain transparency and re-run basic reference checks if >15% variance from original terms

5) Manager responsibilities during hiring and first 90 days (owner: Hiring Manager)
5.1 Commitment windows
  - Intake completion: 3 business days from need identified
  - Interview feedback submitted: within 48 hours of interview completion
  - Final decision communicated to recruiter: within 2 business days after last onsite

5.2 Onboarding and first 90-day plan (must be published in Greenlight Onboard tab prior to start date)
  - Week 0: Welcome logistics, hardware, account provisioning checklist, first-day agenda
  - Week 1: 1:1 daily check-ins for ICs new to role; manager hires: daily plus 90-day goals discussion
  - Week 2–4: Role immersion — product demos, team pairing schedule, top-priority deliverables mapped to scorecard
  - Month 2: Mid-probation review (documented evidence vs. scorecard)
  - Month 3: Probation closeout and permanent goals set, or extension plan defined with concrete success criteria

5.3 Evaluation evidence required at checkpoints\- For every mid-probation and probation closeout, provide 3 artifacts of evidence mapped to the calibrated scorecard (examples: code review snapshots, sales calls recorded with consent, project summaries).

6) Performance feedback cadence alignment (owner: People-ops + Manager)
6.1 Tied to hiring scorecard\- First-cycle feedback must reference the original calibrated scorecard. Managers should avoid ad-hoc criteria that were not in the scorecard during hiring.

6.2 Coaching cadence during probation
  - Weekly 1:1s for first 60 days with a documented short note (one-paragraph) in Greenlight after each session
  - People-ops will auto-request a probation checkpoint report at Day 45 and Day 85

7) Failures, remediation, and role cancellation
7.1 Cancellation before offer
  - If headcount is paused, recruiter or People Partner changes intake state to "Paused" and notifies candidate slate within 48 hours.

7.2 Remediation plans after hire
  - If midpoint evidence at Day 45 is failing, manager creates a 30-day remediation plan with clear success criteria and a weekly review schedule. People-ops supports calibration and, if needed, escalates to HR for policy review.

7.3 Offboarding within probation
  - If termination is required during probation, follow the people-ops termination checklist and record decisions with evidence. People-ops will coordinate exit logistics and reallocation of budget.

8) Templates and artifacts (attached in Greenlight)
  - Intake template (required fields)
  - Calibrated scorecard template (3 success criteria, evidence mapping, interview question bucket)
  - Offer packet template
  - First-90-days plan template
  - Probation checkpoint report template

9) Operational handbook for recruiters and managers (short procedures)
9.1 Recruiter daily ritual
  - T+0: validate new intakes and schedule stakeholder alignment within 2 business days
  - T+2: pipeline kickoff with sourcers and initial candidate outreach
  - Weekly: Hiring review sync with manager to review top-5 slate

9.2 Manager interview and feedback procedure
  - Run calibration readout with interviewers before loops start
  - Submit feedback using the structured scorecard (numerical + narrative) within 48 hours
  - If an interviewer misses feedback window, recruiter has authority to accept late feedback up to 5 business days, after which interviewer is excluded from final decision unless escalated

10) Exceptions and fast-path for critical hires
  - Fast-path criteria: critical bug-fix roles, revenue-impact hires, legal/compliance escalations
  - Fast-path process: 24–48 hour intake vetting, accelerated interview loop with pre-approved interviewers, and Offer Authority delegated to VP+People-ops for same-day offers when warranted

11) Governance, audit, and continuous improvement
  - Quarterly audit of 10 random closed requisitions for compliance with intake, scorecard, and feedback SLAs. Findings published to People-ops readout.
  - Quarterly feedback loop with Hiring Managers to revise SLAs and reduce bottlenecks.

|--------|--------|-------|----------------------|
