Subscriber impact notification orchestration and verbatim scripts

Summary:
This playbook standardizes how we detect customer-impacting incidents, decide who gets notified, and craft clear, consistent messages for status pages, email, and account managers. It focuses on orchestration (who sends what, through which channel and when), escalation criteria tied to SLOs, and ready-to-send verbatim templates to reduce cognitive load during a high-severity incident.

Goals:
- Ensure stakeholders (customers, CS, Sales, Legal) receive timely, accurate information with minimal fragmentation.
- Reduce time-to-first-notice to under 10 minutes for Severity 1 incidents.
- Maintain a single source of truth (the incident bridge and the status page).

When to use this document:
- Any incident classified Severity 1–2 with measurable customer impact (errors, increased latency, failed requests, or degraded embeddings).
- Silent degradations that cross alert thresholds defined below.

Severity definitions and notification triggers:
1) Severity 1 (S1) — Platform outage or severe degradation affecting >10% of requests or any major enterprise customer. Trigger: SLO breach for 5 continuous minutes or on-call pager fire.
2) Severity 2 (S2) — Partial degradation affecting isolated regions or customers, increased error-rate >3x baseline for 15 minutes.
3) Severity 3 (S3) — Minor, intermittent customer impact, limited feature subset.

Notification thresholds (table):
- Metric / Threshold / Action
- 99th latency > 4x baseline for 5m / Open bridge and page customers (S1) / Page on-call + CS_alert
- Error rate > 3x baseline for 15m / Targeted customer notices + status page note (S2) / Page on-call and notify affected accounts
- KV cache miss storm causing token cost >2x for 30m / Internal ops note + product review (S3) / Triage in next shift

Ownership and channels:
- Incident Commander (IC): engineering SRE on-call — owns single source-of-truth and approves public messaging.
- Bridge Operator: opens/hosts bridge (Zoom/Slack/Google Meet), posts timeline, toggles status page drafts.
- Customer Success (CS) Liaison: crafts account-specific notices and coordinates PM/AM outreach.
- Legal/Compliance: review if sensitive customer data or regulatory scope.
- Channels: status.redwood.ai (status page), customer-email, in-app notices, enterprise Slack/Teams, account manager call.

Immediate on-call actions (first 10 minutes):
1) Acknowledge pager and join bridge.
2) Assign an IC and Bridge Operator in the bridge banner.
3) Determine preliminary severity and impacted surface area (use the Impact quick-check checklist below).
4) Post first-look line to status page (Draft) with timeframe, scope, and next update ETA.
5) Notify CS Liaison for S1/S2.
6) Begin evidence collection: logs, traces, metric graphs, request IDs.

Impact quick-check checklist (use rapid one-liners):
- Is production traffic failing or timing out? (yes/no)
- Are multiple regions affected? (list regions)
- Any enterprise customer alerts? (list accounts)
- Is degradation reproducible via synthetic tests? (yes/no)

Status page lifecycle template:
- Draft posting: short title, affected components, impact (e.g., Degraded / Partial outage), scope estimate, and ETA for next update.
- Update cadence: every 15 minutes while S1; every 30–60 minutes for S2 until mitigated.
- Resolution: clear recovery summary and steps taken; link to postmortem when available.

Verbatim message templates (replace placeholders {incident_id}, {component}, {eta}, {workaround}, {customer_list}):
- Initial public status page (S1):
  Title: {component} — Partial outage
  Body: We are investigating an issue impacting {component}. Some requests may fail or experience elevated latency. We have opened an incident ({incident_id}) and are actively working to restore service. Next update: {eta}.

- Initial customer email (enterprise-facing, S1):
  Subject: Immediate notice — service disruption impacting {component} ({incident_id})
  Body: Hi {customer_contact},

  We are actively investigating a service disruption that may affect your requests to {component}. We have opened incident {incident_id}. At this time we believe the impact is: {scope_summary}. The SRE team is working on mitigation. We will provide a status update by {eta}. For urgent concerns, reply to this email or contact your AM/CS lead.

  Apologies for the interruption — we will follow up with a root cause analysis after recovery.

- Update for status page / customer email: 
  Subject (email): Incident {incident_id} — update
  Body (status page): Update: We have identified the likely root cause (summary) and are testing a rollback/mitigation. Current impact: {impact_now}. ETA for next update: {eta}. Workaround: {workaround} if available.

- Resolution notice (status page + email):
  Title: Resolved — {component} incident {incident_id}
  Body: The incident affecting {component} has been resolved as of {resolved_time}. Summary of impact: {brief_impact}. Actions taken: {mitigation_steps}. If you observe ongoing issues, please contact support. A post-incident report will be published at {postmortem_link}.

- Escalation note for regulatory/PII exposures (internal only):
  Subject: URGENT — Potential data exposure {incident_id}
  Body: IC: please notify Legal and Security immediately. Do not send external notices until Legal clears messaging. Summarize: what data classes, affected customer list, logs/IDs collected, retention concerns.

Account-specific outreach guidance:
- For named enterprise customers (contracted SLAs): send targeted email + AM call within 15 minutes for S1. Use the enterprise email template above and include {customer_list}.
- For broad multi-tenant impact: use status page as canonical source and avoid multiple conflicting emails. CS should prepare account-specific context packs for any inbound AM/CS requests.

Post-incident actions and postmortem checklist:
1) Capture timeline in incident document (bridge transcript, key timestamps).
2) Collect evidence artifacts (logs, dashboards, runbooks used).
3) Assign authors and reviewers for the postmortem within 48 hours.
4) Publish a public-facing summary for customers within 72 hours for S1 incidents.
5) Identify action items, owners, and target dates. Track in the Operational Reliability board.

Game day exercises tied to this playbook (quarterly):
- Scenario A: Silent throughput regression (KV cache misconfiguration) — test detection-to-notice time under 15 minutes and validate customer email flow.
- Scenario B: Multi-region outage — validate cross-region routing fallback and enterprise AM call procedure.
- Scenario C: False-positive alert — practice rollback of premature customer notice and retraction messaging.

Templates and automation tips:
- Store canonical templates in the incident tool (PagerDuty templates) with variables.
- Automate initial status page draft via webhook from the bridge when IC is assigned.
- Keep an account-impact list updated in CRM with contact emails and escalation phone numbers.
