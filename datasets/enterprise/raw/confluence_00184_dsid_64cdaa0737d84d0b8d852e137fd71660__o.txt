operational-alignment-and-process-templates-2027

Overview:
This document defines Redwood Inference's cross-functional operational framework for access & permissions, change management, data management, procurement & expenses, vendor governance, travel policy, and the canon of internal templates teams must use. It consolidates decision authority, approval matrices, tooling ownership, and step-by-step procedures so that teams can operate consistently while meeting security, compliance, and finance requirements.

Scope and Purpose:
- Scope: Applies to all Redwood employees, contractors, and external vendors with access to company systems and data.
- Purpose: Reduce operational friction, ensure auditable controls, and standardize escalation paths.

1) Roles and Ownership
- Executive Sponsor: VP Finance (final approver for >$250K commitments).
- Policy Owner: Finance & Legal (primary), Security (co-owner for access and data controls), People Ops (co-owner for travel & role definitions).
- Service Owners and Tools:
  - Identity & Access Management: Redwood-Auth (owned by eng-infra).
  - Procurement & Expenses: SpendTrack (owned by finance).
  - Vendor Portal & Contracts: VendorHub (owned by finance-legal).
  - Data Platform and Retention: Redwood-DataLake (owned by eng-platform).
  - Change Management Ticketing: OpsFlow (owned by eng-sre).

2) Access & Permissions
Purpose: Provide least privilege access while enabling teams to move quickly.
Key principles:
- Role-based access is the default.
- Temporary elevated access requires Just-In-Time (JIT) approval and must be time-bound (max 24 hours for standard elevated requests).
- All access requests must have a documented business justification and owner.
Service-level targets:
- Standard access request SLA: 48 business hours from submission to grant or denial.
- JIT emergency approval SLA: 1 hour during business hours; 4 hours off-hours.
Process (step-by-step):
1. Requestor completes access request form in Redwood-Auth with: justification, resources, requested role, and owner.
2. Auto-route: if request is for pre-defined role -> manager approval -> system grant.
3. For privileged or cross-team roles: manager + service owner approval.
4. For vendor access: legal + service owner sign-off; contractor EULA signed and uploaded to VendorHub.
5. Audit: quarterly access reviews initiated by Security; service owners must certify privileges.
Exceptions:
- Permanent elevated access requires business case, training proof, and approval from Policy Owner and Security.

3) Change Management
Change categories:
- Standard Change: pre-approved, low-risk (e.g., minor config changes). Can be scheduled by service owners without CAB review if within guardrails.
- Major Change: affects availability, latency, or data model. Requires Change Advisory Board (CAB) review and written rollback plan.
- Emergency Change: requires immediate action to prevent or mitigate outage/security incident. Follow emergency change flow and post-facto CAB review within 72 hours.
Time windows and guardrails:
- Standard maintenance window: Saturdays 02:00–06:00 UTC for non-global-impact changes.
- Major changes require at least 72 hours notice and a staged rollout plan (canary ≥5% traffic, monitor 30 minutes, expand).
Change request template (fields):
- Title, owner, impact, rollback plan, validation steps, monitoring runbook, start/end window, risk level, related tickets.
Approval matrix (summary table):
| Change type | Approver(s) | Notification | Rollback required? | CAB needed? |
| Standard | Service owner | 24h | yes | no |
| Major | Service owner + Eng SRE + Product PM | 72h | yes | yes |
| Emergency | Incident commander + Relevant owners | Immediate | yes | Post-facto review |

4) Data Management
Classification levels:
- PUBLIC: safe for external distribution.
- INTERNAL: company operational data (default).
- SENSITIVE: PII, model training data with human labels, customer data requiring access controls and encryption-at-rest.
- RESTRICTED: regulated customer data (PCI, PHI) or data under contractual constraints.
Handling rules by classification:
- Encryption: SENSITIVE and RESTRICTED must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Access: RBAC + least privilege; RESTRICTED requires explicit legal/contract sign-off before access.
- Retention: default retention for INTERNAL is 2 years; SENSITIVE is 7 years or per contract; RESTRICTED per legal requirement.
Data transfer & export:
- Exports of SENSITIVE/RESTRICTED data to external storage require a Data Transfer Request approved by Data Platform + Legal.
- Personal Data deletion requests follow the Data Subject Request template; DSR must be completed within 30 calendar days.
Monitoring and audits:
- Quarterly data inventory updates by data owners.
- Annual tabletop review for high-risk datasets.

5) Procurement & Expenses
Principles:
- Decentralized request with centralized approvals and spend reporting.
- Use SpendTrack for all purchase requests, POs, and expense reports.
Approval thresholds (summary):
- <$5,000: Manager approval.
- $5,000–$50,000: Manager + Finance approval.
- $50,000–$250,000: Director + Finance + Legal review.
- >$250,000: VP Finance + Legal + Executive sponsor.
Procurement flow (example):
1. Submit Purchase Request in SpendTrack with vendor, SOW, estimated cost, GL code, and owner.
2. Finance verifies budget and issues PO.
3. Vendor onboarding in VendorHub (see section 6).
4. Invoice routing: vendor uploads invoice to SpendTrack; Finance matches to PO and pays per Net30 unless otherwise negotiated.
Expense policy highlights:
- Per diem: $75/day domestic, $125/day international (or actuals with receipts up to these caps).
- Hotel: up to $300/night domestic, up to $450/night in high-cost regions with pre-approval.
- Travel approval: manager + People Ops approval for international travel; 10 business days notice recommended.
Fraud and exceptions:
- Any suspected fraud escalated to Finance + Security within 24 hours.

6) Vendor Management
Onboarding checklist:
1. VendorHub profile created with primary contact, legal entity, and risk profile.
2. Security questionnaire (SIG-lite) completed for all vendors.
3. Contract review by Legal; required SLAs and data handling addenda attached for RESTRICTED/SENSITIVE data.
4. Finance sets payment terms in SpendTrack.
5. Operational contact assigned and runbook shared if vendor interfaces with infra.
Ongoing governance:
- Quarterly vendor risk reviews for high and medium risk.
- SLAs & penalties must be codified in contracts for production-impacting vendors (e.g., uptime targets, incident MTTR).
Offboarding:
- Revoke access via Redwood-Auth, collect assets, confirm data deletion per contract, final invoice reconciliation.

7) Travel & Expenses (detailed)
Pre-approval requirements:
- International travel requires completed Travel Request in SpendTrack with risk assessment and manager + People Ops sign-off.
- Travel must align with business objectives; training or personal travel must be split on expense reports.
Reimbursement timelines:
- Submit expense report within 30 days of travel end. Finance processes reimbursements within 10 business days after receipt.
Receipts and documentation:
- Receipts required for all amounts >$25. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.

8) Template Library and Standard Forms
- Access request form (Redwood-Auth form ID: ARF-2025).
- Change request template (OpsFlow: CR-STD).
- Vendor onboarding checklist (VendorHub template: VON-01).
- Data Transfer Request (DTR-01).
- Travel request & expense report templates (SpendTrack TR-2024 / ER-2024).
Location: All templates are published in the company Confluence template gallery and linked from the Finance & Legal team wiki.

9) Audit, Review and Continuous Improvement
- Policy review cadence: annual full review; interim updates for regulatory or tooling changes.
- KPIs to track: average access SLA, number of emergency changes, procurement cycle time, vendor SLA compliance, and travel expense processing time.
- Reporting: Monthly operational metrics in the Redwood Console (Operations Dashboard) and quarterly executive summary to Finance/COO.
