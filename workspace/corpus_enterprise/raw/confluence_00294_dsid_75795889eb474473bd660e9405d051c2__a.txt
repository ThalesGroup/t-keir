Authorization Board: Evidence Synthesis and Retention Catalog

Purpose:
This page codifies the Authorization Board's approach to assembling, validating, and retaining evidence for authorization-related decisions across RBAC, audit logging, data residency, SDLC gates, incident response, and risk exceptions. It is intended for Board members, InfoSec, Engineering leads, and Compliance owners.

Scope:
- All production-facing access changes that affect model serving, customer data access, or tenant isolation.
- SDLC release gates that touch entitlement, cryptographic key handling, or logging schemas.
- Incident evidence for authorization-related incidents (unauthorized access, privilege escalation, service-account compromise).

Summary / Executive guidance:
The Authorization Board must be able to answer three questions for every decision or exception:
1) Who made the change? (provenance)
2) What did they change and why? (scope and rationale)
3) How long will we retain evidence and where? (retention and locality)

Primary Roles & Responsibilities:
- Authorization Board (chair: Security PM): final sign-off for cross-domain exceptions and high-impact entitlement changes.
- InfoSec (owner): maintains evidence schemas, runbooks, and audit-query templates.
- Engineering (service owners): produce change artifacts, rollout notes, authorizing PR IDs, and test evidence.
- SRE / Observability: ensure streaming audit logs, retention, and immutable store integrations.
- Legal / Compliance: set regulatory locality & retention requirements for specific customers or tenants.

Principles:
- Minimal Blast Radius: prefer timebound, scoped entitlements with automated deprovisioning.
- Immutable Evidence: critical authorization events must be persisted to an append-only store (see Audit Schema).
- Contextual Packaging: combine logs + PR metadata + deployment artifact + incident timeline into a single evidence bundle for audits.
- Defense in Depth: combine RBAC, attribute filters, and runtime policy checks; evidence must reflect all relevant layers.

RBAC Patterns and Required Artifacts:
- Pattern: Role-Based + Attribute Filtered (recommended for customer-facing endpoints).
  Required artifacts per change: role-definition YAML, PR link, authorizing ticket, canary/rollback plan, access-review snapshot (pre-change), sampling of affected identities (<= 20 ids for review).
- Pattern: Timebound Elevation (JIT): must include justification, duration, automatic expiry timestamp, and post-expiry verification job.
- Pattern: Service Identity Change (keys/roles): key rotation proof, new key fingerprint, previous key revocation proof, CI build artifact IDs.

Audit Logging Requirements (minimum contract):
- Every authorization decision must emit a structured event with the following fields (pipe table):

| field | type | description | retention-tier |
|-------|------|-------------|----------------|
| event_id | uuid | unique event identifier | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| timestamp | ISO8601 | event time | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| actor_id | string | user or service principal | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| actor_type | enum | human | service | system | hot |
| action | string | e.g., grant_role, revoke, evaluate_acl | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| target_resource | string | resource identifier (model, dataset, route) | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| outcome | enum | allowed | denied | error | hot:30d / cold:3y |
| evidence_refs | list | links to PRs / tickets / deployment IDs | cold:3y |
| locality_tag | string | region/residency label | cold:3y |

Notes: the "evidence_refs" field must contain internal links or object ids; logs without evidence_refs trigger a pre-review alert.

Data Residency & Retention Mapping (catalog):
- Table (pipe):
| artifact | default-retention | storage-class | residency constraint | owner |
|---------|------------------|---------------|----------------------|-------|
| auth-audit-hot | 30 days | hot-ssd (central observability) | none (index only) | observability-team |
| auth-audit-cold | 3 years | object-store-immutable | region-tagged per-tenant | observability-team |
| evidence-bundles | 7 years | immutable-archive | per-customer residency if requested | security |
| sdlc-gate-artifacts | 5 years | object-store | global | eng-platform |
| exception-approvals | 10 years | secure-vault | restricted (legal sensitive) | security/legal |

SDLC Gate Checklist (authorization-impacting changes):
Pre-merge (owner):
1) Attach role-definition and sample policy to PR.
2) Add "authorization-impact" label and link to ticket.
3) Run unit tests including attribute-filtered access tests.
4) Provide mock audit events showing expected log output.

Pre-release (release manager):
1) Confirm evidence_refs are populated with PR and ticket IDs.
2) Confirm canary target and rollback policy.
3) Confirm retention-class of produced logs conforms to tenant residency.

Emergency patch flow (2-step signoff):
1) Quick-approve by on-call engineer + on-call security; create post-facto authorization ticket within 4 hours.
2) Evidence bundle must be assembled and presented to Authorization Board within 48 hours.

Incident Response: Evidence Collection Steps (authorization incidents):
1) Triage: tag incident as AUTHZ and attach incident-id to all artifacts.
2) Preserve hot logs (copy snapshot) and mark snapshots immutable.
3) Export evidence bundle:
   - PRs, merge commits, CI logs (build ids)
   - Deployment manifests and rollout timestamps
   - Audit events (filter: event_type in [grant_role, revoke_role, evaluate_acl])
   - Key material rotation proofs and KMS audit events
   - Access reviews and approval tickets
4) For tenant-impact incidents, consult Legal on cross-border copies before exporting.
5) Hand off to Forensics: provide query templates and a preferred time window.

Compliance Evidence Workflow (packaging & submission):
1) Create evidence-bundle object in Evidence Service (artifact id).
2) Populate metadata: incident_id/exception_id, owner_team, retention_class, residency_tag.
3) Attach artifacts (log snapshots, PRs, tickets, screenshots) and a short executive summary (<=300 words).
4) Board Review: Authorization Board evaluates bundle within 15 business days for high-risk items.
5) Archive: on approval, evidence-bundle is sealed to immutable-archive and retention starts.

Decision & Exception Handling (Authorization Board procedure):
1) Submission: engineering or security submits an Exception Request with the minimal required artifacts.
2) Triage: Board Secretary assesses completeness within 3 business days.
3) Review: Board meets (synchronous or async) and votes; required vote thresholds: security + one engineering + one compliance/legal.
4) Conditions: Board may add conditional mitigations (e.g., additional logging, scoped timebound limits).
5) Approval: Board records decision and shipping ticket; evidence-bundle is created at approval time.
6) Expiry & Re-evaluation: all approvals default to 90 days unless a longer term is justified and documented.

Retention schedule (quick reference):
- Hot auth audit: 30 days
- Cold auth audit (immutable): 3 years (default), extended per regulation to 7+ years
- Evidence bundles for incidents: 7 years (default) or longer if legal requirement
- Exception approvals and change logs: 10 years

Query templates (examples):
- Find all grant_role events by actor:
  SELECT * FROM auth_audit_cold WHERE actor_id = 'svc-deploy-123' AND action = 'grant_role' AND timestamp >= '2026-01-01T00:00:00Z';
- Evidence bundle retrieval:
  curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" 
       https://evidence.internal/api/v1/bundles/{bundle_id} | jq '.'

  "locality_tag": "eu-west-1"
}

Operational Alerts & Automation:
- Missing evidence_refs: alert to submitter and Board secretary after 24 hours.
- Timebound approval expiry: automated reminder 7 days before expiry.
- Post-approval verification: scheduled job at expiry to confirm deprovisioning ran.

Acceptance criteria for auditability (must be met before approval):
- Events are searchable by event_id and actor_id for the required retention period.
- Evidence-bundles include at least one immutable artifact (log snapshot) plus change metadata.
- Residency requirements validated by Legal/Compliance for affected tenants.
