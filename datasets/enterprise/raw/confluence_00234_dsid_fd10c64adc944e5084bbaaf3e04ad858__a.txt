Access & Ops Playbook for Remote-First, Procurement, and Contract Approvals

Overview

Purpose
This playbook defines evergreen practices and step-by-step procedures covering five cross-cutting areas used by all Redwood teams: access & permissions lifecycle, approved change management pathways, data stewardship minimums, procurement & expense approvals, and vendor/travel intake templates. The goal is to reduce friction for remote-first teams while preserving least-privilege, auditability, and financial controls.

Scope
Applies to: all Redwoood employees, contractors, and vendors who request system access, submit procurement/expense requests, or manage changes impacting production systems. Exceptions must be requested via the Risk & Exceptions process (see "Requesting an Exception" below).

Key principles
- Least privilege: grant minimal access required for a task and enforce time-bounded entitlements.
- Separation of duties: approvals are split across requestor, technical owner, and finance/legal as appropriate.
- Remote-first friendliness: friction-reduced templates, automated approvals for low-risk requests, and synchronous approval SLAs for high-risk items.
- Auditability: every access and procurement action must link to a ticket (Jira ID) and include approval metadata for audits.

1) Roles and ownership
- Requestor: individual initiating access, change, or procurement. Responsible for describing business justification and SLA.
- Technical Owner: owner of the resource/service (e.g., runtime-team@redwood.com). Confirms technical validity and risk impact.
- Approver-Finance: finance-legal representative for spend > $5k.
- Approver-Security: security team member for high-scope access (data classified as PII or regulated).
- Access Steward: team responsible for periodic entitlement reviews (quarterly).

Contact matrix (examples)

2) Access & permissions lifecycle
Minimum required fields for an access request (Jira template: ACCESS-REQ):
- Requestor name / email
- Justification & expiration date (required for contractors)
- Resource identifier (ARN, service name, dashboard link)
- Least-privilege role requested and list of actions
- Technical owner approval (required)
- Security sensitivity level: public | internal | restricted | regulated

Standard approval pathways
- Low-risk (read-only to internal services, spend < $1k): auto-approve within 8 hours if technical owner signs off via Slack/Confluence link.
- Medium-risk (write access to internal infra, non-production credentials): Technical Owner + Access Steward approval within 24 hours.
- High-risk (production write, PII access, KMS/HSM keys): Technical Owner + Security + Finance approval; must complete expansion checklist and security review; SLA 48 hours.

Entitlement management
- Time-bound access: default max duration 90 days for contractors and 365 days for employees; extensions require reapproval.
- Quarterly entitlement review: Access Stewards run reports (IAM tool: AegisIAM) and reconcile stale privileges.
- Emergency break-glass: requests via the oncall channel with post-facto Jira ticket and 24-hour incident debrief.

3) Change management (for infra, runtime, and customer-impacting changes)
Change classes and required artifacts
- Class A (Standard): routine config changes with a playbook, pre-approved rollback, no customer-facing impact. Requires Change Request ticket, automated CI test, and scheduled maintenance window.
- Class B (Significant): deployments that modify runtimes, stateful DB schema changes, or cross-cluster network changes. Requires Design Review (ADR link), runbook, canary plan, and request for change (RFC) with 48-hour review.
- Class C (High-risk / Emergency): production outages, security patches affecting secrets/KMS. Requires immediate oncall coordination, postmortem, and retrospective review.

Change request checklist (RFC template):
1. Summary and Jira ID
2. Owner and approver list
3. Risk classification and rollback plan
4. Pre-deploy validation steps and test commands
5. Canary rollout plan (traffic percentages and metrics)
6. Post-deploy validation metrics and SLO targets (latency, error rate)

SLOs and guardrails for deployed changes
- Production latency: no increase > 5% for 30m post-deploy relative to baseline.
- Error rate: no sustained > 2x spike in 15m window.
- If SLO breached, execute automatic rollback policy.

Automated gating examples (CI snippet)
- Run: make infra-plan && terraform apply -target=module.safe_changes --auto-approve
- Safety check: ./scripts/check-deploy-guardrails --canary 10 --observe 15m

4) Data management requirements
Classification levels
- Public: allowed to be shared externally.
- Internal: business data not intended for public release.
- Restricted: sensitive business or employee data; access limited to named roles.
- Regulated: PII/PHI/financial data subject to compliance controls (GDPR/PCI/ISO27001).

Minimum controls by classification
- Encryption at rest + in-transit: required for Restricted and Regulated.
- Audit logging: capture user ID, source IP, action, timestamp. Retain audit logs for 2 years for Regulated, 1 year for Restricted.
- Data residency: regulated datasets must be pinned to approved regions; list available in Data Residency matrix (appendix link).

Data handling checklist for projects
- Data minimization statement: why this dataset is necessary.
- Retention policy assigned & expiration date.
- Access matrix: list of users with access and justification.
- Masking/encryption strategy for non-production copies.

5) Procurement & expenses workflow
Spend thresholds and approval steps
- Under $200: manager-level approval via Expense form (automated).
- $200–$5,000: manager + finance designated approver.
- $5,000–$50,000: manager + procurement + finance.
- > $50,000: manager + procurement + finance + VP level sign-off and legal review for contract terms.

Procurement request fields (PRQ template):
- Business justification & Jira link
- Cost center and budget owner
- Preferred vendor, SOW/quote attachment, contract term length
- Security and data handling classification (is vendor processing regulated data?)
- Requested date and desired delivery/activation date

Vendor selection and negotiation guidance
- Standard vendors (SaaS infra, cloud providers) are pre-approved; use vendor-approved templates. Non-standard vendors require RFP and vendor risk assessment.
- For any vendor with access to Regulated data: require SOC 2 Type II report (or equivalent), encryption attestation, and a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

Payment & billing
- Finance issues purchase orders (POs) for contractual spend > $5k.
- Contractors and small one-off purchases should use the corporate card with receipts uploaded to Expensify within 7 days.

6) Vendor management and contracts
Onboarding checklist for vendors with system access
- Completed vendor intake form + Jira ticket
- Security questionnaire (provided by security-ops) and evidence (SOC reports, pen test summaries)
- Contractual terms: NDAs, DPA, liability limits, termination clauses, and IP assignments as applicable
- Technical onboarding: least-privilege credentials, IP allowlists, and documented support contacts

Periodic review cadence
- Annual risk review for vendors handling Restricted/Regulated data.
- Quarterly performance review for strategic vendors (uptime SLAs, support metrics).

7) Corporate travel and remote work expense policy highlights
- Per diem and lodging caps defined by region (full matrix in appendix). Use company travel portal (TravelPortal) and preferred booking partners.
- Approval: manager pre-approval required for out-of-country travel; for trips > 5 days require People Ops sign-off.
- Expense submission timeline: receipts uploaded within 14 days of trip end. Card reconciliations within 30 days.
- Safety: employees traveling to Medium/High risk countries must register trip via TravelSafety and enroll in emergency assistance.

8) Templates and quick-start checklists (links and examples)
- ACCESS-REQ (Jira template id: ACCESS-REQ): minimal fields + IAM snippet inclusion.
- PRQ (Procurement Request): includes vendor risk section and budget owner approval.
- RFC-CHANGE: change request with canary rollout plan and rollback steps.
- VENDOR-ONBOARD: vendor intake form checklist.

Example procurement YAML (attach to PRQ):
---
vendor: \"Acme Observability\"
quote_amount: 18000
contract_term_months: 12
dpa_required: true
cost_center: \"ENG-PLATFORM\"
justification: |\
  Replace legacy tracing system with vendor-managed SaaS to reduce MTTR and support distributed tracing for Redwood services.
---

9) Requesting an exception
- Exceptions go through the Risk & Exceptions board: file EXC-{team}-{YYYY}{NN} with rationale, compensating controls, and timebox. Exceptions are time-boxed (max 90 days) and require sponsor from the owner_team.

10) Enforcement, monitoring, and continuous improvement
- Monthly dashboards: top 10 stale entitlements, open procurement requests older than SLA, vendors with expiring evidence.
- Quarterly tabletop exercises covering break-glass and vendor incident response.
- Annual policy review owned by finance-legal; suggested updates accepted via PR on the policy repo and reviewed by the board.
