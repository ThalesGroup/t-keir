Customer-facing rollback playbook and communication templates

Summary
-------
This document provides a practical, customer-facing rollback playbook for private (VPC and on-prem) deployments. It focuses on: decision criteria, coordinated communication templates (email, status page, Slack, account manager scripts), a timed checklist for field engineers and customer success, and the artifacts we must collect for compliance and postmortem. This complements technical rollback runbooks maintained by the engineering team; it is the canonical source for customer communications during a rollback event.

Goals
-----
- Protect customer SLAs and expectations through clear, timely communication.
- Ensure consistent messaging across channels (status page, email, AM calls, field notes).
- Minimize churn and trust erosion by setting honest timelines and follow-through.
- Preserve evidence and timelines required for audits and customer compliance reviews.

When to use this playbook
-------------------------
Use this playbook when any of the following apply to a private deployment rollback:
1) A model or runtime change has caused production degradation (latency or accuracy SLA breaches) for >10% of requests for >15 minutes.
2) A deployment upgrade fails in canary/tenant-slice and risk of wider impact is high.
3) Customer escalates and requests immediate revert due to business impact.
4) Compliance or legal constraints require immediate state preservation and rollback (e.g., data residency or regulatory failure).

Decision criteria matrix (short)
-------------------------------
| Signal | Threshold | Action owner | Immediate customer message? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 95th p90 latency increase | >2x baseline sustained 15m | Runtime oncall | Yes (status + AM) |
| Error rate (5xx) | >3% for 5m | Service owner | Yes (status) |
| Model quality regression (eval) | >5% score drop on golden set | ML oncall | Yes (AM + scheduled deep-dive) |
| Canary failure impacting sample customers | Any data corruption risk | Release manager | Yes (immediate AM + legal if needed) |

Roles and responsibilities
--------------------------
- Customer Success (CS): primary communicator to customer, coordinates AM/SE join, validates customer messaging, logs customer consent for rollback steps.
- Release Manager: executes rollback plan (orchestration), confirms target commit, verifies artifacts, and updates status.
- Field/Operator (Ops): performs on-prem steps (service stop, image revert, KV cache eviction), shares runbook outputs.
- Engineering Oncall: provides triage, telemetry interpretation, and approval for roll-forward after rollback.
- Legal/Compliance: consulted when rollback involves data preservation, evidence export, or contractual notice requirements.

Communication cadence and channels
--------------------------------
1) Immediate broadcast (T+0): post to status page and send templated email to the customer primary contacts. Mark message with severity and short ETA.
2) T+15 minutes: follow-up message with initial findings and expected next update. Schedule a 30-minute check-in call if customer impact continues.
3) T+60 minutes: provide action taken (rollback initiated/completed), verification steps customer can run, and updated ETA for full resolution.
4) Post-event (T+24–72 hours): publish incident summary, artifacts (logs, timeline), and remediation plan. Offer a technical liaison session.

Templates (do not edit core placeholders except ETA and links)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Status page snippet (short):
  Incident: Partial degradation for <customer-tenant-id> in eu-west-1 for private deployment.
  Impact: Increased response latency and errors for a subset of requests.
  Action: We have initiated a rollback to the prior stable model/runtime. Next update: {{ETA_15m}}.

- Customer email (initial):
  Subject: Action underway to restore your Redwood private deployment (incident {{INC_ID}})

  Hi {{Customer Primary}},

  We detected degraded behavior impacting your deployment ({{brief symptom}}). Our engineers have initiated a rollback to the previous stable configuration to restore normal service.

  What to expect now:
  - Rolling back model/runtime to commit {{COMMIT_ID}}. Estimated completion: {{ETA}}.
  - No customer data will be removed; we are preserving runtime state and logs for review.
  - Recommended verification: run the smoke test at {{SMOKE_TEST_URL}} once you receive completion confirmation.

  Your technical contact: {{AM Name}} ({{AM email}}). We will update you at {{ETA_15m}} with progress.

  Regards,
  Redwood Inference Customer Success

- Slack/IM alert (for account team):
  [INC {{INC_ID}}] Rollback started for {{customer}} (region: {{region}}). RM: @release-manager, Ops: @oncall-op, Eng: @eng-oncall. Next update T+15m.

Operational checklist (timeboxed)
--------------------------------
- T-0 (detect):
  - CS notifies customer and AM initiates internal call.
  - Release Manager confirms rollback target and posts to status page.
- T+5m:
  - Ops performs pre-rollback snapshot (if supported): capture PV backup, DB snapshot reference, and configuration snapshot.
  - Save the following artifacts: deployment manifest, observed telemetry windows, canary logs.
- T+10m:
  - Begin rollback orchestration (or manual revert steps for on-prem). Record start timestamp.
- T+20m:
  - Verify health endpoints return expected responses for smoke tests.
  - If healthy, communicate 'rollback complete' and provide verification steps.
- T+24h:
  - Deliver postmortem and evidence bundle to customer. Include: timeline, logs (redacted per customer policy), and proposed mitigations.

Required artifacts and retention table
------------------------------------
| Artifact | Who collects | Min retention (customer request) | Format |
| Deployment manifest | Ops | 90 days | YAML/JSON |
| Orchestration logs | Release Manager | 180 days | plaintext + structured events |
| Telemetry slice | Eng Oncall | 90 days | perf/export (parquet) |
| Snapshots (if taken) | Ops | per-customer policy | object storage reference |

Technical signals and useful commands (examples)
------------------------------------------------
- Query rollout state (control-plane API):
  curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" https://controlplane.redwood.example/api/v1/deployments/{{DEPLOYMENT_ID}}/rollout | jq

- Snapshot trigger (onprem operator helper):
  rdwctl snapshot create --deployment {{DEPLOYMENT}} --reason "pre-rollback-{{INC_ID}}"

- Status page API example (JSON payload):
  POST /api/incidents
  {
    "incident_id": "{{INC_ID}}",
    "summary": "Rollback started for tenant {{TENANT}}",
    "severity": "major",
    "links": ["{{RUNBOOK_URL}}"],
    "eta": "{{ETA}}"
  }

Escalation and legal checklist
------------------------------
- If rollback affects customer data or regulatory controls: notify Legal (legal-incident@redwood.example) and Compliance immediately (within 30 minutes).
- If customer contract includes notification SLA (e.g., 60-minute notification), confirm legal timeframe and include signed acknowledgement where required.
- For customers in regulated industries, include an audit copy of artifacts with redaction notes and a statement of non-repudiation.

Post-event deliverables
-----------------------
- Completed timeline with stamps for each action (UTC).
- Evidence bundle: logs, snapshots, manifests, and the verification test results.
- Customer-facing postmortem: 1–2 page summary, technical appendix (optional), remediation plan and ETA for permanent fix.
- Internal retrospective: at least one engaged engineer, product manager, and CS owner present within 5 business days.

Appendix: sample verification checklist for customer
--------------------------------------------------
1) Confirm service end-to-end latency: run the sample client test (curl) three times, median p95 < baseline.
2) Validate model outputs against three golden prompts provided during onboarding.
3) Confirm no unexpected 5xx errors in the last 10 minutes (search logs or use perf dashboard).
