Autoscaling Fidelity Marketplace PRD and Commercialization Playbook

Overview

This document defines the Product Requirements (PRD) and launch playbook for the Autoscaling Fidelity Marketplace — a product capability that automatically selects and scales response-fidelity variants (model variants, quantization levels, KV-cache profiles) against a commercial marketplace of fidelity SKUs. The feature couples runtime autoscaling decisions with commercial entitlements so customers can trade token-cost, latency, and quality dynamically.

Audience
- Product managers, commercial ops, pricing, engineering (serving runtime, platform), and solutions engineering.

Goals
- Provide a production-ready API and Console UX that allows tenants to:
  - Opt into an autoscaling fidelity marketplace with guardrails.
  - Define fidelity budgets and per-route fidelity SLAs.
  - Observe fidelity-driven economics in near-real-time and receive recommendations.
- Reduce average per-token cost for medium-to-high throughput tenants by 18–35% for conversational workloads without measurable quality regressions on customer-defined eval sets.
- Ensure autoscaling decisions respect latency SLOs and tenant isolation (no cross-tenant leaking of cost/variants).

Non-goals
- Not intended to replace manual model-pinning for customers requiring deterministic outputs.
- Not an open marketplace of third-party models in initial launch; variants are Redwood-verified and curated.

Key persona scenarios
1) SaaS product engineer: Wants to lower monthly inference bill while maintaining 95th percentile latency <= 250ms for interactive routes.
2) Platform lead at enterprise: Needs per-route fidelity SLAs and the ability to cap upstream spend per team.
3) Solutions engineer: Onboards customers to a fidelity budget and needs a reproducible rollout plan.

Functional requirements
- FR1: Autoscaler Policy Engine
  - Accepts per-route policy with fields: budget (credits/min), latency_SLO_ms, min_fidelity_variant, max_fidelity_variant, fallthrough_strategy (pin|downgrade|queue).
  - Computes per-request variant assignment and scaling action (promote/demote) in < 5ms on median decision path.
- FR2: Marketplace Catalog
  - Exposes curated fidelity SKUs with metadata: quality_score, expected_tokens_per_response_multiplier, average_latency_ms, quantization_level, recommended_use_cases.
- FR3: Entitlement and Billing Hooks
  - Integrates with billing to charge per-FU (fidelity unit) at time of request; supports committed and overage pricing.
- FR4: Telemetry and Explainability
  - Emit per-decision telemetry (variant_id, reason, pre/post estimated cost delta) to runtime telemetry stream and Console.
- FR5: Eval and Regression Capture
  - Shadow-eval mode captures outputs for a fraction of traffic for side-by-side quality checks; supports per-tenant promptsets.

Non-functional requirements
- NFR1: Isolation — decisions for one tenant must not use other tenants' telemetry as direct inputs (only aggregated market signals).
- NFR2: Availability — autoscaler control-plane must meet 99.95% uptime and 99.9% for marketplace reads.
- NFR3: Latency — additional decision latency must not increase tail response latency by more than 10ms p95 for interactive routes.
- NFR4: Cost accuracy — billing exposures from autoscaling must reconcile within 1 hour with offline metering.

Acceptance criteria
- AC1: End-to-end demo where a test tenant reduces average cost-per-token by >= 20% while keeping specified eval metrics within +-2% of baseline.
- AC2: Rollout automation (canary -> staged -> global) implemented in Console with rollback triggers: fidelity-regression-alert, cost-overrun-alert (>20% above budget), latency-SLO-breach (>5% violation).

Telemetry and success metrics
- Metrics to monitor (per-tenant and aggregated):
  - Average FU per request, FU variance, tokens-per-request, cost-per-1000-tokens.
  - Quality regression rate (eval set pass/fail), fidelity-promote/demote counts, decision latency distribution.
  - Business KPIs: average customer spend reduction, churn correlation, activation rate of autoscaler.

Console UX and admin flows
- New page: "Fidelity Marketplace" under Tenant Settings.
- Key screens:
  - Setup wizard (select routes, upload eval promptset, choose budget template).
  - Policy editor (JSON and guided form).
  - Live insights (heatmaps of variant usage, per-route cost waterfall).
  - Rollout control (canary %, rate, and automatic rollback toggles).

API contract (example)
- POST /v1/tenants/{tenant_id}/fidelity-policies
- Request body (example):
  {
    "route_id": "chat.send",
    "budget_credits_per_min": 1200,
    "latency_slo_ms": 250,
    "min_variant": "gpt-lite-q4",
    "max_variant": "gpt-standard-fp16",
    "fallthrough": "downgrade",
    "canary": {"enabled": true, "initial_pct": 5}
  }

- Decision response sample (streamed to telemetry):
  {
    "request_id": "r_12345",
    "variant_assigned": "gpt-lite-q4",
    "reason": "budget_pressure",
    "estimated_cost_delta": "-0.0025"
  }

Pricing and packaging model (commercial options)
- Pricing design principles:
  - Keep per-FU pricing transparent and additive to baseline per-token pricing.
  - Offer committed FU bundles with sliding discounts and a preemptible warm-capacity option for batch-heavy workloads.

- Example packages (table):
  | SKU | Committed FUs / month | Typical target workload | Discount vs on-demand | Notes |
  | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
  | FM-Silver | 100k | Low-throughput dev & sandbox | 10% | On-demand overflow at standard FU price |
  | FM-Gold | 1M | Production interactive apps, 10–50 qps | 22% | Includes 1 dev support review & monthly recommendation report |
  | FM-Enterprise | 10M+ | High-throughput transactional apps | 35% | SLA-backed allocation & dedicated recommender hook |

Billing hooks and exposure controls
1) Per-route soft budget: automatic throttling and optional email alerts at 75/90/100% thresholds.
2) Hard spend caps: enforced via request rejection with clear error codes and mitigation guidance.
3) Reconciliation: daily FU usage rollups and an hourly delta stream for near-real-time charge surfaces.

Rollout plan (engineering + go-to-market)
1) Phase 0 (Q1) — Internal beta with Redwood teams: validate decision latency, billing reconciliation.
2) Phase 1 (Q2) — Closed private preview with 3 pilot customers from different segments (SaaS, marketplace, enterprise).
3) Phase 2 (Q3) — Public beta with Console UX and limited GA SKUs (Silver, Gold).
4) Phase 3 (Q4) — GA launch: Enterprise SKU, SLA contracts, partner enablement and documented playbooks for migration.

Risks and mitigations
- Risk: Quality regressions for long-tail prompts. Mitigation: mandatory shadow-eval capture and per-tenant rollback window; require quality pass before enterprise activation.
- Risk: Unexpected billing spikes from misconfigured policies. Mitigation: default soft budgets and enforced hard caps for first 30 days of tenant activation.
- Risk: Decision latency causing tail latency SLO breaches. Mitigation: fall back to pinned-variant path and provide telemetry-driven alerting.
