First-Contact Flowbook and CSR Bridge Handshake

Summary:
This page documents the "first-contact" operator flow for any production incident affecting customer-facing inference (hosted API / Dedicated). It codifies the handshake between the first on-call operator and Customer Success/CSR (the "CSR Bridge"), an evidence snapshot format, the triage decision ladder, and templated customer status messages.

Audience:
- SREs, on-call engineers, incident commanders, CSR/Support L1-L3, and Product PMs responsible for customer comms.

Scope:
- Applies to P0/P1/P2 severity events touching API availability, major latency regressions, KV cache corruption, or model-quality regressions that generate customer tickets.
- Does NOT replace component-specific runbooks (see linked runbook library).

Quick reference: severity to action map
1) P0 (outage impacting >10% requests or control plane failures) — immediate bridge, page on-call IC, open incident bridge, status page 15-minute notice.
2) P1 (significant degradation, customer-facing but partial) — notify CSR bridge, open short bridge, begin mitigations within 30 minutes.
3) P2 (minor service regression / performance variance) — ticketed, track in backlog, notify stakeholders if customer-reported.

1) First-contact operator flow (step-by-step)
A. Acknowledge alert (0–2 minutes):
  - Confirm alert source (metric alert, external customer report, Sentry/trace).
  - Set working hypothesis label on incident channel: "latency","errors","capacity","quality".
B. Rapid evidence snapshot (2–8 minutes): collect the Evidence Snapshot (table below).
C. Initial mitigation attempt (8–20 minutes):
  - For latency: apply emergency routing to region fallback OR scale up critical pools as allowed by autoscale policy.
  - For errors: enable guarded fallback model (quantized variant) if validated in service matrix.
  - For KV cache or state corruption: isolate nodes and enable read-only fallback; do not evict caches unless instructed by runtime owner.
D. CSR Bridge handshake (10–25 minutes) — see Handshake script below.
E. Decide: containment vs escalate (20–30 minutes): if mitigation removes >50% customer errors/latency within 20 minutes, continue containment and move to stabilization; otherwise escalate to IC and call for cross-team bridge.

2) The CSR Bridge handshake (objective: align external comms and customer impact quickly)
- Who: first-contact operator (on-call), CSR bridge lead (L2), incident commander (if IC present).
- When: invoked by the on-call operator once an evidence snapshot and a working mitigation attempt are in place (recommended within 25 minutes of first alert).
- Goals:
  1) Confirm which customers are impacted (tenant list or telemetry-derived list).
  2) Agree on an initial public status message and cadence.
  3) Determine whether affected customers need immediate 1:1 outreach.
  4) Assign CSR owner(s) for ongoing inbound customer questions.

CSR Bridge handshake script (copy-paste during bridge):
"Hey CSR team — operator here. We have a [P0|P1|P2] impacting [region / hosted / dedicated / model-serving]. Current hypothesis: [latency / errors / quality / capacity]. Evidence snapshot attached. Initial mitigation: [scaled up pool / routed traffic / enabled fallback model / isolated nodes]. Estimated first stabilization check: T+20 minutes. Recommended customer message: 'We are investigating degraded performance affecting inference in REGION. We will update in 15 minutes.' Assigning CSR owner: @Name. Any immediate customer escalation requests?"

3) Evidence Snapshot format (copyable checklist)
- Incident ID: INC-YYYYMMDD-<short>
- Time range observed: UTC timestamps
- Primary symptom: high 5xx rate / p95 latency climb / model-quality mismatch / etc.
- Service(s) affected: api-gateway, route-engine, runtime-pool-{region}, model-store
- Top three error traces (trace_id list, sample stack/message)
- Recent deploys (last 60 minutes) and rollback flag
- Autoscale events (scale up/down timestamps)
- Affected tenants (derived from routing logs) — list up to 10
- Customer tickets opened (IDs)
- Temporary mitigations applied and who performed them (operator name + time)
- Link to runbook(s) consulted

4) Triage decision ladder (short rules)
- If > 10% global request failure OR control plane unresponsive -> P0 and immediate IC.
- If single-region hit with automatic fallback available -> attempt fallback, notify CSR bridge, escalate only if fallback fails.
- If model-quality regression (LLM hallucinations or top-k shift) -> sample outputs, open model-quality ticket, engage Applied ML and Product.
- If degradation tied to recent deploy -> mark deploy as suspect and initiate rollback if rollback window open.

6) On-call rotation continuity rules (prevent single-point handoffs)
- Warm handoff required for all incidents longer than 1 hour: outgoing on-call must provide a 5-minute verbal summary and the Evidence Snapshot.
- Cold handoff only allowed when incident < 30 minutes and no customer escalations.
- Shadowing windows: new oncall must shadow for two weeks with a mentor.
- Handoff checklist (must be posted in channel before sign-off): incident ID, symptom, actions taken, next immediate steps, CSR owner, escalation contacts.

7) Game day and simulation template (scripting a 90-minute lab)
Objective: validate first-contact flow and CSR bridge within SLA.
Preconditions:
- Synthetic traffic generator configured to hit model variant A/B
- Test tenants: test-tenant-1..5 with simulated alerts enabled
- Observability toggles: inject p50/p95 latency anomalies and 5xx spikes

Scenario script (90 minutes):
  0–10m: alert fires, on-call acknowledges and posts Evidence Snapshot
  10–25m: operator attempts fallback & scales pools; CSR Bridge handshake executed
  25–40m: fallback partially restores; simulate a deploy that increases errors -> operator must detect and rollback
  40–60m: IC declared, cross-team bridge called, customer status update posted
  60–90m: restore and closure; postmortem quick-rubric completed within 48 hours

8) Postmortem quick-rubric (48-hour initial doc, full postmortem within 7 days)
Minimum fields for the 48-hour quick doc:
- Executive summary (1–2 sentences)
- Impact (customers affected, duration, SLO breaches)
- Root cause hypothesis (short)
- Immediate remediation performed
- Next steps / action owners (owner + due date)
Full postmortem must include timeline with evidence, change history, RCA analysis, and actions with measurable success criteria.

9) Status page & customer comm templates
Template: initial 15-minute status (public)
Title: 2026-02-14 — Degraded inference performance in REGION
Body: We are investigating degraded inference performance for customers in REGION. Our engineers have identified an issue affecting request latency for some workloads and have implemented temporary mitigations. We will provide an update within 15 minutes.

Template: internal CSR one-to-one outreach (when customers request direct contact)
Subject: Immediate update: temporary mitigation applied for your traffic
Body: Hi <Customer Name>,
We’re actively investigating elevated latency affecting inference requests for your account (observed T+...). Our engineers have routed traffic to a fallback pool and are monitoring. You may see reduced throughput for complex requests while we stabilize. We will reach out with a follow-up within 1 hour. If you observe errors, please reply with example request IDs.

Template: substantive post-incident customer update (24–72 hours)
Subject: Incident summary and next steps — [INC ID]
Body: Hi <Customer Name>,
Summary: On <date> between <start> and <end> UTC, you may have experienced increased latency/errors for inference requests routed to REGION. Root cause: [brief]. What we did: [actions taken]. What we will do to prevent recurrence: [list of engineering actions + ETA].

10) Evidence retention and regulatory notes
- Save snapshots, traces, and deployment artifacts to the incident evidence bucket under path s3://inc-evidence/YYYY/MM/DD/INC-<id>. Only attach sanitized customer-identifying data.
- For enterprise customers requiring audit evidence, note the evidence ID in the postmortem and copy legal/compliance.

11) Ownership and SLAs for this flowbook
- Document owner: eng-sre (oncall-runbooks owner).
- Review cadence: bi-annual and after every P0.
- Training: new SREs and CS leads must complete the First-Contact Flow lab during onboarding.
