Commitment Lifecycle, Credits, and Sunsetting Policy

Summary

This policy defines the lifecycle for customer commitments (reserved/dedicated capacity, subscription term commitments), the conditions under which credits or refunds are issued, and the operational and accounting steps for sunsetting commitments. It aligns procurement/contracting, billing, and revenue recognition (RevRec) to reduce operational risk and ensure consistent customer communication.

Scope

- Applies to all customer commercial commitments that include prepaid or committed spend, reserved capacity (Redwood Dedicated), and annual subscription commitments that carry usage or capacity guarantees.
- Excludes ad-hoc pay-as-you-go (hosted API per-token) purchases, except where those are bundled in commercial commitments.

Definitions

- Commitment: A contractual customer agreement to commit to a monetary or capacity amount over a defined term (months/years).
- Sunsetting: The controlled termination/phase-out of a product offering or commitment term prior to the contractual end date, either initiated by Redwood or the customer per contract terms.
- Credit: Billing credit applied to a customer account, either as an invoice-level adjustment or account-level credit balance.
- Refund: Cash return to customer, used only in limited circumstances defined in the Approval Matrix.

Policy Principles

1) Minimize retroactive RevRec adjustments: whenever possible, prefer prospective billing credits over retroactive revenue write-offs.
2) Preserve predictable financial reporting: all expected refund/credit exposure must be identified during contracting and added to the finance provisioning model.
3) Customer-first communications: notify impacted customers with 30/60/90 day windows depending on sunset severity and contractual obligations.

Commitment Change Types and Treatment

A. Voluntary Customer Reduction (Customer-initiated decrease mid-term)
- If contract allows reductions with penalty: apply contractual penalty crediting rules. Accounting: defer recognition of penalty revenue until collectability is confirmed; apply breakage calculations if applicable.
- If contract requires notice (e.g., 60 days) and pro-rates remaining term: compute unused committed portion and issue credit per standard pro-rata schedule.

B. Company-Initiated Sunsetting (Redwood retires or materially changes an offering)
- Notification: 90-day minimum notice for Dedicated or Private product sunsetting; 30-day minimum for non-core hosted features unless urgent security or regulatory reason.
- Options presented to customer: migration to an equivalent offering, one-time credit, or pro-rata refund (rare).
- Accounting: estimate migration incentives as contra-revenue or as expense based on legal classification; any refunds must be recorded and reverse previously recognized revenue according to RevRec policy.

C. Operational Failure (capacity shortfall or SLA-driven credits)
- Apply the SLA credit schedule (see SLA Credits table below). Billing team applies invoice-level credits; Finance posts RevRec adjustments when credits reduce recognized revenue materially (>5% of monthly revenue for the affected customer).

SLA Credits (summary)

| Metric | Threshold | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly uptime < SLA target | Varies by tier | 5%–50% of monthly committed fees via invoice credit |
| Sustained throughput below committed baseline | >72 hours | Tiered credit schedule, per contract |

Operational Process: Commitments Change Workflow

1) Request initiation: Account or Solutions submits Commitment Change Request (CCR) in Salesforce using record type: Commitment Change. Include contract ID, amendment terms, and customer acceptance evidence.
2) Contract review: Legal tags amendment and confirms whether amendments trigger RevRec remeasurement.
3) Finance review: Finance calculates expected credit/refund, revrec impact, and necessary provisioning. Use template spreadsheet: Commitment_Change_Template.xlsx.
4) Pricing & Sales approval: Approvers per Approval Matrix sign off.
5) Billing action: Billing issues credit memo or refund. If refund, Treasurer initiates ACH/Wire following refund SOP.

Approval Matrix (high level)

- Up to $50k impact or 10% of customer ARR: Sales Director + Finance Business Partner
- $50k–$250k impact or 10%–30% of ARR: Revenue Finance Manager + Legal Counsel
- >$250k or >30% of ARR or product sunset: VP Finance + GC + CRO approval

Accounting and RevRec Guidance

- Recognition remeasurement: For any change to contracted performance obligations or consideration, perform a contract remeasurement under ASC 606 / IFRS 15 guidance. Document the judgment and retain updated revenue schedules.
- Pro-rata credits: When issuing a pro-rata credit for unused committed service, reduce deferred revenue and recognize any remaining performance obligations per normal schedule.
- Refunds: Refund amounts reverse cash and, if revenue was already recognized in prior periods, trigger a prior-period adjustment under materiality thresholds. Consult VP Finance for material adjustments (>1% company revenue or >$500k).
- Migration incentives and one-time credits: classify as contra-revenue unless the incentive is a separate performance obligation (e.g., migration services).

Example accounting flow (numeric)

- Customer A committed $120,000 for a 12-month Dedicated pool (monthly billing = $10,000). After month 6, Redwood retires the Offering and grants a 3-month credit ($30,000) and migration assistance valued at $20,000.
  - Original deferred revenue remaining: $60,000.
  - Apply $30,000 credit: reduce deferred revenue by $30,000; recognize migration assistance classification: if contra-revenue vs. expense impacts P&L classification.
  - Any cash refund would reduce cash and reverse revenue previously recognized.

Customer Communication Templates

- 90/60/30 day sunset notification (short):
  Subject: Important: Changes to your Redwood Commitment for [Offering]
  Body: Hello [Name], we are reaching out to let you know that we will retire [Offering] on [date]. Options available: 1) migrate to [new offering], 2) receive a one-time credit, or 3) request a pro-rata refund. Your account team is available to review next steps.

- Credit memo messaging (on invoice):
  "Redwood Credit – Contract Amendment / Product Migration: see support ticket #[ticket_id] for details."

Monitoring and Financial Controls

- Monthly exception report: Finance generates a monthly "Commitment Adjustment Exposure" report that lists active commitments with pending amendments, estimated credit exposure, and required approvals.
- Provisioning rule: any single-customer exposure >$100k or aggregate monthly expected credits >$250k require provision hold and CFO notification.
- Audit trail: all CCRs, approvals, and customer acceptance evidence must be attached to the contract record in Salesforce and mirrored to DocStore for retention.

Escalation and Dispute Resolution

1) Billing disputes raised by customer: Billing team triages within 3 business days and provides initial response.
2) If the dispute is contractual or requires legal interpretation, legal responds within 7 business days.
3) Finance provides final calculation and remittance plan within 10 business days of dispute resolution.

Operational Examples and Edge Cases

- Customer requests refund but contract specifies credit: Default to contract; provide exception approval pathway requiring VP Finance + GC approval for cash refunds.
- Customer migrates but declines migration assistance: credit is applied as invoice credit and not reclassified as expense.
- Product sunset with security/regulatory imperative: Emergency sunset may reduce notice period; Finance must estimate exposure and notify external auditors if material.

Responsibilities and Ownership

- Sales/Account: initiate CCR and maintain customer communications.
- Legal: review contract amendments, confirm permissibility of credits/refunds.
- Billing: issue credits/refunds and track account balances.
- Finance (Revenue Ops): compute RevRec impacts, update deferred revenue schedules, and produce provisioning recommendations.
- Treasury: process refunds per payment SOP.

Appendix A — Quick Decision Checklist

- Is the change permitted by contract? 
  - Yes: proceed to finance calc and approval.
  - No: escalate to Legal for amendment.
- Is the estimated exposure >$250k or >30% of customer ARR? 
  - Yes: VP Finance & GC sign-off required.
- Is the change a product sunset? 
  - Yes: notify Customer Success and Product; prepare migration plan.
