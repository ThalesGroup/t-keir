hiring-for-impact-competency-sprints-and-100-day-outcomes-2029

Overview

Purpose: This playbook is designed for hiring managers, interviewers, and people-ops partners who want a structured, repeatable path from role intake to first-100-day measurable outcomes. It formalizes short competency sprints during hiring and prescribes a manager-led feedback cadence to get new hires to independent impact faster while maintaining equitable assessment.

Scope: Applies to all individual contributor and manager-level hires at Redwood Inference. Exclusions: executive hiring (handled by Talent & Exec Recruiting) and very short-term contractors (<30 days).

Key definitions
- Competency Sprint: A two-week focused evaluation and onboarding sub-phase that defines 2–3 observable competencies to validate in hiring and to train during ramp.
- 100-Day Outcomes: A small set (3–5) of measurable outcomes a manager and new hire agree on during onboarding.

Roles & Ownership
- Hiring Manager: defines role-level outcomes, selects competency sprint goals, owns interview panel and final hire decision.
- People Ops Partner: validates hiring rubric equity, ensures diverse panels, schedules competency sprint tasks, owns offer & coordination.
- Interviewers: evaluate against rubric and provide calibrated written feedback within 48 hours.
- New Hire Buddy: peer from a neighboring team who provides day-to-day orientation for first 4 weeks.

Hiring intake -> Competency Sprint (3-step flow)
1) Intake & Outcome Draft (Manager pre-reqs)
   - Manager completes the Role Readiness brief (template below) in Greenhouse before the JD is posted. Required fields: primary 100-day outcomes (3), top 3 competencies for sprint, minimal technical screen criteria, salary band, escalation contacts.
   - People Ops validates banding and reviewer panel diversity within 48 hours.

2) Competency Sprint design (People Ops + Manager, 48–72 hours)
   - For IC roles, sprints are typically engineering tasks or product problem statements that can be completed asynchronously (4–12 hours of candidate work). For managers, sprints are a short case exercise and two structured interviews.
   - Each sprint includes: success rubric, timebox, data or artifact submission instructions, and reviewer rubric questions.

3) Candidate evaluation & calibration (Interviewers)
   - Interviewers score against the rubric (numeric 1–5) and add a short narrative. Scores and narratives must be in Greenhouse within 48 hours of stage completion.
   - Hiring manager runs a 30-minute calibration meeting with the panel within 72 hours of final interview. If calibration variance >1.0 on any competency, arrange a tie-break interview or sample work review.

Manager checklist: Pre-offer -> Day 1
- Pre-offer: confirm final 100-day outcomes, identify buddy, schedule first-week meetings, and confirm onboarding equipment and access with IT and Infra.
- Day 0 (Offer accepted): send Welcome note with first-week agenda and the Outcome Draft PDF.
- Day 1: 30-minute one-on-one to review the 100-day outcomes and competency sprint expectations.

100-Day outcome template (example)
- Outcome 1 (Product/Feature impact): Deliver a validated improvement to the inference logging pipeline that reduces mean tail latency for 95th percentile tail by 5% for synthetic benchmark workload. Acceptance criteria: PR merged to main, benchmark results in CI show reduction, and post-deploy monitoring shows no regression for 7 days.
- Outcome 2 (Operational mastery): Complete the Redwood infra runbook certification with two successful runbook drills signed off by oncall lead.
- Outcome 3 (Team & process): Lead a cross-team retrospective and produce an action list with owners and SLAs.

Performance & feedback cadence during first 100 days
- Weekly 30m 1:1s during first 8 weeks, with explicit agenda items: blockers, outcomes progress, learning needs.
- Bi-weekly written scorecard from manager (short rubric: Learning, Delivery, Collaboration) at weeks 4, 8, and 12. Each scorecard is shared with new hire and People Ops.
- Formal 100-day review (week 12): manager prepares a 2-slide summary of outcomes, evidence, and recommended ongoing development plan. People Ops uses this artifact for calibration and banding adjustments if needed.

Calibration and fairness checks
- People Ops will run a calibration pool for hires at the same band every quarter. Metrics monitored: offer acceptance rate, time-to-ramp (measured by % of 100-day outcomes achieved), and variance in interview scores across demographics.
- If a candidate’s competency sprint is scored with high reviewer variance or conflicting narratives, People Ops will trigger a post-hire audit and provide an independent assessor to reconcile scores.

Example competency sprint rubric (table rendered as text)
| Competency | Evidence | Success threshold | Evaluator notes |
|------------|----------|------------------:|------------------|
| Service design | Design doc + diagram | 3/5 or higher across all reviewers | Look for clear tradeoffs and rollout plan |
| Code quality | PR with tests | No critical issues, 2 reviewers signoff | Tests + CI passing |
| Cross-team comms | Presentation deck | Clear goals + next steps | Evidence of stakeholder mapping |

Manager decision tree: offer -> low confidence -> hire with ramp plan -> no hire
1) If panel consensus is strong (>=3 reviewers, median score >=4), proceed to offer.
2) If median score is 3 with low variance, manager may request a short follow-up assignment.
3) If panel is split (variance >1.0) and concerns are about fit, consider: a) a second look interview led by a neutral reviewer, or b) decline and record learnings in the hiring postmortem.

Onboarding accelerators (practical tools)
- Competency Sprint Library: curated tasks for common roles (engineering, infra, data infra, product ops) maintained in the People Ops repo: git@redwood-internal:people-ops/competency-sprints.git.
- Role Onboarding Checklist (automated): HR triggers account provisioning 7 days before start. SLA: 95% of hires have accounts provisioned by Day 0.
- Runbook certification: a short multiple-choice + live drill validated by SRE.

Escalation & remediation
- If a new hire is not meeting agreed 100-day outcomes at week 8, manager opens a 4-week Remediation Sprint: documented plan, weekly checkpoints, and People Ops involvement. Outcome options: success (stay), extended ramp (up to 8 additional weeks), or transition support (role change or exit).
- People Ops must be notified before any formal performance step (e.g., PIP) is initiated.

Metrics & KPIs (tracked quarterly)
- Median time-to-first-impact: target <= 75 days for IC hires.
- 100-day outcomes achieved: target 80% across organization.
- Offer acceptance rate: target >= 65% for priority roles.
- Onboarding provisioning SLA: 95% Day-0-ready.

Templates & artifacts (appendix)
1) Role Readiness brief (fields): Role summary; 3x 100-day outcomes; competency sprint items; interview panel; salary band; urgency level (1–3).
2) Example 100-day review slide (two bullets per slide): Evidence, Result, Next steps.
3) Competency sprint email template for candidates (includes timebox and submission link).

Implementation notes for managers
- Keep outcomes measurable and narrow. Avoid more than three 100-day outcomes.
- Use competency sprints to reduce interviewing noise — they standardize evidence across candidates.
- Document learning opportunities publicly in the team wiki so future hiring loops improve.
