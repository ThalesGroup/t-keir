Redwood Starter and Career Ops Compendium

Summary

This compendium is a single-page reference for new and existing Redweod contributors that ties together our approach to working at Redwood, role definitions and career ladders, benefits highlights, hiring and onboarding checkpoints, and the internal tools and access flows you will use day-to-day. It is intended to be concise, practical, and actionable — a complement to the deeper linked pages noted in Related pages.

Purpose

- Provide a unified "starter pack" for hires and managers that reduces time-to-impact.
- Map role expectations to practical first-90 actions.
- Centralize the most-used internal tools and the people-ops processes for access, privileges, and cost tagging.

Who should read this

- New hires (all levels) in engineering, product, research, GTM, and support.
- Hiring managers and people-ops partners.
- Platform and security engineers who approve access requests.

Quick start checklist (new hire)

1) Before day 1
   - Confirm offer acceptance in BambooHR and complete benefits enrollment window tasks (see Benefits Quick Reference).
   - Get SSO invite (Okta) and enable MFA.
   - Upload ID for payroll/HR verification.
2) Day 1
   - Join #new-hires Slack channel, complete the 60-minute orientation video, and meet your onboarding buddy.
   - Confirm access to GitHub org, Confluence, Jira, and your team’s Slack channel.
3) Week 1
   - Setup local dev environment using the Quickstart repo; run the smoke test.
   - Meet primary stakeholders: manager, tech lead, people-ops partner.
   - Walk through cost-center tagging and ticket templates in the Console.
4) Month 1
   - Deliver your first "small win" (bugfix, doc update, simple eval).
   - Complete the role-specific 30-day checklist (linked below).
5) Month 3
   - 90-day review prep: compile a short outcomes report, updated goals, and feedback examples for your manager.

Role & career ladder (summary)

This section summarizes expectations across common ladders. For full competency rubrics see the linked role pages.

| Ladder | Typical titles | Core expectations (summary) | First-90 focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual Contributor — Engineering | Associate Engineer, Engineer I, II, Senior, Principal | Delivers features, owns reliability improvements, mentors peers, influences architecture at level-appropriate scope | Ship a feature, own a post-deploy fix, start mentoring a peer |
| Individual Contributor — Product | Associate PM, PM, Senior PM, Group PM | Defines clear requirements, drives experiments, measures outcomes, partners across engineering and GTM | Deliver a scoped spec, run an experiment, present findings |

Benefits quick reference (high level)

- Health & wellbeing: medical, dental, vision — standard Redwood employer contributions; enrollment within 30 days of start.
- Mental health: 8 free therapy sessions per year + employee assistance program (EAP).
- Family leave: parental leave and flexible return-to-work options.
- Remote/hybrid support: home office stipend, monthly coworking allowance.
- Professional development: learning allowance, conference budget, and internal mentorship stipends.
- Financial: 401(k) matching, equity grants, commuter benefit where applicable.

For full plan documents, carriers, and eligibility rules see the Benefits binder (HR link).

Hiring & onboarding overview (process & touchpoints)

1) Requisition to offer (hiring manager responsibilities)
   - Open requisition with people-ops using the standard job template and desired skill matrix.
   - Confirm scorecard with interview loop and rubric (technical, collaboration, growth mindset).
   - Work with recruiting to produce candidate slate and synchronized interview availability.
2) Offer and acceptance
   - People-ops generates offer letter; manager confirms start date and first-week plan; recruiter sets the candidate expectations for remote hardware, security, and documentation.
3) Pre-boarding (people-ops)
   - Initiate BambooHR onboarding tasks, send SSO invite, and assign an onboarding buddy from the team.
4) Day 0–30 (manager + buddy)
   - Ensure local environment bootstrapped, review 30-day goals, schedule cross-functional orientation meetings.
5) 60–90 day check and calibration
   - Conduct 60-day pulse, 90-day outcomes review, and either confirm ongoing role expectations or adjust a growth plan.

Access request flow (step-by-step)

1) Manager files an Access Request ticket (Jira) with: hire name, role, required systems, cost center, and justification.
2) People-ops approves identity & benefits tasks; Security reviews high-risk system requests (cloud infra, HSM, kube-admin).
3) Eng-platform or Security provisions access; engineer verifies ability to run the standard smoke test.
4) Manager confirms access in a 72-hour closure check.

First-90 plan templates (by role family)

- Engineering (IC): 30-day — local dev run, 1st small feature; 60-day — owned nontrivial bug or tiny feature; 90-day — contribute to design/ship medium-risk change.
- Engineering (Manager): 30-day — team syncs, review direct reports; 60-day — hiring priority validated; 90-day — performance calibration and plan.
- Product: 30-day — product context, 1 spec; 60-day — execute experiment; 90-day — measure and iterate.
- Applied ML: 30-day — reproduce baseline eval; 60-day — improve pipeline, 90-day — hand off model for serving.

Performance calibration & promotion rhythm (practical notes)

- Promotion cycles run quarterly. Managers collect evidence aligned to the competency rubrics. Evidence should include outcomes, technical artifacts, design reviews, mentorship examples, and customer/peer feedback.
- People-ops supports calibration panels and ensures consistency across ladders.

Manager checklist for growth-focused onboarding

- Assign an onboarding buddy and set clear 30/60/90 goals within first week.
- Schedule weekly 1:1s for the first 90 days; use the shared 1:1 template in Confluence.
- Plan for a public 30-day demo and a documented 90-day outcomes review.
