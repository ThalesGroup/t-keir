Entitlement Chronology Evidence & Retention Playbook

Summary:
This playbook defines how Redwood captures, preserves, and surface-proofs the full chronology of entitlement changes (grants, revocations, role-bindings, temporary activations) across our control plane, IAM systems, orchestration layers, and model-serving endpoints. The goal is to ensure tamper-resistant, queryable evidence suitable for internal audits, regulatory requests, incident investigations, and legal holds.

Scope:
- Systems in scope: Identity provider (IdP), internal authorization service (redwood-authz), service account management, cloud provider IAM (GCP/AWS/Azure), on-prem role store, and the runtime access delegation layer used by Private deployments.
- Evidence types: entitlement events, access decision logs, policy delta snapshots, approval artifacts (UI/CLI), human approval emails/threads, JIT activation records, and associated principal metadata (requestor, approver, justification).
- Excluded: transient process-local capabilities that do not cross process boundaries and are not persisted (unless explicitly promoted to long-lived entitlements).

Key principles:
1) Chronology preservation: store a complete, ordered timeline of entitlement operations with monotonic timestamps and cryptographic checksums for tamper-evidence.
2) Contextual linkage: link entitlement events to related audit events (API calls, session IDs, container task IDs) and to the exact code/model version when relevant.
3) Minimal-retention vs. evidentiary-retention: apply different retention windows per risk class and regulatory need, with automated retention policies applied in the audit store.
4) Fast-query readiness: evidence must be queryable within 1 minute for operational triage and within 24 hours for legal/forensic exports.

Ownership and roles:
- Security (owner): maintains the Playbook, evidence schema, retention matrix, and onboarding of new data sources. Primary contact: security-owner@redwood.ai.
- Infra IAM team: responsible for instrumenting entitlement operations and ensuring consistent event formats.
- Eng-SRE: maintains the audit event pipeline (collection, durability, indexing) and SLOs.
- Legal/Compliance: provides retention requirements for legal holds and regulatory evidence.
- Customer Success / Enterprise Security (when customers request tenant-specific evidence): coordinates restricted exports.

Evidence model (schema highlights):
- event_id: UUID
- timestamp_utc: ISO8601
- actor: principal id (user/service)
- action: grant | revoke | modify | approve | activate | deactivate
- target: resource id / role id / policy id
- scope: tenant | org | project | service
- reason: free-text justification or approval ticket id
- source: system-of-record (idp | redwood-authz | cloud-iam | private-agent)
- correlation_id: trace/session id to map to other audit logs
- before_snapshot: compact role/member set (optional)
- after_snapshot: compact role/member set (optional)
- integrity_hash: sha256(event_payload || storage_version_tag)

Note: The full schema is stored in the security schema registry; the above is the evidence-facing minimum.

Retention matrix (summary):
| Evidence class | Default retention | Regulatory hold | Rationale |
|----------------|------------------:|----------------:|-----------|
| High-risk entitlement changes (privileged roles, cross-tenant grants) | 7 years | indefinite | Required for long-tail investigations and regulatory requirements |
| Standard entitlements (non-privileged role changes) | 2 years | 7 years | Business continuity + auditability |
| JIT activations / temporary escalations | 1 year | 7 years | Shorter default window but may be extended when linked to incidents |
| Approval artifacts (tickets, signed approvals) | 7 years | indefinite | Legal evidentiary value |
| Correlation logs (session traces) | 90 days | 7 years | High volume; retained longer when associated with investigations |

Operational SLOs and thresholds:
- Ingestion durability: >= 99.99% of entitlement events acked to durable store within 30s.
- Query latency: 95th percentile entitlement-history queries < 1.5s for 30-day time windows.
- Export readiness: evidence export bundle generation for legal/incident requests within 24 hours.
- Tamper-detection: integrity failure alerts routed to security-oncall within 5 minutes.

Evidence collection and enrichment pipeline (high level):
1) Emit: instrument all entitlement operations to synchronous event emitter (SDK or webhook) with correlation_id.
2) Capture: collector agents write to a write-ahead log (WAL) in cloud object storage; small events buffered up to 5s.
3) Normalize: transformation workers enrich events with principal metadata, geolocation, tenant labels, and code/deployment tags.
4) Store: persist normalized events to immutable append-only store (ColdStore) and an indexed query store (HotIndex).
5) Archive & hash: periodic archival snapshots are hashed and notarized to the integrity ledger.
6) Audit UI / Exports: RBAC-protected UI and CLI tools query HotIndex for triage and ColdStore for full evidentiary export.

Step-by-step incident preservation checklist (for oncall use):
1) Identify scope: capture impacted principal IDs, resource IDs, and time windows.
2) Create legal hold: notify Legal/Compliance and create a hold record in the retention manager (include reason and expected duration).
3) Fast-freeze: copy WAL segments for the time window to an immutable bucket labeled with incident id. Command example:
   aws s3 cp s3://audit-wal/wal-2025-11-02-*.log s3://incident-freeze/INC-12345/ --storage-class=GLACIER_IR --acl=private
4) Snapshot index: create an export snapshot from HotIndex for the time window (include full correlation_ids).
5) Compute integrity: run integrity hashing job and store result in the ledger.
6) Notify: send an incident preservation summary to security-oncall, legal, and the assigned investigator.

Preservation CLI examples:
- Create a hold: redwood-retention create-hold --id INC-12345 --reason "suspected privilege misuse" --owner security@redwood.ai --expires 2026-05-01
- Export events: redwood-audit export --from 2025-11-02T00:00:00Z --to 2025-11-03T00:00:00Z --filter "actor:user-123 OR target:role-admin" --out /tmp/INC-12345-events.jsonl

Forensic correlation queries (examples):
- Find changes to a role in 24h window:
  SELECT * FROM entitlement_events WHERE target = 'role-admin' AND timestamp_utc BETWEEN '2025-11-02T00:00:00Z' AND '2025-11-03T00:00:00Z' ORDER BY timestamp_utc ASC;
- Map entitlement change to API calls by correlation id:
  SELECT e.*, a.* FROM entitlement_events e JOIN api_audit a ON e.correlation_id = a.correlation_id WHERE e.correlation_id = 'c-abcdef';

RBAC and scoped access to evidence:
- Principle of least privilege: access to HotIndex / ColdStore must be role-scoped and audited.
- Segregation: only security-privileged roles may perform integrity ledger notarization or legal exports.
- Tenant separation: tenant-specific evidence exports require a customer security request and an export justification.

Approval and exception handling (risk and exceptions):
1) Non-standard retention requests (shorter/longer than default) must be submitted via the control exception workflow and include: justification, proposed retention, compensating controls, and reviewer from Legal.
2) Temporary exception example: a customer requests a 30-day retention for correlation logs due to cost concerns. Control exception form must be filed and reviewed by Security and Legal; decision recorded in the entitlement chronology ledger.
3) Emergency exception: Security-Director may issue a 72-hour emergency exception to remove a hold if signed off by Legal and the CTO. The action must be recorded and propagated to the ledger.

Auditability and test procedures:
- Quarterly smoke test: randomly sample 50 entitlement events across systems and validate: (a) event schema completeness, (b) integrity_hash correctness, (c) ability to reconstruct before/after snapshots.
- Annual full audit: Security and Compliance perform a 3rd-party validated audit of the evidence pipeline and retention enforcement.
- Chaos test: during game days, simulate a high-volume entitlement storm and validate ingestion durability and query latencies.

Export formats and packaging:
- Standard export bundle: JSONL events + integrity manifest (sha256 per file) + export metadata.json (requestor, reason, issued_at, ledger_hash).
- Legal package: additional notarized archive (signed by integrity key, timestamped) and chain-of-custody form.

Appendix B: Common failure modes and mitigations:
- Missing correlation_id: reject ingestion and route event to quarantine for manual enrichment.
- Integrity ledger mismatch: trigger immediate alert, isolate ledger node, and start forensic snapshot.
- HotIndex lag: reroute queries to ColdStore snapshot while SRE remedies the indexing backlog.
