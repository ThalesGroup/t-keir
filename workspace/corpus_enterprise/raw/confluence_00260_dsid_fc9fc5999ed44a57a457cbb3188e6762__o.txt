Onboarding Telemetry Entropy and Stability Guardrails

Overview

Purpose: This playbook defines the telemetry contract, operational guardrails, and runbook steps to keep tenant onboarding stable while minimizing telemetry entropy (noise, sampling drift, duplicates, and cost spikes). It targets enterprise and Dedicated/Private tenants during the first 90 days post-provisioning and during major cutovers.

Audience: Customer Success, Solutions Engineering, SRE, Platform Integrations, and Customer engineering contacts.

Goals
- Enforce a predictable telemetry footprint for enterprise onboarding.
- Detect and contain telemetry entropy before it impacts SLOs or billing.
- Provide clear stop/gate conditions for activation and cutover.
- Ensure actionable signals are routed to the right teams with remediation playbooks.

Scope
- Applies to: hosted, dedicated, and private deployments being onboarded to Redwood (all tiers).
- Excludes: low-volume proof-of-concepts where telemetry contract is explicitly waived in the onboarding checklist.

Definitions
- Telemetry entropy: unexpected increases in telemetry volume, schema drift, duplicate events, or a high rate of low-value telemetry that degrades visibility or increases cost.
- Activation gate: a named, testable condition that must be met before full traffic is routed to Redwood.
- Sampling contract: per-route and per-tenant sampling percentages and retention policies agreed at onboarding.

Ownership and Roles
- Customer Success (CS): owns activation gates, customer comms, and vendor escalations.
- Solutions Engineering (SE): validates telemetry schema and integration during pre-provisioning.
- SRE / Platform: enforces platform-side rate limits, cost-protection, and alerting.
- Customer Engineering: implements SDK/ingress changes and triages application-side issues.

Telemetry Contract (minimum required artifacts)
1) Schema agreement (YAML/JSON): event names, required fields, PII classification, max payload sizes. Example:| event | required_fields | pii_level | max_size_bytes |
|------|----------------|-----------|----------------|
| request_start | request_id, tenant_id, model_id, route | none | 2048 |
| response_end | request_id, status_code, token_count, model_variant | none | 4096 |

2) Sampling and retention table (to be filled by SE/CS):
- Default ingress sampling: 10% for debug-level payloads, 100% for request/response metadata.
- Retention: telemetry metadata 30d (standard), detailed payloads 7d (default), redact PII immediately.

3) Cost budget & throttles: initial per-tenant telemetry budget (USD/day) and emergency cap triggers.
- Soft cap: baseline_budget * 1.5 -> automated notification to CS and tenant.
- Hard cap: baseline_budget * 2.5 -> automatic ingestion throttling to 25% + reduce payload retention.

Activation Gates (must be verified before full traffic)
- Gate A: Schema validated and test events received in production telemetry pipeline (pass if >= 10 representative events within 10 minutes).
- Gate B: Baseline traffic profile observed for 1h with telemetry volume within +/-25% of predicted.
- Gate C: Cost forecast aligned and soft cap configured in billing system.
- Gate D: Live KV cache hit-rate (if used) >= 60% on representative requests or fallback plan documented.

Stability Guardrails and SLOs
- Ingestion availability: 99.9% over rolling 30d.
- Telemetry pipeline lag: p95 <= 90s for metadata; p95 <= 5m for payloads.
- Alert fidelity: false positive rate < 10% for entropy alarms during steady state.
- Cost anomaly detection: detect >40% increase vs hourly baseline within 15 minutes.

Key Metrics to monitor (recommended PromQL / SQL examples)
- Telemetry events per minute per tenant: sum by (tenant_id) (increase_over_time(event_count[5m]))
- Duplicate event rate (heuristic): count by (tenant_id) (event_has_duplicate_id == 1) / count by (tenant_id) (event_count)
- Schema field drift: count by (field_name) (missing_field_flag) over last 1h
- Telemetry ingestion lag: histogram_quantile(0.95, sum(rate(ingest_latency_bucket[5m])) by (le))

Alerting thresholds (recommended)
- Entropy-warning (P1-Low): per-tenant telemetry volume > baseline * 1.5 for 10m -> Notify CS/SE.
- Entropy-critical (P1): per-tenant telemetry volume > baseline * 2.5 for 5m or sustained duplicate rate > 5% for 10m -> Auto-throttle + page SRE.
- Schema-drift (P2): missing required fields > 1% of requests in 1h -> SE validation task.
- Cost-forecast breach (P1): predicted daily spend > budget * 1.25 within 30m -> warn finance and CS.

Runbook: Telemetry Entropy Incident (High-level steps)
1) Detection & Triage (0–10m)
  a) Alert fires -> SRE on-call page.
  b) SRE runs 'telemetry-quick-check' (CLI snippet below) and opens incident channel.
2) Containment (10–30m)
  a) Apply ingress-side soft cap for tenant: limit sample rate to 10%.
  b) Engage CS to notify customer of degraded visibility and temporary caps.
  c) If cost hard cap reached -> apply hard throttle to 25% and reduce retention.
3) Diagnostic (30–90m)
  a) Correlate deploys/rollouts (customer and Redwood) against start time.
  b) Query for schema anomalies, duplicate ids, and payload size spikes.
  c) If duplicates detected, enable dedupe window in ingress for 5m.
4) Remediation (90m–4h)
  a) Patch client SDK or ingest adapter to fix malformed events or reduce verbosity.
  b) Gradually lift caps once sustained metrics return to within 20% of baseline for 30m.
5) Postmortem and QBR action (within 7 days)
  a) Produce RCA with impact to SLOs and cost.
  b) Add onboarding checklist item and automated telemetry tests to prevent recurrence.

CLI and quick commands (examples)
- telemetry-quick-check (internal script):
  ./bin/telemetry-quick-check --tenant $TENANT_ID --window 10m --metrics "event_count,duplicate_rate,avg_payload_size"

- Query duplicates (example SQL):
  SELECT tenant_id, count(*) as events, count(distinct event_id) as distinct_ids, (1 - count(distinct event_id)::float / count(*)) as duplicate_rate
  FROM telemetry_events
  WHERE received_at > now() - interval '10 minutes'
  GROUP BY tenant_id
  ORDER BY duplicate_rate DESC

Containment knobs (what Platform can toggle)
- Ingress sampling overrides (per-tenant, per-route).
- Payload size caps and auto-truncate (with hashed tail).
- Dedupe window duration (in seconds).
- Retention policy short-circuit (7d -> 24h).
- Emergency hard cap throttle to 25% ingestion.

Customer comms templates (short forms)
- Initial notification (automated):
  Subject: Redwood telemetry alert for tenant {{tenant_name}}
  Body: We detected abnormal telemetry volume from your integration at {{time}}. To protect stability and limit costs we have temporarily reduced ingestion rate. Our team is investigating and will update within 30 minutes.

- Follow-up (after containment):
  Body: We have stabilized telemetry ingestion and reduced noisy events by applying a temporary sample and retention change. Next steps: coordinate a patch with your engineering team.

Onboarding checklist additions (must be validated and recorded in customer ticket)
- Telemetry schema document uploaded to onboarding ticket.
- Baseline traffic profile recorded (sample traces + 1h of representative traffic).
- Soft and hard telemetry caps configured and agreed by billing/compliance owners.
- Automated telemetry health test executed (pass criteria: ingestion <= 25s lag p95, no duplicates >0.5% for 1h).

QBR and metrics alignment (what to present)
- RCA summary of any entropy incidents during onboarding.
- Telemetry cost vs predicted and mitigation actions.
- Proposed permanent sampling/retention changes and business impact.
- Action items for product improvements (SDK dedupe, improved schema validation).

Examples and anti-patterns observed in previous onboardings
- Anti-pattern: Customer sends full request/response payloads at 100% sampling; result: 6x expected storage and pipeline lag spike. Remediation: set payload sampling to 10% and redact text fields.
- Anti-pattern: Client retries with non-idempotent request_ids -> creates duplicate events. Remediation: enforce UUID request_id and add simple idempotency check in SDK.

Appendix: Troubleshooting quick checklist
- If ingestion lag > 2x threshold: check downstream storage throughput and apply retention short-circuit.
- If duplicate_rate > 5% and source deploys not found: validate SDK patch level and enable dedupe window.
- If cost forecast spikes but volume stable: inspect payload sizes and remove unnecessary attachments.
