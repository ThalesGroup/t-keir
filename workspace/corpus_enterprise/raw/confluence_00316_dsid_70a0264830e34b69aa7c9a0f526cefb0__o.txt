On-call Shadow Labs and Continuity Manifest

Summary

This document defines the On-call Shadow Lab program and the accompanying Continuity Manifest used to validate responder readiness, handoff fidelity, and incident continuity for Redwood Inference. The Shadow Lab program runs scheduled, low-risk experiments (synthetic signals and twilight incidents) against staging and canary surfaces to exercise runbooks, rotation handoffs, and customer communication paths.

Goals

- Verify that every on-call rotation has at least one active shadowing trainee per cycle.
- Prove runbooks, automation, and status-page tooling through repeatable synthetic-signal scenarios.
- Validate handoff fidelity and evidence capture for postmortems and regulatory audits.
- Reduce time-to-stabilize for tiers 0–2 incidents by 30% across the next two quarters.

Program overview

1) Shadow Labs (weekly): lightweight, scheduled exercises where an experienced responder (mentor) pairs with a trainee (shadow) and performs a simulated detection and triage using non-production signals. Exercises focus on one play at a time (e.g., KV cache miss storm, batching-pressure, N+1 latency).
2) Synthetic Signal Days (monthly): automated scenarios injected against isolated canaries that generate realistic observability signals (SLO deviation, error spikes, missing traces). These are run under a test-ops guard and always target staging/canary routes.
3) Continuity Manifest (quarterly): a checklist-driven audit verifying runbooks, rotation contact lists, backup responders, and status page templates are current.

Roles and responsibilities

- Lead Responder (mentor): prepares scenario, reviews evidence, signs off on trainee readiness. Ownership: eng-sre.
- Shadow Trainee: performs triage and executes runbook steps under mentor guidance. Ownership: rotating engineers/ML engineers.
- Continuity Owner: owns the quarterly Manifest and remediation tracking. Ownership: platform-ops.
- Status Page Operator: maintains status page templates and triggers customer messages if scenario crosses customer-impact threshold. Ownership: customer-success on-call.

On-call shadow lab schedule (recommended cadence)

- Weekly shadow session (1 hour): 1 mentor + 1 trainee. Focus: single runbook execution, handoff practice.
- Monthly synthetic scenario (2 hours): test automation team runs scripts; on-call participates.
- Quarterly continuity audit (half day): review manifest items, rotate backup responders, update runbooks.

Runbook template (single-file canonical pattern)

Every runbook used in Shadow Labs should follow the template below. This ensures consistent evidence collection and easy automation integration.

- Runbook title: short, unique
- Service / component: service name(s) and route(s) affected
- Severity tiers and customer impact mapping
- Detection signals: metrics, logs, traces to check (include dashboard urls)
- Initial triage checklist (numbered):
  1. Confirm alert validity and time window.
  2. Identify impacted routes and customers via request tags.
  3. Check autoscaling and queue length metrics.
  4. If model-related, check kv-cache size and recent model-rollout history.
- Mitigation steps (ordered):
  1. Apply fast rollback to previous model variant (if applicable).
  2. Increase worker pool or scale burst group. CLI:
     curl -X POST "https://internal-api.redwood/v1/ops/scale?pool=ingest&delta=+2" \n  3. Enable prefix-KV bypass (short-lived) and monitor error rate.
- Verification steps: smoke tests and SLO checks (include target metrics).
- Evidence capture checklist: screenshots of dashboards, links to logs (packet id, trace id), pager ids, bridge transcript.
- Post-incident next-steps and owners.

Incident handoff and continuity rules (manifest items)

The Continuity Manifest is a simple checklist to be completed at handoff and during audits. Key items:

- Rotation roster verified (primary + backup) with phone and escalation order.
- Runbooks validated for current top-10 incident classes.
- Status page templates checked and test-sent to internal channel (no external publish).
- Backup responder health: confirm 2 responders in different regions with VPN access.
- Automation playbooks functional (CI smoke for runbook runner).

Handoff procedure (5-step sequence)

1. Outgoing responder posts concise status: current state, mitigation in progress, >customer impact? Y/N, tasks outstanding.
2. Incoming responder reviews runbook & evidence links, acknowledges within 5 minutes.
3. Confirm pager/bridge access and test a single quick command (e.g., get cluster health).
4. Update rotation calendar and mark handoff complete in the oncall ledger.
5. If escalations expected, notify Customer Success and Legal (if SLA breach likely).

Status page and customer comms templates

Use these concise, staged templates tailored to the incident tier. Keep language factual and avoid conjecture.

- Short incident announcement (initial)
  Title: Service degradation for <service-name> in <region>
  Body: We are investigating elevated error rates impacting <subset-of-customers>. We have engaged the on-call team and will provide updates within 30 minutes.

- Update (staged)
  Title: Update: mitigation in progress for <service-name>
  Body: We are deploying mitigations and observing partial improvement. A portion of requests are still affected. Next update in 30 minutes or on progress.

- Resolution
  Title: Resolved: <service-name>
  Body: The issue has been resolved at <time>. Root cause: <short summary>. Impacted customers: <list>. Postmortem will be published in X business days.

Postmortem / evidence checklist

- Incident timeline with timestamps (UTC).
- Evidence: dashboard links, logs (trace ids), bridge transcript, customer messages.
- Root cause hypothesis and validation steps performed.
- Rollback/mitigation timeline and why chosen.
- Action items with owners and due dates (include verification plan).
- Regulatory evidence pack (if customer-impacting): signed by Continuity Owner.

Game day scoring and pass/fail criteria

- Completion of runbook steps within target windows (ack within 5m, mitigation attempted within 20m).
- Quality of evidence captured (screenshots, links) scored 0–5.
- Handoff fidelity (outgoing: 5 checks present) scored pass/fail.
- Customer comms template used correctly (language and cadence).
- Overall pass = 80%+ on combined criteria. Failures create a remediation ticket assigned to continuity owner.

Onboarding a new responder (quick checklist)

1. Shadow two weekly labs (observe and run one under mentor).
2. Pass a synthetic-signal scenario with independent execution and evidence capture.
3. Confirm access to bridge, status page, and runbook editor.
4. Sign the Continuity Manifest and provide emergency contact.
