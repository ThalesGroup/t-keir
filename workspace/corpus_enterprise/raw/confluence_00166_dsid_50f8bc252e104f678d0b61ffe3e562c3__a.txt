Access, Change, Data, and Procurement Operational Handbook

Purpose
-------
This handbook consolidates company-wide policies and standardized processes for access & permissions, change management, data management, procurement & expenses, vendor management, travel, and the canonical templates used across Redwood Inference. It is intended for managers, approvers, engineering leads, security, finance, and people-ops staff responsible for compliance and day-to-day operations.

Scope
-----
Applies to all Redwood employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with accounts on company systems or who perform work on behalf of Redwood. This document covers:
- Identity lifecycle (onboarding, role changes, offboarding)
- System and infrastructure change controls (standard and emergency)
- Data classification, handling, and retention
- Procurement, purchase orders, and expense reconciliation
- Vendor onboarding, due diligence, and SLA expectations
- Business travel approvals and allowable expenses
- Reusable templates for requests and approvals

Principles (applies to all sections)
--------------------------------
- Least privilege by default: grant the minimal access necessary and approve broader access only with documented justification.
- Separation of duties: no single individual should both request and permanently approve high-impact changes or vendor payments.
- Traceability: every access grant, change, and procurement action must be auditable with an owner and timestamped approval.
- Risk-based controls: higher sensitivity data and critical infrastructure require elevated review and approval paths.

Roles & Responsibilities
------------------------
- Requester: initiates access/change/procurement/travel request with justification and required attachments.
- Approver: business manager and/or owner of the resource; approves only requests within their authority.
- Security/Compliance: performs risk assessment for access to sensitive data and vendor security reviews.
- Finance/Procurement: validates budgets, issues POs, and processes vendor payments.
- IT/Platform: performs technical implementation for access and change requests; records implementation notes.
- People-Ops: ensures identity lifecycle aligns to employment status and schedules access reviews.

Access & Permissions
--------------------
Access categories:
1) Standard Access — tools for daily work (Slack, Email, Confluence). Granted by manager approval, auto-provisioned via Okta groups. SLA: 1 business day.
2) Elevated Access — production consoles, admin roles, database access. Requires manager approval + technical owner approval + security review. SLA: 3 business days.
3) Temporary/Emergency Access — timeboxed elevated access for incidents or critical work. Must be requested with explicit expiry and post-action audit.
4) Service Accounts — machine/service identities used by infrastructure. Managed via identity-service (idp-redwood) with lifecycle tags.

Access request process (standard)
1. Complete Access Request template (see Templates section). Include justification, data sensitivity level, and expected duration.
2. Manager approves in JIRA access-requests board.
3. Technical owner (resource owner) reviews and verifies minimal required scope.
4. Okta automation adds user to the relevant group; IT documents the change in the ticket.
5. Requester confirms access and closes ticket.

Access review and attestation
- Quarterly automated reviews for all admins and service accounts.
- Semi-annual attestation by resource owners for access to systems categorized as "sensitive" (see Data Classification).
- Revocation target: access flagged for removal should be revoked within 5 business days.

Change Management
-----------------
Change categories:
- Low-risk (configuration tweaks, documentation updates): owner approval + single approver. Target lead time: ad-hoc.
- Standard (non-breaking infra changes, deployment to staging): change request submitted, business owner approval, SRE review. Planned maintenance windows preferred.
- High-impact (schema migrations, production DB changes, network changes, model rollout with >10% customer impact): change advisory board (CAB) approval required and rollout plan + rollback plan + monitoring checklists. Notice period: 72 hours.
- Emergency: use Emergency Change procedure; must be declared by on-call SRE or engineering lead. Follow-up: retrospective within 48 hours and formal change record within 5 business days.

Standard change request workflow
1. Create a JIRA change-ticket using the Change Request template.
2. Attach runbook/rollout plan: steps, expected timeline, feature gates, metrics to watch, rollback steps.
3. Triage by platform on-call; categorize risk.
4. If required, schedule for CAB review; CAB meets Tue/Thu 09:00 PT for high-impact windows.
5. After approvals, schedule rollout in maintenance window and coordinate with SRE and support.
6. Post-deploy: run smoke tests, annotate incident metrics, and close change ticket with results.

Emergency change handler (minimum fields)
- Declaration by: name, role, time, and incident ID.
- Reason and risk summary.
- Actions taken and owners for each step.
- Post-change review owner and timeline.

Change metrics & thresholds
- Percentage of emergency changes (target < 5% of changes per quarter).
- Rollback rate (target < 1% of standard changes).
- Change-related incidents within 30 days (target: zero high-severity incidents).

Data Management & Classification
--------------------------------
Data sensitivity categories:
- Public: meant for public consumption, no protections required.
- Internal: company information not for public release but not legally protected. Basic access controls.
- Restricted: PII, financials, customer data (non-aggregated), model training datasets with customer content. Requires encryption at rest and in transit, limited access, and audit logging.
- Regulated: data subject to legal/regulatory controls (HIPAA, PCI, regional data residency). Requires additional contractual and technical controls; contact Security & Legal before processing.

Handling rules (restricted/regulatory)
- Storage: only approved storage classes in GCP/AWS with encryption and logging enabled.
- Transfer: use secure channels (TLS 1.2+) and SFTP/transfer appliances where required.
- Masking/Anonymization: remove or tokenize PII prior to use in development or eval environments.
- Backups: encrypted backups with retention per retention table below and periodic restore tests.

Retention & deletion
- Default: 3 years for internal operational logs; 7 years for financial and contract records unless otherwise stated.
- Personal data: retention driven by legal requirements and data subject requests.
- Deletion process: submit Deletion Request template, include scope, datasets, and retention justification. Implement and verify deletion with a checksum audit.

Data access controls & monitoring
- All access to Restricted/Regulated datasets must be logged and reviewed monthly for anomalies.
- Anomaly thresholds: more than 10 distinct data exports by one user in 7 days triggers an investigation.

Procurement & Expenses
----------------------
Procurement tiers and approval thresholds (USD):
- Tier 0 (<= $1,000): Requester + Manager approval. Credit card purchase allowed.
- Tier 1 ($1,001 - $25,000): Requester + Manager + Finance approval. PO required for vendors.
- Tier 2 ($25,001 - $250,000): Procurement + Legal + Finance + Exec Sponsor approval. Competitive bidding encouraged.
- Tier 3 (> $250,000): Board-level notification & multi-vendor RFP recommended.

Vendor onboarding checklist
1. Business justification + budget code.
2. Vendor security questionnaire (standard vendor-security-questionnaire.pdf).
3. Contract review: Legal must sign off.
4. PO issuance and finance coding.
5. Technical integration plan and data flow diagram if vendor handles company data.
6. SLA and monitoring expectations documented and owner assigned.

Expense policy highlights
- Airfare: economy for domestic; business class allowed for flights >8 hours with Exec approval.
- Lodging: reasonable single room rate in line with corporate travel band.
- Meals and incidentals: per diem or receipts (choose one per trip); private events require advance approval.
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable (e.g., family upgrades, leisure).
- Receipts required for all expenses > $25; card statements alone are insufficient.

Payment and reconciliation
- Net 30 standard for vendor invoices unless contract specifies otherwise.
- Finance performs monthly reconciliation of POs and expense reports. Discrepancies > $500 require escalation to the requester’s manager.

Vendor Management & SLAs
-----------------------
- Security due diligence: all vendors with access to Restricted data must complete security questionnaire and a pen-test summary where required.
- SLA minimums: uptime targets, response time objectives, and incident escalation paths. Critical vendors must provide four-hour incident response commitments and a 99.9% monthly uptime objective unless otherwise negotiated.
- Contract clauses required: data residency, breach notification (<=72 hours), liability limits, SLAs, and termination/exit clauses for data extraction.

Vendor review cadence
- Annual security review for vendors handling Restricted/Regulated data.
- Quarterly performance review for top 10 spend vendors.

Travel Policy (business travel)
-------------------------------
Pre-approval: all trips > $500 must be pre-approved in Concur (or approved travel tool) with estimated budget and duration.
Booking guidelines:
- Use corporate travel portal where available.
- Schedule meetings to maximize overlap and reduce duplicate trips.
Safety & emergency: travelers must enroll in Redwood travel safety program and share itinerary with People-Ops.
Domestic per diem example:
- Meals: $60/day
- Incidentals: $10/day
International per diem: varies by country; use Finance country band lookup.

Templates (canonical, copy/paste to JIRA/Confluence)
--------------------------------------------------
1) Access Request (short):
- requester: NAME
- role: requested-role
- system: e.g., "prod-db-redwood"
- justification: Business reason (link to ticket)
- duration: e.g., 7 days (for temporary)
- approvals required: Manager; technical owner; security if Restricted
2) Change Request (short):
- change-summary: brief description
- risk-category: Low/Standard/High
- owner: implementer name
- planned-start: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM TZ
- rollback-plan: steps to revert and verification
- monitoring: metrics to watch and thresholds
3) Vendor Onboarding:
- vendor-name, service-description, data-handled, contract-owner, PO-required(Y/N), security-questionnaire-complete(Y/N)
4) Travel Expense:
- traveler, dates, purpose, itinerary, total-amount, receipts-attached(Y/N), policy-exception(Y/N)

Appendix A — Retention table (examples)
- Contracts: 7 years
- Financial records: 7 years
- Access logs (standard): 90 days online, archived for 3 years
- Audit logs (sensitive infra): 1 year online, archived 7 years
