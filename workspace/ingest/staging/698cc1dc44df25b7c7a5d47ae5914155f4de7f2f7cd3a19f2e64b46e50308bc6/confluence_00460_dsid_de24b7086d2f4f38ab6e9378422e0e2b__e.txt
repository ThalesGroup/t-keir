Ephemeral Access Evidence Catalog & Decision Playbook

Summary
-------
This playbook defines how Redwood captures, catalogs, retains, and uses evidence for ephemeral access incidents: short-lived credentials, just-in-time (JIT) privileges, temporary session tokens, and ephemeral service accounts. The goal is to provide a repeatable decision lifecycle that preserves chain-of-custody, meets data residency constraints, integrates with audit logging, and supports regulatory evidence requests with clear SLAs.

Scope
-----
- Applies to ephemeral authentication/authorization mechanisms used across Redwood products and deployments (hosted, dedicated, private).
- Covers human and machine ephemeral sessions (SSH jump, temporary API keys, OIDC short-lived tokens, CI job tokens, ephemeral k8s service accounts).
- Includes interactions with RBAC systems, audit collectors, SSO providers, KMS/HSM, SIEM, and long-term evidence stores.
- Excludes permanent entitlement changes (managed in entitlement change runbook).

Definitions
-----------
- Ephemeral Access: any authentication or authorization grant with a TTL <= 24 hours.
- Evidence Artifact: any recorded artifact used to demonstrate actions or provenance (audit events, session recordings, token issuance records, TTL policies, KMS sign logs, hash manifests).
- Evidence Catalog: canonical index of artifacts for a given incident.
- Custodian: named security owner responsible for evidence integrity.

High-level Principles
---------------------
1. Immutable first: always prefer append-only artifacts (audit stream, object store with versioning).
2. Minimal scope: capture only the needed attributes to reduce PII exposure while preserving investigatory value.
3. Provenance: every artifact must include origin, timestamp, and a hash signed by a KMS key.
4. Data residency-aware: evidence placement must respect regional retention and residency requirements.
5. Time-to-evidence SLA: for P1 incidents, security must deliver an initial assembled evidence package within 4 hours.

Evidence Catalog (standard schema)
---------------------------------
The Evidence Catalog is stored as a single JSON manifest per incident in our evidence-bucket under path: evidence/{incident_id}/manifest.json. Minimal manifest fields: incident_id, custodian, region, artifacts[] (list). Each artifact entry must be flattened with string values only. Example manifest entry (conceptual):

- incident_id: "INC-2026-0421-07"
- custodian: "Ava Martinez"
- region: "eu-west-1"
- artifacts: ["audit:2026-02-09T11:12:33Z:authz-issue-log-202602091112.json.sha256:sha256:abcdef...", "session:2026-02-09T11:15:00Z:ssh-session-42.mp4.sha256:sha256:012345..."]

Artifact types and retention guidance
------------------------------------
1) Authorization Issuance Record (OIDC / STS / CI token):
   - What to capture: issuer, token_id, subject, scopes/claims, TTL, issuance_time, requester_ip, request_headers (only headers relevant to auth decision).
   - Storage: append to audit-stream; retain 365 days by default.
   - TTL tagging: ephemeral-tag:ttl_minutes (e.g. 15).

2) RBAC Entitlement Snapshot:
   - What to capture: flattened role bindings applied at token issuance, resource selectors, effective permissions, source change-id.
   - Storage: evidence-bucket, versioned; retain 540 days if tied to regulated customer data, otherwise 365.

3) Session Recording / Screen Capture (if enabled):
   - What to capture: start/end timestamps, participant identities, description of actions, associated resources.
   - Storage: encrypted object store with region-specific buckets (e.g., eu-west-1-video-store). Retain 90 days default; escalate to 3 years for legal holds.

4) KMS/HSM Audit of Key Use:
   - What to capture: key_id, operation (sign/verify), principal, timestamp, request_id.
   - Storage: central KMS audit log stream; proof-of-signature must be referenced via SHA256 in manifest. Retain per key-policy (minimum 365 days).

5) Network Forensics Snapshot:
   - What to capture: PCAP slice or VPC flow logs for the window of interest, requester and target endpoints, NAT mappings.
   - Storage: encrypted bucket; default retention 180 days. If data residency prohibits cross-border transfer, keep snapshots in-region and include a pointer in the manifest.

Decision Flow: When ephemeral access is flagged
--------------------------------------------
1. Detection: SIEM raises alert (e.g., anomalous JIT activation, token reuse, or elevation outside maintenance window). Alert is triaged by oncall.
2. Triage (15–30 minutes): determine severity, affected tenants, and region. Assign incident_id and custodian.
3. Evidence Capture Kickoff (within 30 minutes of triage):
   - Custodian runs automated capture job (see Runbook) to gather required artifacts and generate manifest.
   - CI token revocation or access freeze is triggered if immediate containment is needed.
4. Assembly (SLA: 4 hours P1, 24 hours P2): collate manifest and artifacts into evidence-bucket with signed hashes.
5. Legal Hold / Resident Retention: if escalation to legal is required, change the artifact retention tags and notify storage team to apply hold.
6. Resolution & Closure: produce a findings report and close incident when remediation and evidence handoff are complete.

Runbook: Evidence Capture for Ephemeral Access (Automated + Manual)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Prereqs: access to audit-query role (audit.read), access to evidence-bucket writer (evidence.write), key for signing (kms-evidence-sign).

Automated procedure (preferred):
1. Run collector script with incident_id and time-window:
   - CLI: ./tools/collect-ephemeral-evidence --incident INC-YYYY-NNN --start 2026-02-09T11:00:00Z --end 2026-02-09T12:00:00Z --region eu-west-1
2. Collector actions:
   - Query audit stream for issuance events matching subject/resource patterns. Example SIEM query:
     - index=auth_issuance source=oidc issuer=redwood-oidc earliest=2026-02-09T11:00:00Z latest=2026-02-09T12:00:00Z | table time,token_id,subject,client_ip,ttl
   - Snapshot RBAC state via roles API: curl -H "Authorization: Bearer ${AUDIT_TOKEN}" https://rbac-api.internal/v1/effective?subject={subject}
   - Request KMS sign of manifest: aws kms sign --key-id alias/kms-evidence-sign --message fileb://manifest.json --output text --query Signature | base64 --decode > manifest.sig
   - Upload artifacts and manifest to evidence-bucket: aws s3 cp --acl bucket-owner-full-control *.json s3://evidence/evidence/{incident_id}/
3. Collector outputs a flattened manifest referencing artifacts by: type:timestamp:filename:hash-algo:hash-value.

Manual augmentation (when automated capture not available):
1. Note exact timestamps of issuance and activities.
2. Pull raw logs from audit-stream console as text and save with a stable filename.
3. Compute SHA256 on each saved artifact and record in manifest.
4. Have custodian sign manifest with KMS (document the key-id and sign timestamp).

Integration with SIEM and Audit Logging
-------------------------------------
- Event schema requirements for ephemeral access events (flattened and key-only):
  - event_type, time_iso, principal_id, principal_type, action, resource, token_id, ttl_minutes, request_id, requester_ip, region, auth_provider, trace_id
  - Example minimal event (as a single-line JSON string in logs):
    {"event_type":"token_issue","time_iso":"2026-02-09T11:12:33Z","principal_id":"user:alice","action":"issue_token","resource":"/models/bert/serve","token_id":"tok-0a1b2c","ttl_minutes":"15","requester_ip":"203.0.113.7","region":"us-west-2","auth_provider":"okta","trace_id":"trace-abcdef"}
- SIEM-owned playbooks must map token_id -> audit indices and enrich with role snapshots.

Data Residency and Retention Controls
------------------------------------
- Evidence placement must follow the incident's affected-region. If incident touches EU-resident customer data, all artifacts must be stored in EU buckets (eu-west-1).
- Default retention policy matrix:
  - audit issuance records: 365 days
  - RBAC snapshots: 365 (540 for regulated customers)
  - session recordings: 90 days (legal hold overrides)
  - network snapshots: 180 days
- Legal holds: custodian marks manifest with hold=true and contacts legal@redwood.com. Storage team will apply object locks and extend retention to hold_until date.

Secure SDLC and Evidence Tooling Requirements
--------------------------------------------
- Collector tools must be part of source control, code-reviewed, and subject to the Secure SDLC pipeline: unit tests, dependency scanning, and SCA reports.
- Release gating: changes to collector or manifest schema must have an ADR and a migration plan; backwards compatibility is required for 12 months.

Post-incident: Evidence Packaging and Handoff
--------------------------------------------
1. Produce a signed zip archive: evidence-{incident_id}-{region}.zip and a manifest.sig signed by kms-evidence-sign.
2. Handoff to requestor (security, legal, customer success) with a transfer record in the evidence-tracking audit index. Handoff record fields: incident_id, recipient, transfer_time, custodian, transfer_method (secure-s3-link / sftp).
3. Maintain chain-of-custody log as a flat CSV in evidence-bucket: incident_id,artifact,hash,stored_at,accessed_by,access_time,action.

Ownership, Roles and SLAs
------------------------
- Custodian (security): assemble artifacts, sign manifest, apply legal holds. SLA: initial manifest in 4 hours for P1.
- Storage (eng-infra): apply retention changes and regional placement within 2 hours of request from custodian.
- SIEM Team: provide raw query results and correlation events within 60 minutes of request for P1.
- Legal: assess hold and evidence release within 24 hours of notification for P1 incidents.

Auditability & Verification
--------------------------
- Hash-and-sign verification steps: verify each artifact SHA256 matches manifest entry and verify manifest signature with kms-evidence-verify.
- Periodic integrity checks: weekly job re-computes hashes for artifacts with retention > 1 year and reports drift to security@sre.

Appendix A — Commands and snippets
---------------------------------
- Compute SHA256 and upload: sha256sum artifact.json | awk '{print $1}' > artifact.json.sha256
  aws s3 cp artifact.json s3://evidence/evidence/INC-2026-0421-07/ --acl bucket-owner-full-control

- KMS sign (example): aws kms sign --key-id arn:aws:kms:eu-west-1:111122223333:key/abcd-1234 --message fileb://manifest.json --output text --query Signature | base64 --decode > manifest.sig
