Integrated Access, Change, Procurement & Travel Guidance

Overview

This playbook consolidates company-wide operational policies for access & permissions, change management, data handling, procurement & expenses, vendor onboarding, and travel. It is intended for any employee initiating an access request, change, purchase, vendor engagement, or business travel. The goal is to reduce friction, centralize approvals, and preserve auditability while keeping business velocity.

Owner & Scope

- Owner: Finance & Legal (policy steward)
- Operational leads: Security (access), Eng Platform (change), Finance (procurements & expenses), People Ops (travel)
- Applies to: All Redwood employees, contractors, and third-party vendors interacting with Redwood systems or purchasing on behalf of Redwood.

Key Principles

1) Principle of least privilege: Grant minimum access required, with time-bound approvals for temporary access.
2) Separation of duties: Procurement approvals and payment execution should be separate from requesters.
3) Auditability: Every access, change, procurement, vendor, and travel request must be recorded in the originating system (Access Portal, Changeboard, Procurement Hub, Concur).
4) Risk-based controls: Higher-sensitivity resources require stronger checks (e.g., background check, contract, additional approvals).

1) Access & Permissions

Summary: Standardize identity, RBAC, group naming, onboarding/offboarding, and emergency access procedures.

- Identity & SSO
  - All production access requires SSO and MFA. Supported IdP: Okta (primary).
  - Contractors must be in a contractor Okta org and tagged with expiration date.

- RBAC & Groups
  - Role patterns: read-only, developer, admin, infra-operator, billing.
  - Group name convention: team-<team-name>-<role> (e.g., team-platform-devops-admin).
  - All groups must list an owner and purpose in the group metadata.

- Access request workflow (basic)
  1. Requestor files request in Access Portal with justification, duration, and resources.
  2. Team owner reviews: verify least privilege and business justification. (48 hour target)
  3. Approver (team lead or designated manager) approves; security checks auto-run (Snyk/IAM policy scan).
  4. Provisioning handled by automation (Where possible via self-service).
  5. Audit record created with request, approver, expiry.

- Emergency access (break-glass)
  - Emergency access must be requested via the on-call escalation channel and followed by an automatic temporary grant (max 4 hours). Post-incident, the requester must submit a full Access Portal form within 24 hours and attach incident ticket.

SLA & Metrics

- Approval SLA: 48 hours for routine requests; 4 hours for expedited (requires justification).
- Monthly audit: 100% of admin groups reviewed quarterly.

2) Change Management

Purpose: Minimize service disruption and maintain traceable approvals for changes to production systems.

Change types & thresholds

| Change Type       | Examples                              | Approval Path                       | Timeline        |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Standard (low risk)| Config flips, documentation updates   | Auto-approved via Changeboard       | 24–72 hours     |
| Normal            | Deployments, infra changes            | Team owner -> Eng Platform Review   | 48–120 hours    |
| Significant       | Schema changes, migrations, rollback plans | Change Advisory Board (CAB)        | 5–14 days       |
| Emergency         | P0 fixes, security hotpatches          | Emergency CAB (on-call + sec lead)  | Immediate       |

Change process (normal)

1. Create Change Request in Changeboard with scope, rollout plan, monitoring & rollback playbook, risk assessment, and stakeholders.
2. Automated checks run: linting, ownership verification, impacted-service list.
3. Team owner and Eng Platform reviewer approve.
4. If Significant, submit to CAB for calendar slot and pre-deployment review.
5. Deployment window executed with runbook; post-deploy verification & telemetry checks for 4 hours.
6. Close change and attach post-deployment notes.

Rollback & Observability Requirements

- Every production change must include an explicit rollback plan, health check thresholds (error rate > X% or latency > Y ms triggers alert), and a post-mortem owner.
- Minimum observability coverage: uptime dashboard, key business metric, and error budget signal.

3) Data Management & Classification

Policy summary: Classify data, map owners, define retention, and set controls by classification.

Classification table:

| Classification | Examples                         | Access Controls                                 | Retention                  |
|----------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Public         | Blog posts, marketing assets     | No restrictions                                  | indefinite                |
| Internal       | Design docs, non-sensitive logs  | Company SSO, MFA                                 | 2 years default          |
| Confidential   | Customer PII, contracts          | RBAC with additional approvers; encryption at rest | 7 years or contractual reqs |
| Restricted     | Secrets, KMS keys, PHI           | Restricted groups, hardware-backed keys (HSM)    | As required by law/contract |

Data handling rules:

- Confidential and Restricted data must be stored in approved systems (VPC-only storage, approved DBs).
- Exfiltration controls: DLP scanning enabled on file shares and email for Confidential+.
- Data subject requests: Privacy team manages DSARs; escalate via privacy@redwood.com.

4) Procurement & Expenses

Procurement thresholds & approvals:

| Spend band (USD)          | Required approvals                        | Procurement path               |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| <$1,000                 | Requester manager                         | Self-service procurement form |
| $1,000–$25,000          | Manager + Finance approver                | Procurement Hub ticket        |
| $25,000–$250,000        | Manager + Finance + Legal review          | Procurement SOW & PO          |
| >$250,000               | Exec sponsor + Legal + Finance Director   | Strategic procurement          |

Key rules:

- All purchases require a business justification, cost center, and expected contract term.
- For SaaS vendors that process customer data, vendor security questionnaire and DPA must be completed before purchase.
- P-card usage: limited to <$1,500 per transaction and subject to monthly reconciliation.

Expense policy highlights:

- Per diem: Domestic $75/day; International $125/day. Receipts required for anything above per diem.
- Air travel: Economy for flights < 6 hours; premium economy allowed for 6–9 hour flights with manager approval; business class requires Exec approval for >9 hours.
- Accommodation: Standard hotel rate within corporate travel policy; use corporate booking tool when possible.

Template snippets (expense reporting)

- Required fields: purpose, attendees, dates, cost center, receipt attachments, card used.
- Example description: "Customer kickoff — ACME Corp — 2025-02-04 — Team: Sales — Cost center: 4001"

5) Vendor Onboarding & Management

Minimum onboarding checklist:

1. Procurement Hub ticket with SOW/statement of work and owner.
2. Security review: vendor questionnaire completed; high-risk vendors require a penetration test report or SOC2 Type II.
3. Legal: Master Services Agreement (MSA) and DPA as required.
4. Finance: PO creation and payment terms agreed.
5. Account provisioning and least-privilege access setup (if vendor will access systems).
6. Enter vendor into vendor registry with owner, contract dates, and renewal reminders.

Vendor risk cadence:

- High-risk vendors: quarterly security reviews and contract health check.
- Medium-risk: annual reviews.
- Low-risk: biennial check-ins.

6) Travel Policy (Business Travel)

Booking & approvals:

- Submit travel request in Travel Portal at least 7 business days prior for domestic and 14 for international travel.
- Manager approval required; Finance reviews for high-dollar itineraries.

Safety & emergency:

- Register international travel with People Ops and consult travel advisories.
- In case of emergency, contact People Ops emergency line and on-call HR.

8) Roles & Responsibilities

- Requester: Completes request with accurate justification and attachments.
- Approver: Verifies need and least privilege; approves in stated SLA.
- Policy Owner (Finance & Legal): Maintains playbook and coordinates cross-functional reviews.
- Security: Runs automated checks, maintains RBAC guidance, and performs audits.
- Eng Platform: Reviews changes touching runtime/infrastructure.

9) Compliance & Audit

- Quarterly audits for admin groups, vendor contracts approaching renewal, and expense reconciliation.
- Audit artifacts (access logs, change tickets, invoices, signed contracts) must be retained per data classification and legal guidelines.

10) Exceptions & Change Control for the Playbook

- Exceptions: Submit an exception request to Finance & Legal with duration and remediation plan. Exceptional approvals limited to 90 days by default.
- Playbook changes: Minor edits can be made by owners; material policy changes follow a 2-week review and a published change log.

Appendix A — Quick approval matrix (short)

- Access < 30 days: Team lead
- Access > 30 days or admin role: Team lead + Security
- Procurement > $25k: Finance + Legal
- Production change that modifies DB schema: CAB approval required
