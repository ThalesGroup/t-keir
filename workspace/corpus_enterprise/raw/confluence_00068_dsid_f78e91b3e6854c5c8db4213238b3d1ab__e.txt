Error budget billing reconciliation and priority credit runbook

Summary:

This runbook defines the deterministic process for reconciling SLO-driven error budget burn with customer billing and for issuing priority credits (temporary elevated capacity or monetary credits) when SLO violations materially impacted customer SLAs. It covers detection, verification, audit evidence retention, automated reconciliation jobs, manual approval flows, credit issuance, and post-action review. The intent is to provide a reproducible, auditable, and customer-safe process that minimizes manual disputes and protects the platform’s cost model.

Audience:
- SRE oncall and escalation engineers
- Revenue operations and billing team (Finance)
- Customer Success managers
- Account execs for enterprise customers

Scope and goals:
1) Map SLO violations and error budget consumption to customer-facing impact windows and quantify eligible credits.
2) Provide an end-to-end automated reconciliation pipeline that produces an evidence bundle for Finance and Customer Success.
3) Define safe, time-bounded priority credits (capacity reservation) to maintain customer experience while minimizing platform cost.
4) Ensure all credits are traceable, approved, and reversible.

Key definitions:
- SLO incident window: contiguous period where a service-level metric (e.g., p99 latency or availability) is below target per the SLO definition.
- Error budget consumption (EBC): fraction of budget consumed in a rolling 28-day window attributable to platform service degradation.
- Impact-adjusted customer exposure (IACE): portion of EBC attributable to a given customer based on routing, region, and tenancy.
- Priority credit: temporary reservation of dedicated capacity or monetary credit applied to billing.

Ownership matrix:
- Detection & telemetry: Eng-SRE Observability team (primary)
- Reconciliation automation and evidence packaging: Eng-SRE Operational Tooling (primary) + Finance (consumer)
- Credit approval: Finance (final), CS (customer notification), SRE (technical justification)
- Priority capacity execution: Eng-Platform (implementation), Eng-SRE (validation)

Trigger conditions (automated):
1) Any SLO incident with severity >= P2 and duration >= 10 minutes triggers a partial run of the reconciliation pipeline.
2) Aggregate EBC > 12% during a 28-day window flags automated credit calculation.
3) Customer-specific telemetry indicates >10% request routing loss to fallback model/region during the incident window.

High-level workflow (steps):
1) Detection: Observability alerts populate a reconciliation ticket in the SRE Reconciler queue (Jira label: sb-reconcile).
2) Evidence collection (automated): run evidence collector job that pulls traces, token-level sampling, traffic routing logs, KV cache miss rates, model fallback metrics, and tenant routing records for the incident window. Evidence saved to S3 with 90-day retention (see retention policy).
3) Impact attribution: run impact calculator to produce IACE per customer. The calculator uses routing logs and token counters to weigh per-tenant exposure.
4) Credit calculation: apply the credit rules table (below) to compute recommended monetary credit and/or priority-capacity minutes.
5) Approval: packet with recommended amount sent to Finance + CS + SRE owner for sign-off via Slack workflow + Jira approval step.
6) Execution: Finance issues billing credit or Eng-Platform provisions priority capacity as agreed.
7) Customer notification: CS sends templated explanation and remediation notes.
8) Post-action review: SRE leads a 2-week follow-up to confirm incident remediation and avoid duplicate credits.

Credit rules (config):
- Monetary credit = floor(IACE * Customer-monthly-recurring-revenue * adjustment-factor), where adjustment-factor defaults to 0.2 for availability incidents and 0.1 for latency incidents unless escalated.
- Priority capacity credit = min(1200 minutes, IACE * 2880 minutes) — expressed as reserved GPU-minutes on Dedicated or ephemeral priority tokens on Hosted.
- Minimum credit threshold: $50 or 100 GPU-minutes. Events below threshold require manual review before crediting.

Example credit matrix (text table):
- Small customer (MRR < $5k): IACE 20% during 2h outage -> monetary credit = floor(0.2 * $2,000 * 0.2) = $80 -> Issue monetary credit + 240 GPU-min priority if requested.
- Enterprise customer (MRR > $100k): IACE 5% during sustained 24h degradation -> monetary credit = floor(0.05 * $150,000 * 0.2) = $1,500 -> Offer priority capacity + account-level remediation SLA.

Automation implementation notes (CLI & SQL snippets):
1) Evidence collector (example CLI trigger):
- ./reconciler collect --service=model-serving --window-start=2026-05-18T03:12Z --window-end=2026-05-18T05:22Z --out-s3=s3://reconciliation-evidence/incident-20260518-0312

2) Sample SQL to compute per-tenant token exposure (Postgres / analytics DB):
- SELECT tenant_id, SUM(tokens) as token_count
  FROM token_usage
  WHERE ingest_ts >= '2026-05-18T03:12:00Z' AND ingest_ts <= '2026-05-18T05:22:00Z'
    AND route in (SELECT route FROM route_table WHERE service='model-serving')
  GROUP BY tenant_id
  ORDER BY token_count DESC;

3) Impact attribution (pseudo):
- For each tenant: IACE = tenant_token_count / total_token_count * incident_service_degraded_fraction

4) Automated approval Slack workflow (example payload to /slack/workflows):
- POST /api/workflows/trigger
  {"trigger_id": "reconcile-12345", "approvers": ["finance@redwood.com","cs@redwood.com"], "summary": "Recommended credit: $1,500 for tenant ACME (IACE 5%)"}

Evidence retention policy:
- Raw telemetry (traces/metric-series/raw-logs): retain 30 days by default; extend to 90 days for incident evidence bundles linked to reconciliations.
- Aggregated reconciliation artifacts (impact reports, SQL extracts, approval history): retain 3 years for audit and accounting.
- Access controls: evidence buckets are restricted to Eng-SRE, Finance, and CS with read/write auditing enabled.

Monitoring & alerts for the pipeline itself:
- Reconciler job failures: PagerDuty to Eng-SRE Operational Tooling (P1 if collection fails for critical incidents).
- Evidence-packaging delays > 30 minutes: Email + Slack to SRE tooling owners.
- Drift between automated recommended credit vs. Finance manual: Weekly reconciliation report and retraining of adjustment-factor.

Escalation & manual override guidance:
- Finance has authority to override automated monetary credits but must add a reason to the approval ticket and attach manual calculation.
- Eng-SRE can request priority capacity without monetary credit for short-duration critical production incidents; Finance must be notified within 24 hours.
- Any override that increases credit above 2x automated recommendation requires Director-level sign-off from Finance.

Rollback and reversal:
- Credits issued in error: Finance will reverse via billing system and create a reversal ticket with reason. All reversals must include the reconciliation artifact.
- Priority capacity revocation: Eng-Platform will de-provision reserved capacity and log the action to the incident ticket. If the customer incurred costs externally (rare), CS and Finance coordinate remediation.

Telemetry queries (examples to paste into Grafana or Observability):
- Availability SLO: query service_health.policy('model-serving', 'availability', '28d') to see rolling window.
- Tenant exposure: custom panel that joins token usage and route health during the incident window; pre-built panel id: sre.impact.tenant-exposure. Note: panel references require SSO access.

Checklist for initiating a reconciliation (quick reference):
1) Confirm incident window and severity.
2) Kick evidence collector (automated if trigger conditions met).
3) Validate impact attribution outputs (top 10 tenants).
4) Review recommended credit and compare to SLA in customer contract.
5) Create approval ticket and trigger Slack approval workflow.
6) After approval, notify CS to coordinate customer communication.

KPIs & telemetry for continuous improvement:
- Mean time from incident detection to recommended credit (target < 4 hours).
- Percentage of automated recommendations accepted by Finance (target > 85%).
- Number of credit disputes raised by customers per quarter (target < 2% of credits).
- Cost delta introduced by priority capacity credits as % of platform spend (target < 1.5% monthly).

Post-incident review template (to attach to Jira ticket):
- Incident summary and timeline (UTC)
- SLO metrics and graphs for the window
- Evidence bundle S3 path
- Per-tenant IACE table and credit recommendation details
- Approvals and execution evidence (Finance ticket ids, Slack approvals)
- Lessons learned and follow-up actions (owners + due dates)

Security and compliance notes:
- Do not include PII in evidence bundles. Strip or redact any request payloads before archiving.
- All evidence access is logged; Finance access is time-limited to relevant artifacts.

Appendix: troubleshooting common failure modes
- No token entries for tenant in incident window: confirm routing TTLs and whether tenant was served by a fallback region; check ingress proxies for sampling.
- Evidence collector times out: increase collector parallelism for large incidents and file a tooling incident.
- Discrepancy with Finance ledger: run the monthly reconciliation audit job and attach delta report to the ticket.
