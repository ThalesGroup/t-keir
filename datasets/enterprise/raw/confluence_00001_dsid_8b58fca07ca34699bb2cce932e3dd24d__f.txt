Forensic-ready Compliance Playbook for Emergency Deployments

Overview

Purpose:
This playbook describes the combined technical and procedural controls required to remain forensically and legally compliant when performing emergency or out-of-band deployments, regional failovers, or temporary entitlement exceptions that cross normal RBAC, data residency, and SDLC boundaries. It prescribes: who may authorize an emergency deployment, what audit evidence must be captured, how to preserve data residency guarantees, and how to assemble a compliance evidence package suitable for regulators and internal audit.

Scope:
- Applies to hosted, dedicated, and private (VPC/on-prem) Redwood deployments.
- Covers emergency code releases, rollback operations, region-to-region failover, and temporary role escalations lasting <= 72 hours.
- Excludes planned maintenance with advance approval from Change Control.

Key Principles:
1) Forensic readiness: any emergency action must be captured end-to-end with immutable audit records, cryptographic verification where applicable, and a minimal evidence manifest.
2) Least privilege + compensating controls: temporary exceptions must be timeboxed, scoped, and paired with increased telemetry and monitoring.
3) Data locality preservation: customer residency SLAs must be honored or explicitly waived and logged in the evidence bundle.
4) Post-fact governance: all emergency actions require retrospective review, remediation, and either a permanent policy change or revocation.

Roles & Ownership:
- Incident Commander (IC): Security & Compliance oncall (sec-oncall). Authorizes emergency exception and certifies the evidence bundle.
- Deployment Lead: Eng-Platform owner of the deployment-pipeline service (service: deployment-gate). Executes the emergency deploy.
- Auth Owner: Eng-Auth (service: authz-service). Applies temporary role grants and records RBAC deltas.
- Audit Custodian: Eng-SRE (service: audit-collector + evidence-orchestrator). Ensures immutable storage and artifact packaging.
- Legal/Privacy Reviewer: Legal team. Consulted when cross-border data movement occurs.

Pre-deployment Checklist (must be completed before executing emergency action):
- IC approval recorded in the incident ticket (Jira ID required).
- Timebox declared (default: 72 hours).
- Affected customers/tenants enumerated and residency requirements noted.
- RBAC deltas planned and scoped. Use short-lived groups (prefix: emergency-YYYYMMDD).
- Audit pipeline health check: audit-collector retention tier \u003e= 30 days and append-only store reachable.
- Snapshot: automated snapshot of configuration and model artifacts stored with SHA256 checksums in artifacts-bucket (kms-integrator).

Emergency Exception Workflow (step-by-step):
1) Request & Approval
   - Requester opens incident ticket and selects template: Emergency Deployment – Compliance Evidence.
   - IC confirms necessity on #sec-incident and records explicit authorization: UTC timestamp, approver, reason, scope.
2) Scoped RBAC Grant
   - Auth Owner creates temporary role in authz-service with TTL <= declared timebox. Naming: emergency-grant-<ticket>.
   - All role creations/assignments must include originator, justification, and correlating ticket id.
3) Audit Hardening
   - Turn on verbose auditing for involved services: deployment-gate, model-serving, data-proxy.
   - Increase retention for the incident partition to legal_hold by toggling retention-tag via audit-collector API.
4) Deployment & Data Controls
   - Deployment Lead performs deploy through deployment-gate using --evidence-manifest flag.
   - If cross-region movement occurs, data-residency waiver must be recorded and signed-off by Legal.
5) Evidence Packaging (see Evidence Collection section).
6) Revoke & Remediate
   - Immediately after stabilized: revoke emergency roles, rotate affected keys, revert any temporary config flags that broaden access.
   - Open a post-incident CR for permanent remediation if needed.

Evidence Collection (minimum artifact set):
- Immutable audit log slice (start_ts..end_ts) for: authz-service, deployment-gate, audit-collector, data-proxy, model-serving-runtime. Format: JSONL, signed at storage by evidence-orchestrator.
- RBAC delta manifest: CSV listing grants, principal, scope, TTL, created_by, created_at.
- Deployment manifest: git SHA, image digests, environment variables with redaction markers for secrets, and operation transcript.
- Data residency waiver (if applicable): PDF signed by Legal with timestamp and list of affected tenant IDs.
- Snapshot of configuration plus SHA256 of model artifacts and schema versions.
- Incident ticket & approver log (Jira export).
- Chain-of-custody log for any data extracts (who accessed, purpose, retention).

Audit Logging Configuration (operational details):
- Required fields: timestamp (ISO8601), service, operation, principal_id, request_id, correlation_id, before/state deltas, after/state deltas, outcome, region, retention_tag.
- SLOs: audit ingestion 99.99% availability; mean ingestion latency \u003c= 2s; immutable store replication across 2 regions for hosted deployments.
- Retention mapping: default customer-audit: 1 year; legal_hold: 7 years; ephemeral_debug: 30 days.
- Immutable storage: evidence-orchestrator writes a signed bundle to s3://evidence-locked/{ticket}/bundle-{timestamp}.tar.gz with kms-integrator envelope encryption.

RBAC Adjustments & Controls:
- All emergency grants must be implemented via authz-service API with the header X-Incident-ID set. Ad-hoc console grants are forbidden.
- Short-lived credentials must be provisioned via the service-token-exchange (max TTL 8 hours).
- Deprovisioning hook: revocation events are automatically recorded and a revocation-proof object is attached to the evidence bundle.

Data Residency and Retention Considerations:
- If data must cross jurisdictional boundaries during failover, the Legal/Privacy Reviewer must approve and sign a waiver before the action proceeds. The waiver must state the minimal subset of affected data and retention/erasure commitments.
- For private deployments, customers must be notified within SLA windows specified in their contract (default: 24 hours). Notification artifacts are included in the evidence bundle.
- All temporary copies created for forensic or rollback purposes must be tagged with retention_tag=ephemeral_debug and auto-deleted after 30 days unless escalated to legal_hold.

SDLC Gating and Emergency Releases:
- Deployment-gate has two distinct flows: normal (CI->canary->gradual) and emergency (IC-authorized single-step). Emergency runs must still produce the same artifacts as the normal flow (build manifest, test run ids) and include rationale for bypassing gates.
- Code review exemptions are conditional: emergency PRs must be triaged and tagged emergency-pr and undergo retrospective code review within 5 business days.

Evidence Packaging & Submission (artifact manifest format):
- evidence-manifest.yaml (top level):
  - ticket_id: STRING
  - ic_approver: STRING
  - start_ts: ISO8601
  - end_ts: ISO8601
  - artifacts: [list of artifact filenames]
  - retention_policy_applied: STRING

Example evidence-manifest.yaml snippet:

```yaml
ticket_id: INC-2025-1234
ic_approver: a.kapoor@redwood.com
start_ts: 2025-09-12T14:03:21Z
end_ts: 2025-09-12T14:21:09Z
artifacts:
  - audit-slice-authz-20250912.jsonl
  - rbac-delta-INC-2025-1234.csv
  - deployment-manifest-20250912.json
retention_policy_applied: legal_hold
```

Evidence submission process:
1) Audit Custodian runs evidence-orchestrator --package --ticket INC-2025-1234.
2) The orchestrator validates signatures, computes checksums, uploads to s3://evidence-locked/ and writes a pointer to the internal evidence index (immutable).
3) Notification sent to Legal + Compliance channel with secure link (signed URL TTL: 7 days).

Post-incident Review & Remediation:
- Conduct a Post-Incident Compliance Review (PICR) within 10 business days. Required attendees: IC, Deployment Lead, Auth Owner, Audit Custodian, Legal Reviewer, and the affected product owner.
- Deliverables: updated incident timeline, lessons learned, concrete remediation tickets, confirmation of role/secret rotation, and closure of temporary artifacts or escalation to legal_hold.

Escalation & Exceptions Register (risk and exceptions):
- Any emergency action that increases cross-border data exposure beyond the approved waiver must be escalated immediately to the Head of Security and the Chief Privacy Officer.
- Maintain an Exceptions Register (sheet-id: protect://exceptions/registry) that captures: ticket, approver, business rationale, risk_assessment_score (1-100), expiration, and artifacts. Entries are audited quarterly.

Operational Runbook Snippets (CLI examples):
- Create a timeboxed role (Auth Owner):

```bash
curl -X POST https://authz.internal.redwood/v1/roles \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"name":"emergency-grant-INC-2025-1234","ttl_seconds":259200,"scopes":["deploy:execute","config:write"] ,"metadata":{"incident_id":"INC-2025-1234","created_by":"deployment-lead@redwood.com"}}'
```

- Package evidence (Audit Custodian):

```bash
evidence-orchestrator package --ticket INC-2025-1234 --output /tmp/INC-2025-1234-bundle.tar.gz \
  --sign-key /var/secrets/evidence-signing-key.pem --upload-bucket evidence-locked
```

Appendix B — Quick decision checklist (for IC):
- Is emergency action the only viable path to meet customer SLAs or safety?
- Has Legal been contacted for cross-jurisdictional data movement?
- Are audit pipelines green and archivable?
- Is there a clear rollback plan and timebox?
