Gatekeeper: Spend, Access, and Change Guidelines

Overview

This document (the Gatekeeper guide) defines a single, cross-functional playbook for approving and executing access requests, infrastructure and configuration changes, procurement and expense flows, vendor onboarding, travel approvals, and basic data handling obligations. The goal is to reduce latency for routine operations while preserving security, compliance, and predictable financial control.

Scope and purpose

- Applies to all Redwood employees, contractors, and short-term vendors interacting with company systems and purchasing resources.
- Covers: access and permissions, change management for production and platform components, data classification and retention rules, procurement and expense approvals, vendor onboarding/offboarding, and travel booking/expense rules.
- Excludes: long-term contracts (handled by Legal), specialized classified-data projects (see security-and-compliance restricted policies).

Definitions and role matrix

- Requester: person initiating access/purchase/change/travel request.
- Approver: person or role responsible for authorizing the request per the matrix below.
- Gatekeeper: delegated finance or security role who performs final verification for spend over the threshold or high-risk access.

Approval matrix (summary)

- Low-risk access (read-only to non-sensitive services): Team Lead approves.
- Developer access to non-production infra: Team Lead + Infra Owner approves.
- Production access (any write/exec in prod): SRE Manager + Security Reviewer, must be time-bound.
- Purchases under $3,000: Requester’s Manager.
- Purchases $3,000–$50,000: Manager + Finance Gatekeeper.
- Purchases > $50,000: Manager + Finance Gatekeeper + Legal + VP sign-off.
- Travel (domestic): Manager approval. International travel or travel including high-risk jurisdictions: Manager + Security review.

Access and permissions

Principles

1. Least privilege by default: Only grant minimum permissions required to complete the task.
2. Temporary access: Production access must have an explicit expiry. Default expiry is 30 days; critical tasks should use a temporary jump-host or ephemeral role.
3. Justification and auditability: Every access grant must include a brief justification and an associated ticket id.

Process: requesting access

1. Open an Access Request in the central tools (Confluence link: /access-requests or via the corporate ServiceNow instance).
2. Include: system name, exact resource(s), justification, expected duration, and approval chain.
3. Team Lead performs initial review for completeness; infra or SRE owner validates technical scope.
4. Security performs elevated review for production or sensitive-data systems.

Break-glass and emergency access

- Emergency access is allowed only via the break-glass process: phone escalation to on-call SRE and Security, followed by a temporary token issuance documented in the incident ticket.
- All break-glass actions must be logged in the audit trail and reviewed within 48 hours by Security and the requester's manager.

Change management (config and infra changes)

Categories

- Low-risk: documentation updates, non-prod config updates.
- Medium-risk: schema migrations in staging, infra scaling operations.
- High-risk: production schema migrations, config changes affecting customer traffic or security posture.

Change workflow

1. Create a Change Request (CR) in the central tracker with rollforward and rollback plans and a risk assessment (low/med/high).
2. For medium/high risk, schedule a Change Window aligned to the SRE calendar and notify stakeholders 72 hours in advance.
3. Pre-change checklist (must be completed in the CR): test results, canary plan, monitoring dashboards, alert thresholds, communication plan, rollback steps.
4. Post-change validation: owner documents validation steps and marks the CR as complete within 24 hours.

SLOs and guardrails

- All high-risk changes require a postmortem within 7 days if they trigger any P1/P2 incident.
- Medium- and high-risk changes require a production readiness review at least 48 hours before the window.

Data management and classification

Classification table (summary)

| Classification | Examples | Encryption | Retention | Access control |
|----------------|----------|------------|-----------|----------------|
| Public | Marketing site content | Not required | 3 years | Open to all employees |
| Internal | Internal docs, non-sensitive logs | At rest recommended | 7 years | Team-level role-based |
| Confidential | Customer metadata, billing records | Mandatory (KMS) | 7 years | Justified, audited access |
| Restricted | PII, regulated customer data | Mandatory (KMS + HSM option) | Per contract/legal | Minimal, controlled by Security |

Key rules

- Use only company-approved storage locations for Confidential and Restricted data. Approval from Data Governance is required to create any new data store for Restricted data.
- Retention schedules are enforced via automated lifecycle policies where possible; manual retention overrides must have Governance approval.

Procurement and expenses

Principles

- Clear documentation for approvals, separation of duties, and cost center tagging are mandatory.
- Preferred vendors should be used where contracts exist. Use the vendor registry before initiating new procurement.

Quick purchase procedure (<= $3,000)

1. Submit a Purchase Request (PR) in the procurement system with line-item detail and cost center.
2. Manager approves.
3. Finance Gatekeeper validates budget coding and issues a Purchase Order (PO) or credit card instruction.

Standard procurement (> $3,000)

1. Require at least one comparison quote or a vendor justification when a sole-source is used.
2. Finance Gatekeeper engages Legal for contract review when vendor terms exceed standard risk thresholds or include data processing.

Vendor onboarding and offboarding

Checklist for onboarding

- Complete Vendor Intake Form with security questionnaire.
- Confirm insurance and SOC2/ISO documentation as required by risk tier.
- Assign vendor account owner and access scope (principle of least privilege).
- Establish contract with data handling and termination clauses.
- Enter vendor into the vendor registry and tag by risk tier.

Offboarding steps

- Revoke all access and API keys within 24 hours of contract termination.
- Confirm deletion or return of data in accordance with contract.
- Close vendor accounts and archive the onboarding package in vendor registry.

Travel policies and expense rules

- Travel must be pre-approved through the corporate travel tool. Manager approval required for all trips.
- For international travel: Security review for region-specific advisories, and Finance approval for per-diem and budget impact.
- Standard booking class: economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy or business only with VP approval.
- Hotel: mid-range business hotels preferred; nightly rate caps are maintained in the expense system per region.

Expense reporting and reconciliation

- Submit expenses within 30 days of travel end. Include receipts for all items above $25.
- Cardholders must reconcile corporate card transactions weekly.
- Finance performs monthly audits of random expense reports (target 10% sample).

Operational responsibilities and escalation

- Team Lead: first-level approver for low-risk actions, maintains access rosters.
- Security: final reviewer for restricted data and production access.
- Finance Gatekeeper: validates purchases, budget alignment, and vendor risk.
- Legal: mandatory for contract reviews that include non-standard terms or >$50k spend.
- SRE/Platform: approves production changes and defines rollback procedures.

Escalation path

1. Manager -> Gatekeeper (Finance/Security) -> Legal as required.
2. If timing is critical and approvals would block a business-critical activity, request an expedited review via the "gatekeeper-express" channel with explicit business justification; the Gatekeeper team target is 4 business hours for expedited approvals.

Monitoring, audits, and reporting

- All access grants, changes, and high-value purchases are logged in the central governance ledger. The ledger is exported monthly for reconciliation and audit.
- Quarterly reviews: Security and Finance run a joint audit of production access lists, vendor registry entries, and top 50 spenders.

Exceptions and risk acceptance

- Exception requests must be submitted to the Governance board with a compensating control plan and defined expiry (maximum 6 months).
- Risk acceptance for residual risks over $50k or critical customer impact must be signed by VP of Product and Legal.

Appendix: sample checklists

Purchase checklist (short)

- Business case attached
- Cost center tagged
- Manager approval recorded
- Vendor in registry or vendor justification provided
- Finance Gatekeeper approval for > $3k

Production change pre-flight checklist

- Tests passed in staging (include test ids)
- Canary plan defined and automated
- Monitoring and alerts validated
- Rollback steps tested
- Communications plan with incident contacts
