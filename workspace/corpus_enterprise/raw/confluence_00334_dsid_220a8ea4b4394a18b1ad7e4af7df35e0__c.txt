Conversational feedback loop & labeling integration playbook

Overview:

This playbook describes an end-to-end integration pattern for ingesting conversational feedback from customer applications, enriching and routing it to a labeling hub and model-eval harness, and surfacing high-quality signal into QBR reporting and model retraining pipelines. The goal is to make feedback ingestion reliable, auditable and cost-efficient while providing deterministic lanes for immediate remediation, quality telemetry and curated label sets for retraining.

Scope and intended audience:
- Audience: Customer Success Engineers, Integration Engineers, Observability owners and ML Ops.
- Out of scope: research-only annotation experiments and external crowdworker contracting.

Prerequisites and required services:
- ingress-gateway: mTLS or token-auth endpoint customers post feedback to.
- telemetry-gatekeeper: JSON schema validation, deduplication and rate limiting.
- event bus: Kafka (telemetry-events-prod) or durable queue (PubSub/SQS) with DLQ.
- labeling-hub: supports API and web UI for human labeling and enrichment.
- model-eval-harness: nightly batch and online scoring harness.
- telemetry-archive: S3-compatible archive for long-term storage and legal hold.

Logical flow (text diagram):
Customer App -> ingress-gateway -> telemetry-gatekeeper -> telemetry-events-prod (feedback.raw) -> enrichment workers -> labeling-hub (labeling.inbound) -> human labelers/auto-labelers -> labeling-hub.outbound -> model-eval-harness -> eval.results -> telemetry-archive / qbr-dataset

Data contract and schema (required fields):
- event_id (string): globally unique id, e.g. evt_01F4YZ
- tenant_id (string): tenant-abc
- timestamp_iso (string): RFC3339 UTC, e.g. 2026-01-11T16:23:12Z
- source (string): enum {chat-widget, voice, api, webhook}
- feedback_type (string): enum {correction, rating, taxonomy_tag, ignore}
- payload (string): JSON-encoded payload, e.g. '{"correction":"Prefer X"}'
Optional fields: user_id (hash recommended), session_id, original_response (trimmed to 8k tokens), confidence (0..1).

Security & privacy controls:
- PII handling: user_id must be hashed at client or ingress-gateway (default SHA256 with tenant-managed salt) unless contract allows plaintext.
- Access controls: labeling-hub and telemetry-archive must be RBAC-restricted. Roles: viewer, labeler, curator, admin.
- Legal hold: telemetry-archive retention tags; events may be marked retention=legal_hold.

Ingestion examples (cURL):
1) Minimal rating event:

  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json'

Validation and common rejection reasons (telemetry-gatekeeper):
- 400 INVALID_SCHEMA: missing required field(s)
- 401 AUTH_FAILED: token expired or invalid
- 429 RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED: per-tenant rate control
- 413 PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE: payload/original_response exceeds 64kB limit
- 422 DUPLICATE_EVENT: event_id seen in last 24h

Enrichment and automated labeling rules (examples):
- Enrichment adds tenant_plan, model_version, route.
- Auto-label rules:
  - rating <= 0 -> auto-label=low-quality
  - correction length < 30 chars -> short-corrections queue for curator review
  - banned-content regex match -> escalate to Safety queue

Sampling and retention policy for QBR datasets:
- Deterministic sampling: 10% of feedback events per tenant per week, stratified by feedback_type and model_version.
- High-signal retention: any event auto-labeled low-quality or routed to Safety retained 2 years.

Operational runbook (triage playbook):
1) Alert: labeling-hub throughput drop (LH_Throughput_p95_below_threshold)
   - Check telemetry-gatekeeper: error rates and 429s.
   - Verify consumer lag: kafka-consumer-groups --describe --group labeling-inbound-consumer --bootstrap-server kafka.prod:9092
   - If DLQ growth, inspect 413/413s for oversized payloads.
2) Duplicate-event storm
   - Mitigation: enable dedupe-mode=drop-duplicates on telemetry-gatekeeper, route ingest to fallback buffer endpoint returning 202 while investigating.
3) Suspected PII incident
   - Action: tag suspect tenant with legal_hold, export batch for secure wipe (follow data-erasure playbook), notify Legal.

Integration rollout checklist:
Preflight:
- Confirm tenant_id mapping and auth method (mTLS or token)
- Exchange public keys / JWKS endpoints
- Validate sample payloads against telemetry-gatekeeper staging endpoint
- Confirm labeling workflow and roles list
Canary (1 week):
- Route 1% traffic to feedback pipeline; monitor ingestion success, p95 latency and false-positive auto-label rate
- Weekly sync with Solutions Engineering and CS to tune rules
Full launch:
- Enable full traffic routing, enable stratified sampling for QBR and add tenant to retention dashboards

Model quality & QBR integration:
- model-eval-harness runs nightly jobs joining labeled items with model metadata to compute metrics (precision@k for corrections, conversion lift, false-positive rate).
- QBR outputs: weekly top-10 failure patterns; monthly curated dataset (~5k examples) for retraining candidates; quarterly executive summary with net quality delta and remediation plan.

Troubleshooting cheatsheet:
- Ingest 401s: confirm JWT audience (aud) matches ingress-gateway expected_aud; check clock skew > 2 minutes.
- High 413s: clients sending full conversation traces; recommend client-side trimming and summaries.
- Sudden low-quality spike: check recent model-version rollout and latency regressions.

Suggested monitoring dashboard panels:
- Ingest: events/sec, p50/p95 ingestion latency, error rates (400/401/413/429), DLQ growth.
- Quality: auto-label precision (sample-verified), manual label throughput, median time-to-label.
- Business: tenant-level feedback rate normalized by MAU.

FAQ:
Q: Can we accept raw audio feedback?
A: Ingest audio only after transcription by a companion service; mark source=voice and include transcript in payload.
