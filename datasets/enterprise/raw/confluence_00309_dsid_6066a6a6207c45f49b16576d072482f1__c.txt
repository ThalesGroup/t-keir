Cross-role starter kits and benefits mapping

Overview

Purpose: This document defines cross-functional starter kits (role-specific sets of accounts, access, learning artifacts, and benefits eligibility steps) and a single source of truth for mapping benefits and entitlements to role types. It reduces variance in new-hire experience, speeds provisioning, and clarifies ownership for role changes and reboarding.

Scope: Applies to all full-time employees, contractors on >90 day engagements, interns, and role-transfers within Redwood. Does NOT replace team-specific orientation content hosted in team wikis; it standardizes the baseline.

Who should use this doc
- People Ops: run provisioning, benefits enrollment, and audits.
- Hiring managers: verify and accept starter-kit items during the first 7 days.
- IT/Security: fulfill access requests and ensure least-privilege.
- New hires and role-transfers: follow checklists and provide completion confirmations.

Design principles
- Minimal friction: baseline starter kits must be automatable via Okta and our provisioning playbooks.
- Role-first: every role type gets a curated, small set of immediate access and learning items to minimize cognitive load.
- Observable SLAs: defined turnarounds for provisioning and benefits enrollment with measurable metrics.

Summary of responsibilities
- People Ops: authorizes offers, triggers access pipeline, manages benefits mapping. SLA: 24 hours to start the pipeline after offer acceptance.
- IT (Access team): completes Okta/Google Workspace/Zoom/Miro provisioning. SLA: 48 business hours (standard); 4 hours for priority hires (exec/critical runway).
- Security: approves elevated access and runs quarterly audits.
- Hiring Manager: completes role-specific onboarding tasks and schedules role shadowing.

Starter-kit checklists (detailed)
A. IC - Software (first 7 days)
1. Pre-onboarding (People Ops)
   - Offer accepted -> trigger provisioning pipeline (Okta + email + hardware request).
   - Send the Pre-Start packet: Slack channel invite link, team README, role goals template.
2. Day 0-1 (IT / Manager)
   - Provision laptop, MDM enrollment, corporate email.
   - Join team Slack + add to #team-oncall (if relevant).
   - Grant GitHub team membership and create local dev environment doc.
3. Day 2-7 (Manager / Buddy)
   - Assign buddy and schedule 1:1s.
   - Complete codebase walkthrough checklist and first-issue guide.
   - Confirm onboarding tasks in Workday/Jira.

B. SRE / IC-Infra (first 14 days)
1. Pre-onboarding (Security & People Ops)
   - Submit elevated access request with justification and owner.
2. Day 0-3 (Security/IT)
   - Grant VPN and bastion access after MFA and training completion.
   - Enroll in privileged access management (PAM) system.
3. Day 4-14 (SRE Manager)
   - Pair with on-call engineer and validate runbook access.
   - Ensure monitoring dashboards and alerts are shared.

C. Managers (first 30 days)
- All IC checklist items plus:
  - Finance: request team budget access (Concur approver role).
  - HR: complete manager training in Lattice and schedule a people calibration review.
  - Tools: access to team-level admin in Jira/GitHub as needed.

Benefits mapping (eligibility & enrollment)
- Core benefits (eligible day 1 for FT employees): medical, dental, vision, 401(k) match, commuter stipend.
- Time-based benefits: parental leave (eligible after 90 days); sabbatical eligibility after 3 years.
- Contractors: medical stipend only when contract >= 6 months and flagged in offer letter.
- International hires: benefits mapped by location; People Ops will share country-specific details at offer stage.

Benefits enrollment process
1. People Ops sends enrollment email within 24h of start date (SLA).
2. Employee completes online forms; People Ops confirms completion and logs the enrollment ID.
3. Finance confirms payroll deductions within one pay cycle.

Access provisioning flow (detailed steps)
1. Automated trigger (People Ops) on offer acceptance: creates a provisioning ticket in ServiceDesk (SD-<hire-id>).
2. IT/Access team runs Okta onboarding playbook (playbook link: /confluence/eng-infra/ci-cd-and-build/okta-onboarding-playbook).
3. Security gates check: MFA enabled, security awareness course scheduled.
4. DevOps/Eng Tools assigns GitHub/Jira teams based on role taxonomy.
5. People Ops sends benefits enrollment and collects I-9 (US) or local equivalent.

SLOs and monitoring
- Provisioning start: People Ops triggers pipeline within 24 hours of signed offer.
- Standard provisioning completion: 48 business hours for accounts and Slack; 3 business days for hardware.
- Elevated access (infra/admin): decision within 5 business days; approvals logged in PAM.
- Measurement: weekly dashboard in Redwood Console (Onboarding pipeline) with these KPIs: % within SLA, median time-to-complete, outstanding tickets by stage.

Exception and expedited paths
- For critical hires (product launch, sales closing, execs) mark ticket with priority: P1. IT SLO: 4 hours for accounts; hardware expedited shipping. Manager must add business justification to ticket.
- Role transfers: manager opens a "role-change" request which runs an access reconciliation; expected completion 7 business days.

Deprovisioning and role changes
- On exit/contract end: People Ops triggers deprovisioning ticket. Standard deprovisioning timeline: 7 calendar days to remove accounts, 30 days to archive mailbox per retention policy.
- Role downscopes: submit an access-reduction request; Security reviews and schedules change within 5 business days.

Audit and compliance
- Quarterly audit: Security runs an automated Okta group membership export; People Ops reconciles against active headcount.
- Evidence: store audit exports in the compliance bucket (s3://redwood-compliance/onboarding-audits/YYYY-MM).
