Benefits activation and eligibility playbook

Overview

Purpose: This playbook documents how Redwood Inference activates, verifies, and maintains employee benefits eligibility across role changes, onboarding events, leaves, and internal transfers. It is intended for people-ops partners, hiring managers, and enrollment specialists.

Scope: medical, dental, vision, retirement (401k), paid time off (PTO), parental leave, tuition & professional development stipends, commuter/office stipends, and company sabbatical programs.

Quick summary of owner and SLA
- Owner: People Operations Benefits & Enrollment (people-ops-benefits@redwood.ai)
- Escalation: benefits-ops-lead -> Head of People Ops -> General Counsel (for exceptions)
- SLA: new-hire benefit provisioning within 5 business days after first-day completion; eligibility disputes resolved within 10 business days.

Eligibility rules (high-level)
1) New hires\   - Regular full-time employees (FTE) are eligible for core benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401k) on the first of the month following 30 days of service unless local country rules require earlier coverage.\   - Part-time employees: eligibility follows the part-time policy table below.\   - Contractors: contractors are eligible for limited programs (commuter, learning stipend) only when explicitly stated in the contract.

2) Role changes and promotions\   - If a role change does not involve a break in service, benefits continue uninterrupted. People-ops must be notified via the Role Change Form (HR-ROLE-REQ) within 3 business days.\   - Compensation level changes do not change medical eligibility; however, certain perks (executive coaching, expanded stipend) may require manager-requested provisioning.

3) Internal transfers between legal entities or regions\   - Transfers that change the legal employing entity require benefits re-enrollment. The transfer coordinator (hiring manager + people-ops reps) must initiate cross-entity enrollment at least 30 days before the target start date in the new entity.

4) Leaves of absence\   - Short-term leaves (<= 30 days): benefits remain active; payroll deductions continue per normal cadence.\   - Medical/parental leaves > 30 days: people-ops will notify benefits carriers and provide options for paid/unpaid continuation; employees are sent a Benefits Continuation Packet with deadlines and payment instructions.

Activation checklist (new hires) — step-by-step
1. Hiring manager: submit New-Hire Ticket with candidate start date and working location at offer acceptance.
2. HRBP: verify offer details and confirm start date in HRIS.
3. On employee first day: people-ops sends enrollment packet and credentials for benefits portal.
4. Employee: completes benefits elections within 30 days (default plan applied if no election).
5. People-ops: confirms enrollment with carriers and verifies access to ID cards within 5 business days of election.
6. Payroll: validates first payroll deduction; report any discrepancies to benefits@redwood.ai.

Manager responsibilities during onboarding and role changes\- Ensure the New-Hire Ticket is accurate and includes relocation or cross-entity details.\- Complete the Role Change Form for promotions/transfers within 3 business days.\- Approve one-time requests tied to role level (e.g., onboarding relocation top-ups, special equipment allowances).

Special programs and eligibility triggers\- Professional Development Stipend: All FTEs at level IC2 and above receive a $1,200 annual stipend; managers may allocate additional budget for role-critical certifications. Stipend requests must be submitted via the Learning Portal and approved by the manager.
- Sabbatical (Service-based): Employees are eligible for a 6-week paid sabbatical after 6 years of continuous full-time service, or a 12-week unpaid sabbatical after 10 years, subject to product/people operations approval.
- Returnship & phased reboarding: People-ops offers a 12-week phased return for employees returning from extended leave; managers should pair returning contributors with a reboarding buddy.

How benefits change with role level (rules of thumb)
- Level IC1–IC2: core benefits and standard stipends.
- Level IC3–IC4: increased professional development budgets and eligibility for expanded coaching programs.
- Manager levels (M1+): access to leadership development stipend and executive health screening (where available by region).
- Executive/Director: eligible for supplementary financial planning sessions and elevated relocation allowance.

Examples: common scenarios and resolution steps\- Scenario A: New hire misses the 30-day election window.\  Resolution: Default plan assigned. Employee can change plans during next open enrollment or within 60 days if a qualifying life event occurs. Submit a Benefits Exception Form to people-ops for retroactive change requests.\- Scenario B: Employee transfers from US entity to UK entity effective July 1.\  Resolution: Initiate cross-entity transfer 30 days prior. Employee completes local enrollment; benefits in the old entity end the day before new entity start. Coordinate tax withholding and payroll timing with finance.

Operational runbook: benefits enrollment exception workflow
1. Employee requests exception via Benefits Exception Form (people-ops) with supporting documents.\2. People-ops triages request; if standard exception (e.g., late enrollment within 60 days due to documented IT outage) approve within 5 business days.\3. For non-standard exceptions (policy deviation, cross-entity legal issues), route to benefits-ops-lead for review and escalate to Head of People Ops with recommended compensating controls.\4. If exception approved, create audit log entry and notify payroll to process any retroactive adjustments.

Audit & compliance notes\- Quarterly audits: benefits-ops runs a quarterly reconciliation comparing HRIS enrollments to carrier rosters and payroll deductions. Discrepancies > $500 per employee must be investigated and resolved within 30 days.\- Documentation retention: benefits enrollment forms and exception approvals are retained for 7 years for tax and audit purposes.
