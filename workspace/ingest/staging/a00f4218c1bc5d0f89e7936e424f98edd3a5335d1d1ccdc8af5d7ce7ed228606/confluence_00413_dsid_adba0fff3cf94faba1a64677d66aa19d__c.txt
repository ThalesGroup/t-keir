Customer comms during mitigation (status page + support brief)

# Goal
Ensure customer communications are timely, accurate, and consistent during incident mitigation and emergency changes.

# When to post to the status page
Post a status page update when any of the following are true:
- The incident is Sev1 (always)
- The incident is Sev2 and customer-visible (e.g., elevated latency, increased 5xx, streaming instability)
- We are deploying a mitigation that changes customer experience (e.g., reduced max output tokens, temporary throttling, traffic shifting)

# Required coordination
- **Comms Lead** coordinates content with IC and Support Lead
- Notify Support + CS in #support and #customer-success with a brief before posting

# What not to say
- Do not name specific customers
- Do not speculate on root cause
- Do not commit to exact recovery times
- Do not reference internal system names that customers do not know

# Status page update template
**Title:** Degraded performance for Redwood Hosted API
**Body (example):**
We are investigating increased error rates and elevated latency affecting a subset of requests. We are deploying mitigations to restore normal performance. Next update in 30 minutes.

# Support brief template (internal)
Send to #support and pin in incident channel:
- Impact: what customers see (timeouts, 5xx, slower streaming)
- Scope: regions/routes/tiers if known
- Mitigation: what were doing (rollback, config change, capacity shift)
- Customer workaround: if any
- Next update time

# Direct customer communication guidance
For enterprise/dedicated customers with active escalations:
- CS should send a short email referencing the status page (if posted)
- Include only what is known + next update time
- If we apply a mitigation that changes behavior (limits): explicitly call it out

# After mitigation
- Post a Monitoring update once metrics are stable for 30 minutes
- Post Resolved only after the IC confirms exit criteria
