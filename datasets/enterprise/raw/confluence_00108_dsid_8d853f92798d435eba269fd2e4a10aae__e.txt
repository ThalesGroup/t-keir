Employee Success Pathways and Tooling Playbook

Overview

Purpose
This playbook describes how Redwood supports employee success through three coordinated lenses: (1) role-level expectations (career pathways), (2) benefits & operational guardrails that remove onboarding friction, and (3) a compact internal tooling orientation that teams can reference during the first 120 days. Use this as the single source for manager hand-offs and first-week checklists.

Scope
Applies to full-time and fixed-term Redwood employees across engineering, product, research, go-to-market, and support teams. For contractors and consultants see the contractor onboarding appendix (link in Related pages).

---

1) Quick-start manager checklist (owner: People Ops; SLA: 95% completion within 48 hours of start date)

1. Confirm offer paperwork is complete and start-date is visible in HRIS (PeopleFlow).
2. Provision accounts (GitHub, Slack, Zendesk, Redwood Console staging, internal Google Workspace). Target: 95% provisioned within 24 hours; remediation: contact people-ops@redwood.ai.
3. Invite to team channel and calendar recurring 1:1 for first 90 days.
4. Assign onboarding buddy and kickoff first-week plan (see First-14-days).
5. Notify IT to ship required hardware (standard MacBook Pro or approved alternate). Target ship time: 3 business days for US, 7 business days for APAC.

---

2) First-14-days (new hire checklist)

Must-complete items:
- HR orientation (PeopleFlow): benefits enrollment, W-4 / local tax setup, emergency contact.
- Security baseline: complete SSO/MFA via Okta and hardware token enrollment if applicable.
- Code of Conduct / Security training (SC-1): target completion within 7 calendar days.
- Tools setup: GitHub access, Linear project board, Slack, Redwood Console read-only, Datadog learner account.
- Manager 1:1: goals for first 30/60/90 days established and logged in PeopleFlow (Goal module).

Helpful resources:
- Internal docs: Quickstarts (eng-developer-experience/api-examples), Runbooks (eng-sre/runbooks).

3) Career pathways and leveled expectations (summary table)

This table maps broad career pathways at Redwood to the core outcomes managers should use for leveling and promotion conversations. Use the detailed Role Framework doc for rubrics and calibration sessions.

| Pathway | Common levels | Core outcomes (promotion signal) | Typical time-to-promote (tenure) | Stakeholder review cadence |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Individual Contributor — Software (IC-Eng) | IC1, IC2, IC3, Senior, Staff | Ship low-latency features, own modules, mentor 1-2 peers, improve SLOs by measurable delta (e.g., 10% latency reduction) | IC1→IC2: ~12–18 months | Quarterly calibration + FY promotion window |
| Individual Contributor — Research/Applied ML | R1, R2, R3, Senior Researcher | Publish internal evals, onboard new models with quantization profiles, demonstrate measurable cost/quality trade-offs | 12–24 months | Biannual review + project milestones |
| Managerial Track | M1 (Manager), M2, Director | Team delivery, hiring velocity, retention, career growth of direct reports, OKR attainment | 18–36 months | Quarterly 1:1 + calibration |
| Customer/Platform Roles | CS1, CS2, Solutions Eng | Customer health metrics, NPS uplift, closed escalations with <72h SLA | 12–24 months | Monthly customer health review |

Notes:
- Time-to-promote is indicative and depends on demonstrated impact, not tenure.
- For full rubrics, see: /confluence/company-handbook/02_roles-and-career/role-framework-and-growth-paths-2025.json

4) Promotion & calibration process (step-by-step)

1. Candidate prepares promotion packet: accomplishments, measurable metrics, peer feedback (standardized template).
2. Manager compiles evidence, solicits 3–5 peer reviewers, and files packet in PeopleFlow.
3. Calibration panel (manager's org + People Ops) reviews against rubric.
4. Decision and calibration notes recorded; cross-org exceptions escalated to VP People Ops.

Timing: aim to complete within 6 weeks of submission during the regular promotion window. Ad-hoc promotions can be fast-tracked with Director approval.

5) Benefits & flexibility highlights (how to use them effectively)

- Parental Leave: Primary caregiver 16 weeks fully paid; secondary caregiver 8 weeks. Submit intent in PeopleFlow 30 days in advance when possible.
- Sabbatical: Eligible after 4 years of service; apply to people-ops@redwood.ai with a 90-day lead.
- Flexible returnship program: up to 6 months to phase back from parental leave; coordinate with manager and People Ops.
- Wellness stipend: $150 / quarter (submit receipts in Expenses portal).

Operational guidance:
- Benefits disputes: escalate to benefits@redwood.ai; expected response SLA 5 business days.

6) Internal tooling primer (role-specific highlights and owners)

- PeopleFlow (HRIS / goals / promotion packets) — Owner: People Ops — URL: https://peopleflow.internal.redwood.ai — Key tip: add manager and buddy in the profile so tasks route automatically.
- Identity: Okta SSO / YubiKey workflow — Owner: Security — Target: MFA onboard within 48 hours.
- Code & Review: GitHub (org: redwood-inference) — Owner: Eng Devex — Default team permissions: read to prod repo unless requested.
- Task tracking: Linear — Owner: Eng PMs — Use issue templates: bug, feature, infra.
- Observability: Datadog + Grafana dashboards (read-only learner accounts for new hires) — Owner: Eng SRE — Access requests via internal portal.
- Customer tools: Zendesk (support), Gainsight (CS health) — Owner: Customer Success — Onboarding guided by CS manager.

Tooling SLAs & expectations:
- Account provisioning: 95% within 24 hours for internal services, 48 hours for vendor integrations.
- Target ramp (time-to-meaningful-contribution): 60 days for individual contributors; 90 days for managers. Track with mentor logs in PeopleFlow.

7) First-30/60/90/120 day milestone plan (manager+new hire)

- Day 0–14: Access, paperwork, security training, buddy pairing, first deliverable scoped.
- Day 15–30: Complete a small cross-team deliverable or documentation contribution. Manager evaluates progress and updates goals.
- Day 31–60: Lead one minor feature/analysis and present learnings in team demo.
- Day 61–90: Close a medium-size project with ownership of rollout and post-release metrics.
- Day 91–120: Conduct a 120-day retrospective with manager and update development plan; identify promotion-readiness signals if applicable.

Deliverable checklist (use as gating criteria):
- Completed security training & MFA
- First PR merged and code reviewed
- One customer or internal stakeholder conversation logged
- Documented learnings in team wiki

8) Manager toolkit (templates & meeting cadences)

- Weekly 1:1 template: wins, blockers, development item, action items.
- Onboarding handoff: template that includes hardware serials, required access, initial code area, and buddy notes.
- Check-ins at day 7, 30, 60, 90, 120 (short status + evidence against deliverables).

Checklist sample (day 30):
- [ ] Goal 1 defined and progress visible in PeopleFlow
- [ ] Buddy notes added to profile
- [ ] First PR merged
- [ ] Security & Compliance training completed

---

9) Frequently encountered friction & mitigations

- Delay in account provisioning: Pre-approve service access for an expected hire two days before start date. People Ops can fast-track with ticket tag: "provision-critical".
- Hardware shipping delays in APAC: Offer loaner stipend or temporary remote VM access to staging.
- Knowledge transfer gaps: Encourage recording of 30-minute walkthroughs and adding to team wiki. Provide editing guidance for docs.
