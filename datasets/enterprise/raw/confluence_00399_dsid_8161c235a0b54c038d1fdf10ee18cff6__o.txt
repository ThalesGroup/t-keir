Observability sampling stewardship and cost-aware SLI playbook

Title: Observability sampling stewardship and cost-aware SLI playbook

Summary
This playbook defines a company-wide standard for telemetry sampling (metrics, traces, logs) that balances SLI fidelity with ingestion cost and operational signal. It prescribes:
- an SLI-weighted sampling algorithm for traces
- retention and tiering for logs and high-cardinality metrics
- policy templates for service owners to opt into or override
- rollout and migration steps with example configs and observability queries

Audience
Service owners, SREs, observability engineers, and platform infra teams responsible for telemetry cost, reliability, and alert fidelity.

Goals
1. Preserve high-fidelity telemetry for SLO-impacting traffic while controlling ingestion costs.
2. Provide a transparent, auditable sampling policy that service owners can reason about.
3. Ensure critical incidents retain full traces/logs for at least 72 hours.
4. Enable safe defaults such that new services start with conservative retention and can opt for higher fidelity after review.

Scope
- Applies to all Redwood-hosted services and customer-facing API traffic (hosted, dedicated).
- Includes distributed traces, request-level logs, and custom application metrics.
- Does NOT replace customer data-retention contracts or compliance-mandated logging for Private deployments; those are handled per customer configuration.

Principles
- Signal-first: prioritize telemetry that contributes directly to SLO calculation and alerting.
- Cost transparency: every policy must include estimated monthly ingestion and retention cost delta.
- Deterministic sampling where possible to make replay and attribution tractable.
- Escalation-safe: any sampling policy must guarantee trace capture for requests that trigger SLO breach or high-severity incidents.

Key terms
- Sampling unit: request trace (recommended primary unit), not individual spans.
- SLI-weighted sampling (SWS): probabilistic sampling where sampling probability is a function of SLI risk score.
- Traffic class: an orthogonal label (e.g., realtime, batch, admin) set by service owners in deployment config.

Policy overview
1) Default global settings (platform-level):
   - Trace sample rate: 2% for general traffic.
   - Trace retention: indexed traces stored 30d; raw trace payloads 7d.
   - Logs: ERROR+ saved at full fidelity for 30d, INFO saved to aggregated summaries for 14d, DEBUG disabled by default.
   - High-cardinality metrics: 90-day retention for aggregated series; raw cardinality cutoffs apply (see table below).
2) SLI-weighted sampling: increase sampling probability for requests with higher risk of contributing to SLO failure.
   - base_p = global_trace_sample_rate (e.g., 0.02)
   - risk_score = f(latency_percentile, error_flag, user_tier) normalized to [0,1]
   - sample_p = min(1.0, base_p * (1 + alpha * risk_score)) where alpha is tunable (default 20)

3) High-importance paths: services can declare endpoints as "critical_paths" which receive elevated deterministic sampling (e.g., 50% by default) after approval from Observability Steering.

SLI-weighted sampling algorithm (example)\Pseudocode:
1. For each request, compute risk_score:
   - latency_factor = clamp((latency_ms - p50_ms) / (p95_ms - p50_ms), 0, 1)
   - error_factor = error ? 1 : 0
   - tier_factor = (user_tier == "enterprise") ? 1.0 : 0.5
   - risk_score = 0.6*latency_factor + 0.3*error_factor + 0.1*tier_factor
2. sample_prob = min(1.0, base_p * (1 + 20 * risk_score))
3. sample_decision = deterministic_hash(request_id) < sample_prob

Notes: deterministic_hash uses a keyed 64-bit hash on request_id and service-salt to make sampling replayable across components.

Cost and fidelity tiers (examples)\| Tier | Trace sample rate | Indexed retention | Log retention (ERROR) | Intended use |
|------|-------------------:|------------------:|---------------------:|-------------|
| conservative-default | 2% | 30d | 30d | New services, low-volume internal features |
| production-standard | 10% | 90d | 90d | Customer-facing services with SLOs |
| high-fidelity | 50%+ | 365d (archived) | 365d | Financial/regulated or high-risk paths |

Config examples\1) Service manifest snippet (YAML):

observability:
  trace_sampling:
    mode: "sli_weighted" # options: fixed, sli_weighted, deterministic\    base_rate: 0.02\    alpha: 20\    critical_paths:
      - "/v1/charge"\      - "/v1/commit"\  log_retention:
    error_days: 30\    info_days: 14\    debug_enabled: false\  traffic_class: "realtime"

2) Edge/agent snippet (env var style):
REDWOOD_TRACE_MODE=sli_weighted
REDWOOD_TRACE_BASE=0.02
REDWOOD_TRACE_ALPHA=20

Rollout and migration plan\1. Platform feature gating (week 0): enable SWS in a shadow mode where sampling decisions are computed and logged but not enforced. Collect telemetry on decision distribution and expected ingestion delta.
2. Pilot (weeks 2–4): pick 3 medium-volume services across different teams to opt into enforced SWS with base_rate=0.02. Observe retention cost delta, SLI alert false-positive/negative changes.
3. Expand (weeks 5–8): roll SWS to all customer-facing services with base_rate=0.02 and default alpha=20.
4. Hardening (weeks 9–12): allow service-level overrides via review; add deterministic capture exceptions for incident-triggered requests.
5. Review cadence: quarterly reviews with Observability Steering to adjust alpha and base rates based on cost/FDR targets.

Monitoring and verification
- Essential dashboards:
  - Sampling coverage heatmap (service x endpoint) showing expected vs actual sampled traces.
  - SLI fidelity delta dashboard: tracks variance in SLI when sampling enabled vs ground truth (shadow mode).
  - Cost projection panel: estimated ingestion cost delta from sampling changes.
- Alerts:
  - Alert if sampled_trace_rate < 50% of expected for any production-standard service for > 10 minutes.
  - Alert on SLI fidelity delta > 5% (e.g., error rate estimate changes by more than 5%)

Runbook: degraded telemetry or missing signals
1. If dashboards show a sudden drop in sampled traces: check agent/sidecar version and service-salt rotation events.
2. If SLI estimates diverge from raw counters: switch the service to shadow_mode and compare sample decisions for 30 minutes.
3. If ingestion costs spike: reduce base_rate by 50% for the affected traffic class, and open a postmortem if cost exceeds budget by >25%.

Operational roles and ownership
- Observability Platform (team): implements sampling library, agents, and guardrails. Owner: Avery Chen.
- Service Owner: declares traffic_class, maintains cost estimate, and requests exceptions.
- Observability Steering: cross-functional board (SRE, Infra, Product) that approves critical_path exceptions and high-fidelity tiers.
- Cost Engineering: validates monthly ingestion estimates and flags outliers.

Approval and exception process
- Exceptions for high-fidelity tiers require:
  1) Service-level justification (business and SLO impact).
  2) Monthly cost estimate and mitigation plan.
  3) Approval from Observability Steering (min 2 approvers including one SRE).
- Approved exceptions are time-boxed (90d) and must include a reevaluation plan.

Appendix: example queries and checks
- Sampling coverage:
  sum(sampled_traces{service=\"payments\"}) by (endpoint)
  / sum(requests_total{service=\"payments\"}) by (endpoint)
- Estimated ingestion delta after policy change (PromQL-like pseudo):
  sum(rate(estimated_trace_bytes[1d])) - baseline_estimated_trace_bytes
